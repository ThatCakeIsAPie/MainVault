# Mentorship vs Consulting

The LTD Amway notes repeatedly contrast a built-in mentorship environment with paid expert advice. This comparison clarifies what is actually different.

| Dimension | Consulting | Mentorship |
|---|---|---|
| Primary exchange | Money for advice, deliverables, or expertise | Relationship, example, correction, and development over time |
| Incentive structure | Consultant is paid whether or not the client transforms, depending on contract | Mentor's upside is connected to the mentee's growth and duplication |
| Time horizon | Project-based or retainer-based | Long-term and character-forming |
| Scope | Usually domain-specific | Often spills into habits, marriage/family, faith, leadership, and identity |
| Access standard | Ability to pay | Willingness to be coached, show up, and produce effort/results |
| Failure mode | Advice without transformation | Entitlement to leader time without performance or humility |

## LTD-Specific Framing

Tyler Sheridan emphasizes that mentorship from high-level earners often costs thousands elsewhere, while in the network-marketing environment it is built in because the mentor's success is tied to helping others win. ^[Business/LTD Amway/Info Sessions/2026/2026-06-04 Tyler Sheridan.md]

Dale Jones distinguishes Amway as the manufacturer and LTD as the leadership training provider, making mentorship the growth mechanism layered on top of the product platform. ^[Business/LTD Amway/Info Sessions/2026/2026-05-28 Dale Jones.md]

## Synthesis

Consulting transfers knowledge. Mentorship transfers posture, standards, judgment, and identity through repeated exposure and correction. That is why [[offshoots/mentorship-compression-through-proven-paths]] is central to the LTD Amway corpus.
