## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Control planes are becoming products:** Agent gateways, model-spend guardrails, GovCon evidence lockers, direct-selling claim review, and ownership formulas all convert ambiguous judgment into named authority, policy, receipts, and escalation.
- **Cheap inputs can hide expensive failures:** Low token/video prices and AI-rollup labor claims mean little without provider semantics, accepted results, correction time, supervision, compliance, working capital, and fallback.
- **Scale increases blast radius:** White-label agents, AI advertising, automated recruiting, proposal drafting, and rollups can multiply output faster than governance. Distribution is not proof; it is leverage applied to whatever quality already exists.
- **Mechanism beats label:** “Latest model,” “compliance-first,” “AI-powered rollup,” and “employee ownership” are not specifications. Each requires economics, authority, evidence, failure handling, and appeal/rollback.
