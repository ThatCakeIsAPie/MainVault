## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Governance is the product.** Agent auth, GovCon evidence lockers, model budgets, direct-selling compliance scripts, rollup integration checklists, and ownership design are all control systems.
- **Workflow beats tool.** The recurring high-value pattern is repeatable operating systems: agent work units, proposal radar, creative pipelines, model routing, leadership scripts, acquisition diligence, and profit-share architecture.
- **Human approval remains the economic moat.** The useful AI trend is not removing humans; it is giving humans better triage, draft, audit, and escalation systems.
- **Cost visibility is becoming operational discipline.** OpenRouter cache rates, video iteration cost, proposal drafting tokens, and acquisition integration cost all need dashboards, not vibes.
