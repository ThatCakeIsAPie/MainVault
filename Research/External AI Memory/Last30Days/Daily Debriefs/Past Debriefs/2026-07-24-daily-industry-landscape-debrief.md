## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Routing is becoming a horizontal control layer:** OpenRouter for text models and Runway Media Router for generative media both shift value from choosing one model to optimizing across models under explicit preferences, costs, and quality criteria.
- **Evidence beats labels:** `agent-ready`, `compliant`, `employee-owned`, `AI-generated`, and `free model` are weak claims without permissions, receipts, economics, rights, and fallbacks.
- **Automation expands the blast radius of ambiguity:** malicious repositories, incorrect ISR classification, synthetic-media provenance, earnings claims, and unclear ownership promises all become cheaper to repeat at scale.
- **Real operating systems close loops:** accepted-result evals, corrected ISR receipts, finished-media metrics, paid fallbacks, compliant scripts, integration owners, and participant appeal rights are the mechanisms that convert narratives into outcomes.
