## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Control layer beats capability demo.** Agents, GovCon automation, model routing, and ownership design all point to the same requirement: permissions, audit trails, and explicit decision rights.
- **Routing is replacing monolithic selection.** OpenRouter routing, video-model aggregators, GovCon source ingestion, and agent orchestration all reward picking the right tool/model/person for the step.
- **Compliance and trust are becoming product features.** GovCon, MLM, agentic automation, and employee ownership all punish vague claims and reward clear records.
- **AI leverage favors operators with process clarity.** PE/search/rollups and Faleth subsidiaries should automate known workflows, not use AI to cover for undefined operations.
