## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Structured workflows are beating generic generation:** Army MAX, Muse Code, TikTok Symphony, GovCon opportunity agents, and IDS review all package AI inside stages, artifacts, owners, and handoffs.
- **Identity, evidence, and authority are converging:** coding agents need credentials and branch ownership; GovCon needs source/approval receipts; ad production needs reference/provenance controls; MLM needs claim review; ownership structures need fiduciary and formula clarity.
- **Distribution is part of the product:** TikTok bundles generation into ads; OpenRouter bundles model availability into routing; Army MAX connects requirements to acquisition; direct selling scales messages. A wrong output distributed faster is not leverage—it is merely efficient embarrassment.
- **Accepted-result economics remains the common KPI:** model prices, agent cost, proposal time, creative performance, acquisition integration, and compensation systems should all be evaluated against accepted business outcomes, not activity volume.
