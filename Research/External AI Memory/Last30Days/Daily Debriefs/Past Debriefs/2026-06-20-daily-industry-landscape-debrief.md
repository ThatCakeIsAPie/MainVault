## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Recurring workflows are the unit.** Agents, GovCon capture, video production, model routing, and ownership administration are all moving from one-off tasks to repeatable operating systems.
- **Governance beats raw capability.** Whether the domain is scheduled agents, proposal AI, MLM earnings claims, model routing, or employee ownership, the hard part is not generating words — it is authority, disclosure, auditability, incentives, and accountability.
- **Cost control is becoming architectural.** AI video pricing, OpenRouter cache/budget features, and GovCon proposal-model routing all point to the same rule: design workflows around cost observability from day one.
- **Human trust remains the constraint.** SMB acquisitions, network leadership, GovCon submissions, and employee ownership transitions all fail when the process ignores trust transfer and legitimacy.
