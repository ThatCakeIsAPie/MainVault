## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Integration beats novelty:** MCP inside MATLAB/HR systems, creative testing inside agencies, AI inside capture workflows, and EOTs inside succession plans all point toward embedded operating systems rather than standalone features.
- **The real product is the control surface:** model fallbacks, agent budgets, ISR receipts, claims guardrails, creative-performance metrics, acquisition diligence, and ownership-rights maps turn noisy categories into manageable systems.
- **Cheap capacity is not durable capacity:** OpenRouter's six free-route removals are the clearest daily example, but the same logic applies to promotional AI-video tools, outsourced GovCon automation, and capital-market narratives.
- **Relationship-driven businesses resist spreadsheet-only transitions:** accounting firms, agencies, GovCon partnerships, network leadership, and EOT conversions all depend on trust transfer, named accountability, and continuity.
- **VXE remains the binding constraint:** contract cash timing, post-deadline compliance closure, and fulfillment readiness matter more than tool tourism, acquisition theater, or yet another strategic diagram of capitalism.
