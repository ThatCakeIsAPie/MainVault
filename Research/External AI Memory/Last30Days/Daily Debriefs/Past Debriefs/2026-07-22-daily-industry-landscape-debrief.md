## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Boundaries are becoming the product:** agent sandboxes, MCP governance, government-data clauses, model routing, synthetic-media provenance, approved claims, acquisition integration, and ownership rights all require explicit limits and evidence.
- **Economic participation is not operational competence:** cheap models need benchmarks, PE capital needs operators, ownership needs engagement systems, and AI-video access needs editorial judgment.
- **The market is moving from generation to control:** model output, proposals, videos, outreach, and employee upside all become valuable only when humans can inspect, revise, approve, attribute, and stop them.
- **Flat reassurance is dangerous:** “CMMC is paused,” “the agent is sandboxed,” “the model is free,” “employees own it,” or “AI made the video” are labels, not controls.
- **VXE execution remains the binding priority:** ISR closure, clause/CMMC mapping, cash timing, and fulfillment readiness still outrank acquisition scouting and tool switching.
