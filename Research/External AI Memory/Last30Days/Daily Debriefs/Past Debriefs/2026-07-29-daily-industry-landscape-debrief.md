## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Asynchronous work is becoming a priced infrastructure tier:** OpenRouter batch routes formalize cheaper delayed model execution while agent platforms package recurring enterprise workflows. Route by urgency rather than paying interactive prices for work nobody is watching.
- **Authority and verification are converging:** agent gateways, autonomous ad changes, ISR corrections, earnings claims, rollup productivity, and ownership labels all need a named authority plus evidence of what actually happened.
- **Automation claims need accepted-output economics:** “triple output,” cheap batch inference, autonomous EDA, and creative agents are only useful after retries, supervision, quality, latency, and downstream outcomes are counted.
- **Mechanism beats label:** `batch`, `agentic`, `compliant`, `employee-owned`, and `automation-led` are incomplete without scope, price, rights, controls, and receipts.
