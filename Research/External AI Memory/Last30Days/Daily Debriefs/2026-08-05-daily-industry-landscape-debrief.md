## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Identity and authority are becoming first-class infrastructure:** SailPoint's agent identities, SAM.gov named-human responsibility, creative approval, model route receipts, MLM claim review, acquisition operator ownership, and ESOP fiduciary process all answer the same question: who is authorized, accountable, and able to stop the system?
- **The editor/control plane is the moat:** Flow Studio edits scenes; OpenEdit edits video through agents; GovCon evidence lockers edit/approve grounded proposals; model routers select providers; ownership vehicles encode rights. Raw generation is becoming abundant while controlled revision remains valuable.
- **Automation claims are moving from capability to economics:** OpenRouter cost continuity, token-economics work, PE workforce pressure, and agent governance all demand accepted-result accounting rather than raw token, labor, or generation counts.
- **Availability is not authorization:** A model route, open repo, generated asset, AI compliance suggestion, or ownership label does not itself establish production approval, legal permission, compliance, fiduciary sufficiency, or customer value.
