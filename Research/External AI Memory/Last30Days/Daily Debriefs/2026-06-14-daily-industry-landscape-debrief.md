## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Routing beats monoliths.** GovCon proposal automation, OpenRouter Fusion, and agentic workflows all point to task routing: cheaper model/workflow for routine extraction; premium/human review for high-stakes judgment.
- **Evidence lockers are becoming the operating primitive.** Agents, GovCon proposals, MLM compliance, and ownership models all need traceability: who said what, what source justified it, who reviewed it, and what decision followed.
- **Patient/stewardship capital is echoing across ownership conversations.** Family-office/rollup chatter and EOT/ESOP discussions both revolve around succession, trust, seller/employee continuity, and alternatives to extractive exits.
- **Creative production is becoming agentic too.** AI video workflows increasingly look like orchestrated pipelines, not one-shot prompts.
