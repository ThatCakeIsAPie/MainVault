## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Boundaries must be mechanically true:** Anthropic's eval incident, MCP permissions, GovCon evidence, MLM net-impression rules, and ownership rights all punish systems whose written story differs from operational reality.
- **Cheap capability increases governance demand:** Lower Terra/Luna prices, DeepSeek V4 Flash, H3 video pricing, and callable MCP servers make experimentation cheaper—and increase the number of workflows that need identity, scope, receipts, and stop controls.
- **Catalogs and wrappers are not durable rights:** An OpenRouter ID can disappear; an ESOP can later sell; an agent permission can broaden; a compliance deadline can pass. Dependency maps and exit/fallback mechanisms matter.
- **Accepted-result economics remain the common KPI:** Token price, video seconds, agent actions, proposal drafts, acquisition multiples, and contribution points matter only after quality, supervision, risk, timing, and cash are counted.
