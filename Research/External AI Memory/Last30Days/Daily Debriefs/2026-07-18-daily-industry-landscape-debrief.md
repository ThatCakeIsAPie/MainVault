## Cross-Industry Patterns
- **Flat totals conceal operational churn:** OpenRouter stayed at 344 while four IDs changed. The same lesson applies to compliance and ownership—headline status is less useful than exact underlying rights, clauses, controls, and routes.
- **Governance is moving into the execution layer:** agent integration, model routing, GovCon compliance, and employee ownership all require explicit authority, evidence, review, and fallback mechanics.
- **Popularity is not policy:** aggregate model spend, top-earner stories, and abundant PE capital are market signals—not reasons to delegate routing, compliance, leadership language, or acquisitions to the crowd.
- **Succession and continuity connect PE and employee ownership:** sellers increasingly have alternatives to a conventional PE exit, but every path still depends on operator capacity, financial literacy, and governance clarity.
- **VXE cash timing still dominates:** CMMC/ISR assurance and fulfillment readiness outrank model tourism or acquisition theater.
