# Reconciled Tensions and Anti-Patterns

Where teachings collide, keep both truths and name the operating rule.

## 1. Long intake funnel vs invitation velocity

- **Tension:** Quality filter and relationship prep vs compounding invite math.
- **Reconcile:** Both can be true. Long funnels raise cost-per-prospect; better retention could theoretically compensate—but only if measured.
- **Rule:** Operate inside required funnel now; maximize inventory and post-intake culture; track retention/volume; change front door only with leverage. Do not let dojo vision psychologically erase funnel cost.

Source: [[ltd-amway/NM-Room-Tension-Discussion-2026-07-05]]

## 2. Protect new-person attention vs suppress growth stories

- **Tension:** Distraction risk vs personal-development-as-product.
- **Reconcile:** Time-bound attention protection for early stage can be stewardship. Broad permanent muting of non-competing growth undercuts the trust/growth mechanism of the model.
- **Rule:** Protect early focus from shiny income chases; do not ban character/health/relationship fruit as "distraction."

## 3. "This is easy" vs "boring hard consistency"

- **Tension:** Narrative ease (HOAL room) vs Joe Markiewicz "boring stuff more often."
- **Reconcile:** Simple process, emotionally hard execution. Easy narrative fights self-sabotage; hard narrative fights fantasy.
- **Rule:** Keep process simple; expect emotional difficulty; never sell effortless income.

## 4. Personal volume vs organization

- **Tension:** Customer/PV craft vs sponsoring depth.
- **Reconcile:** Two oars. Customer PV funds belief and residual; depth multiplies. Neglect either and the boat spins.
- **Rule:** Weekly scoreboard includes both customers and pipeline/sponsors.

## 5. Sponsor ratios (4-pack vs one-in-twelve vs plan-shower)

- **Tension:** Different denominators look like contradictions.
- **Reconcile:** Different questions.
  - 4-pack: early sponsor outcomes / finding 3 builders.
  - One-in-twelve: personal sponsors → eventual Platinum-bearing width group.
  - Plan-shower: org composition among active builders.
- **Rule:** Never mix denominators. Plan with the model that matches the decision.

## 6. PV / Platinum count vs Qualification Credits

- **Tension:** Field shorthand vs official award math.
- **Reconcile:** 7500PV model builds one Platinum-shaped org. Higher pins use FP groups + QCs.
- **Rule:** Withdrawn: 52 × 7500PV extrapolations. Track QC properly.

## 7. Defend the business vs qualify hunger

- **Tension:** Belief-sharing vs argument addiction.
- **Reconcile:** Belief is for leaners and builders; debate is for ego.
- **Rule:** Filter. Friendly to all. Energy to hungry.

## 8. Stats honesty (99% lose) vs builder reality

- **Tension:** Industry critique vs active-builder experience.
- **Reconcile:** Zero-inflated signups dominate denominators; official IDS still shows low averages including zeros—both matter.
- **Rule:** Be honest about selection and work; never counter with illegal typicality claims. Point to IDS and required work.

## 9. Trust coaches vs inspect systems

- **Tension:** Mentorship loyalty vs blind authority.
- **Reconcile:** Mentorship compresses mistakes; measurement protects agency.
- **Rule:** Honor fruit-bearing coaches; keep leading indicators; retain moral agency.

## 10. LTD all-in language vs Lyle's season

- **Tension:** Room full-commitment rhetoric vs VXE cash timing.
- **Reconcile:** Sequencing model. LTD incubates leadership; primary economic execution can sit elsewhere this season.
- **Rule:** Equip, do not displace. No "go all-in on LTD" counsel this season.

## 11. Growth stories outside the room vs information control

- **Tension:** Room policy vs Faleth open/dojo posture.
- **Reconcile:** Proof-first influence; shape post-intake culture now; policy fights later if ever.
- **Rule:** Build results; peaceful alignment; open exit ethics from Faleth constitution as personal standard.

## 12. AI / labor fear as recruiting fuel

- **Tension:** Real labor disruption vs fear-selling.
- **Reconcile:** Agency argument is valid; panic recruiting is not.
- **Rule:** Increase capability and ownership; never manufacture terror.

## Anti-pattern shortlist

- Thin pipeline + heavy emotional attachment to one prospect
- Registration vanity without 7-day action bridge
- Doing all meetings for the team forever
- Edification addiction
- Dashboard without activity
- Conference high as substitute for calendar
- Multiplying unlike units (QC × PV)
- Implied lifestyle earnings
- Identity = pin
- Comparing inactive industry denominators to your active week without adjusting
