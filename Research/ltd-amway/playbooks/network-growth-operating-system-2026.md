# Network Growth Operating System (LTD/Amway)

Actionable synthesis of best practices for growing a network marketing business, drawn from Lyle's LTD notes, field math models, conference distillates, and Research-layer industry context. Platform = Amway. Leadership environment = LTD. This document is an operating system, not a pep talk.

## Current-season guardrail

LTD equips character, communication, discipline, association, and leadership. It does not replace VXE/GovCon cash timing this season. Use these playbooks to strengthen the man who shows up for the live economic work—not as permission to "go all-in on LTD" while cash timing is elsewhere.

## Operating law

**Results follow identity-backed, measured, sustained action—not emotion, intention, or occasional intensity.**

Causal chain (field model):

> Conversations → opened doors → booked MG1s → run MG1s → sponsors → viable builders → core legs → depth → PV / FP groups / QCs

Lagging output is PV and pin. Leading work is conversations, MG progression, customer creation, sponsor activity, depth movement, and system participation.

## One-page scoreboard (baseline Platinum ignition)

From the user-confirmed field models (not Amway guarantees):

| Cadence | Metric |
|---|---:|
| Daily | 10 conversations |
| Daily | 3 opening-the-door conversations |
| Weekly | 4 booked MG1s |
| Monthly | 10 MG1s run |
| Monthly | 1 sponsor (baseline 12/year) |
| Monthly in depth | ~80 MG1s across the organization |
| Team shape | ~50 seats × ~150PV ≈ 7500PV, usually uneven (4k / 2.5k / 1k + smalls) |

Stretch personal year: ~14 conversations/day, 4 opens/day, 6 MG1s booked/week, 14 MG1s run/month, 16 sponsors/year.

## Four engines (keep them separate)

1. **Personal width engine** — conversations, opens, MG1s, personal sponsors, annual cohort distribution.
2. **Customer/PV engine** — personal use, customers, VCS/ditto, retention service, personal circle PV.
3. **Depth/duplication engine** — hard runners, plan-showers in depth, ~80 monthly MG1s org-wide, communication rhythm.
4. **Higher-pin engine** — Founders Platinum groups (width) + Qualification Credits (depth). Do not multiply 52 × 7500PV; QC ≠ Platinum count.

## Diagnosis before motivation (WKRP + pipe)

When stalled, inspect in order:

1. **W — Work:** volume too low → everything feels complicated.
2. **K — Knowledge:** what to say / how meetings move.
3. **R — Right people:** hunger, ambition, coachability.
4. **P — Personality / posture:** defending, neediness, self-image.

Then stage of the pipe: conversations → opens → bookings → shows → sponsors → builders → depth → PV.

## Compliance floor (non-optional)

- Paid on product volume and customer value, not "recruiting as the product."
- Earnings language: IDS-backed, typical-results-aware, expense-aware. "Results not typical" is not a cure-all.
- Separate banned frames: job, investment/return, guaranteed/passive income, health outcomes beyond approved claims, lifestyle implication, synthetic testimonials.
- Industry backdrop: Amway IDS, Rules of Conduct, FTC MLM guidance; field enthusiasm does not override disclosure.

## Lyle-specific operating posture

- **Dojo inside the room:** post-intake, high demonstration, growth stories allowed in his circle; front door still follows upline funnel until leverage exists ([[ltd-amway/NM-Room-Tension-Discussion-2026-07-05]]).
- **Proof over policy fights:** build results; do not contest intake policy as the growth plan.
- **Identity root:** Christ-rooted identity; business is vehicle and training ground, not savior or scoreboard of worth.
- **Season honesty:** track LTD funnel cost without letting aspirational vision paper over velocity constraints.
