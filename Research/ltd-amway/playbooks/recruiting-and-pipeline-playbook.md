# Recruiting and Pipeline Playbook

## Job

Fill a pipe so no single prospect carries emotional weight. Contacting is **filtering hunger**, not winning debates.

## Standard throughput (baseline)

| Stage | Pace |
|---|---|
| Conversations | 10 / active day |
| Opening the door | 3 / active day |
| MG1s booked | 4 / week |
| MG1s run | 10 / month |
| Personal sponsors | 1 / month (12 / year baseline; 16 stretch) |

Annual width math for one expected Platinum-bearing group: ~2,500 conversations → 750 opens → 200 bookings → 120 run MG1s → 12 sponsors → 3 hard runners → 1 eventual Platinum-bearing group (planning heuristic).

Sources: [[Business/LTD Amway/Platinum 7500PV Activity Math]], [[Business/LTD Amway/Founders Triple Diamond Work Requirement Model]]

## Operating rules

1. **Win = meet people**, not "get the number." Agenda-light marketplace presence reduces weirdness and increases authenticity (Hadden / Gordon Night Owl).
2. **Assume many are not hungry.** ~2/10 contacts lean in; preserve friendliness for all; spend sit-down time on leaners (Josh Gordon).
3. **Stop defending the business to unhungry people.** Qualifying > convincing.
4. **List work is permanent.** "No for now" ≠ "no forever." Refresh A/B lists; seasons change.
5. **Follow-up is leadership.** High volume of clean follow-up beats clever one-liners.
6. **8–10 people in process** is emotional stability. Thin pipe → neediness and over-attachment.
7. **Sponsor the easy ones** once you know the phrases that surface lookers—but never invent hunger.
8. **Bring sharp people to the system** (info session / proven plan shower). Maximize upline plan time; study the show until you can run it.
9. **Customer conversations are a doorway.** Goals and needs (gym, wellness, product) can become business conversations when hunger appears—do not force every chat into a pitch.
10. **Compliance:** product/customer value first; no job/investment/guaranteed-income framing; IDS if earnings appear.

## Funnel diagnostics

| Symptom | Likely leak | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Few conversations | Calendar / fear / isolation | Block marketplace hours; track contacts only |
| Conversations but no opens | Weak invite / no curiosity | Practice door-open language; more reps |
| Opens but no bookings | Soft close / no calendar | Book on the spot; same-week slots |
| Bookings but no-shows | Weak confirm process | Elevate: one team cut no-shows 70% → 20% by process change |
| Shows but no sponsors | Wrong people / plan skill / pressure | Hunger filter; improve plan; remove neediness |
| Sponsors but no builders | Onboarding leak | See retention playbook; get someone under them fast |

## Intake tension (live constraint)

Upline long funnel (multiple 1:1s + weekly meetings) raises cost-per-prospect and can throttle invitation velocity. Do **not** pretend the dojo vision already fixed the front door.

- **Live now:** maximize quality of every slot inside the required funnel; keep personal list and marketplace volume high enough that the funnel has inventory.
- **Later leverage:** simpler team front door only when structure/influence supports it.
- Track retention and volume honestly rather than arguing theory.

Source: [[ltd-amway/NM-Room-Tension-Discussion-2026-07-05]]

## Practical weekly loop

1. Refresh list (names + next action).
2. Hit conversation and open targets on active days.
3. Book 4 MG1s; confirm shows.
4. Run meetings; debrief with upline same week.
5. Log stage conversions; one qualitative bottleneck note.
6. Association/read/listen to refill desire—not as substitute for pipeline.
