# Rank Advancement and Volume Architecture Playbook

## Job

Separate **personal volume**, **organization shape**, **Founders Platinum width**, and **Qualification Credits**—then build each with the right engine.

## Layer 1 — Personal circle / customer PV

- Two revenue paths at the base: buy/use and sell/serve customers.
- Early objective often includes personal circle stability (~150PV launch patterns, VCS/ditto growth).
- Customer retention and lifetime value beat one-time sample churn.
- Personal PV creates confidence and story; it is not the whole Platinum business.

## Layer 2 — Platinum / 7500PV organization shape

7500PV is **not** a personal sales heroics target. It is aggregate org output.

Typical concentrated shape:

- 1 × ~4000PV leg
- 1 × ~2500PV leg
- 1 × ~1000PV leg
- Several 150–600PV legs filling remainder
- Clean average model: ~50 seats × ~150PV

Rough leg density shorthand (Lyle model):

- ~1000PV leg ≈ emerging ~4-person productive density
- ~2500PV leg ≈ ~3/10 organization
- ~4000PV leg ≈ ~6/25 organization

Timeline heuristic: Year 1 width/builders; Year 2 depth to Platinum shape if baseline activity holds (~2 years standing start).

Sources: [[Business/LTD Amway/Platinum 7500PV Activity Math]], [[Research/raw/transcripts/2026-07-11-nm-platinum-activity-benchmarks]]

## Layer 3 — Finding the three core legs

NM 4-sponsor funnel → ~12–15 sponsors to locate 3 builders.

Lyle one-in-twelve planning model (user-confirmed):

> 12 personal sponsors/year → 3 quit / 3 stick / 3 do something / 3 run hard → 1 of the hard runners eventually produces a Platinum-bearing group (often in depth).

The personally sponsored person need not pin Platinum personally. Width counts the **group** that contains FP.

## Layer 4 — Higher pins (official units)

September 2024 U.S. Amway Business Reference Guide:

| Award | Min FP Groups | QC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Triple Diamond | 10+ | 43 |
| Founders Triple Diamond | 10+ | 52 |

**Correction:** 52 is Qualification Credits, not 52 Platinums. Do not run 52 × 7500PV fantasy math. QC has schedules and per-group caps.

Personal width engine for 10 FP-bearing groups at 1-in-12: expected **120 personal sponsors** (~10 sponsor-years at 12/year, or ~7.5 at 16/year), plus maturity lag. Depth/QC tracked separately.

Sources: [[Business/LTD Amway/Founders Triple Diamond Work Requirement Model]], [[Research/raw/transcripts/2024-09-amway-business-reference-guide-global-award-recognition-extract]]

## Personal volume vs organization (reconciled)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I "sell my way" to durable Platinum alone? | No. Structure and depth dominate 7500PV. |
| Is personal/customer PV optional? | No. It funds belief, story, and residual; weak personal circle weakens everything. |
| What scales income? | Duplicated leaders and depth systems (B quadrant), not permanent personal heroics (S trap). |
| What do I track for higher pins? | FP groups + QCs, not "Platinum count" as a fake QC. |

## Rank milestones as mile markers

Dale Jones / room pattern: HOAL, 3/10, 6/25, Ruby components are **products of building**, not the goal itself. Qualifying is evidence of a build agreement kept under pressure. Bodies without builders are self-deception.

Field note: sponsor rate must outpace quit rate over time; rebuild after conference quits is normal (multi-year arcs common).

## Advancement checklist

### To Platinum-shaped org

- [ ] Baseline personal funnel held for months, not weeks
- [ ] ≥12–15 cumulative sponsors or clear path
- [ ] 3 identifiable hard-running legs
- [ ] Depth MG1s moving toward ~80/month org-wide
- [ ] Customer + personal circle not neglected
- [ ] Communication rhythm with builders weekly

### Toward multi-FP width

- [ ] Each year produces a sponsor cohort with visible hard runners
- [ ] At least one path maturing toward Platinum-in-depth per cohort expectation
- [ ] Separate QC tracking begun before Diamond math fantasies
- [ ] Character/platform can hold the pin (Elevate: Platinum right)

## Compliance reminder

Pin stories and lifestyle talk require IDS context. Average earnings for Founders Platinum and below are low on official disclosure when zeros are included. Field ambition is fine; implied typicality is not.
