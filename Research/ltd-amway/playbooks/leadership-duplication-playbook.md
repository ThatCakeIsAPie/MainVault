# Leadership Duplication Playbook

## Job

Stop being the best individual performer in your own business. Master once, then **train yourself out of a job** so depth produces PV and leaders produce leaders.

## Core law

Where there is no duplication, there is no systemized income. Charismatic growth is not reliably duplicatable; systematic leadership is. Organized growth creates momentum; unorganized growth creates chaos.

## What must be duplicated (not your personality)

1. **Thinking** — how decisions get made under rejection and delay.
2. **Speaking** — door opens, invites, plan language, follow-up.
3. **Communication rhythm** — upline and downline cadence.
4. **Habits** — read, listen, associate, track, move.
5. **Standards** — who gets time, what counts as work, what "done" means.
6. **Customer craft** — product confidence and service.
7. **Meeting skill** — MG1/MG2 and info-session excellence.

## Four skills system (Matsumoto Platinum system)

1. Listening — 2–3 LTD audios/day (system, not random podcasts).
2. Affirmations — written, time-bound, 2–3×/day.
3. Reading — 30 min or 30 pages printed (~2 books/month).
4. Radical association — Night Owls / rooms non-optional for builders.

System law: motivation ≠ system. A system is a system followed consistently. Education without eyeball-to-eyeball building is null.

Source: [[Research/ltd-amway/info-sessions/2026-07-02-chris-colgrove-zach-ana-distillate]]

## Depth architecture

Personal activity is ignition. Durable Platinum requires **distributed** activity:

- Target ~80 MG1s/month in depth, not only personal.
- Platinum org composition (heuristic): ~50 volume IBOs; 3–4 plan-showers; 1–2 getting results; 1 making a multi-year commitment.
- Depth-to-PV rule of thumb: 3 deep ≈ 1k PV; 5 deep ≈ 2.5–4k; 7 deep ≈ 7.5k (correlation, not guarantee).
- Bodies ≠ builders. 25 people who want to build ≠ 25 names.

## Mentorship mechanics

| Practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Over-communicate with upline | Counsel flow; later they communicate downline the same way |
| Pursue coaching; do not wait to be chased | Feedback loop must leave before it returns |
| Edify the right people in context | Transfers trust without becoming a praise economy |
| Protect sacred upline moments | Borrow mind more than time; make contact precious |
| Partner on plan shows | Belief transfers faster eyeball-to-eyeball than alone |
| Fruit over flaws | Do not use leaders' gaps as your excuse not to grow |
| Raise standards on who you sponsor | "He quit on me" often means selection failure |

## Leadership progression (practical)

1. **Self-leader:** calendar, personal use, customers, conversations without mood veto.
2. **Partner:** brings people to the system; studies the plan.
3. **Builder:** sponsors and develops; weekly scoreboard with team.
4. **Depth leader:** multiplies plan-showers; 80 MG1s org-wide starts to appear.
5. **Platform leader:** character can carry pin; systems survive quits; FP groups emerge.

Pin without depth is fragile. Goal is **Platinum right**, not merely fast Platinum.

## Duplication drills (weekly with each hard runner)

1. Joint list review and open practice.
2. One observed meeting (they watch / they run / debrief).
3. One customer or product conversation reviewed.
4. One upward communication modeled, then required from them to their people.
5. One number fixed: shows, no-shows, sponsors, or depth MG1s.
6. One identity/posture check: defending vs qualifying; needy vs free.

## Anti-patterns

- Doing all the work so "it gets done" (kills duplication).
- Personality cult instead of teachable process.
- Edification chasing as self-worth.
- Blind loyalty without inspectable feedback (Elevate tension: trust coaches *and* measure).
- Depth talk without weekly distributed activity metrics.
- Using LTD as identity capture instead of leadership incubator.
