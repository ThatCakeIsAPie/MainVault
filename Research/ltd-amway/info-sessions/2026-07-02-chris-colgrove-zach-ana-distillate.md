# LTD Info Session distillate — 2026-07-02 (Thursday week)

## Chris Colgrove — framing

- **How you want to live** vs **what you want to be** — path-changing question for 18–30 economic pressure (housing +70% vs income +20%, debt traps).
- **Tangibles vs intangibles** — finances as unlock for community impact people wished they could fund.
- **Real mentorship** = 1:1 coaching, not parasocial “mentors”; LTD team fills gaps.
- **Corporate ceiling** — promotion only if boss dies; no employer plan for *your* goals; LTD **12–120 month** financial plan contrast.
- **Who > what** — *The people I work with is far more important than what I am doing.*
- **Product freedom** — sell what you believe in; 300 SKUs, not slave to catalog.

## Zach & Ana Matsumoto — platinum system (4 skills)

1. **Listening** — 2–3 LTD audios/day (not random podcasts); mindset + leadership.
2. **Affirmations** — 2–3×/day, written, time-bound goals (programs mind/self-talk).
3. **Reading** — 30 min or 30 pages, **printed books** (~2/mo); Ana: audio doesn’t stick like print.
4. **Radical association** — everything, Night Owls non-optional for growth stories.

**System law:** motivation ≠ system; *A system is built on a system followed consistently.* Education without **eyeball-to-eyeball** building is null.

**Stats honesty:** FTC “99% lose” mixes **zero-inflated** signups (50% quit in 30 days). Active builders ≠ industry denominator. Platinum ≈ **~50 IBO volume**, few show plan, **1–2** getting results — conviction stays with **upline plan**, not comparison.

**Platinum posture:** *How much can I serve?* Unapologetic work habit; skill via **blunt-force reps**; rising tide with friends.

## Lyle-relevant hooks (post-layoff / VXE)
