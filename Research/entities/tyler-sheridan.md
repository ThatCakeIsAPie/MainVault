# Tyler Sheridan

Tyler Sheridan is a recurring LTD Amway speaker/leader in the user's notes. His sessions emphasize win-win mentorship, family legacy, leverage through helping others build, endurance, and becoming the kind of person who can lead others.

## Repeated Themes

- The business should be people helping other people win, not a zero-sum extraction model.
- Mentorship has to be earned through performance and showing up; free help does not mean entitlement to unlimited leader time.
- Network marketing is framed as "leveraged leadership income": build once, then earn as others win.
- Family legacy and purpose-driven marriage are treated as major reasons for building.
- Long-term thinking matters: wealthy people think in decades while poor people think in days, weeks, or months.
- Endurance and remaining with the flock/community are presented as protections against quitting or emotional decision-making.
