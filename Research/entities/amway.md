# Amway

Amway is the product-distribution company underlying the user's LTD Amway notes. In the current wiki, Amway appears primarily as the business/product platform through which the broader [[ltd-amway]] mentorship and leadership-development environment operates.

## Current Wiki Relevance

The notes use Amway less as an isolated company case study and more as the operational substrate for recurring principles:

- Low-startup-cost business ownership and product flow
- Product usage as integrity signal for business owners
- Team growth and customer/product volume as income mechanisms
- Product distribution connected to leadership development and duplication

## Tyler Sheridan Session Notes

The 2026-06-04 Tyler Sheridan notes emphasize that business owners should use the products they sell, that product flow matters, and that the business can produce leveraged income through helping others build their own businesses. The same notes also mention Amway-operated farms dating back to the 1930s and product claims around Double X phytonutrients. ^[Business/LTD Amway/Info Sessions/2026/2026-06-04 Tyler Sheridan.md]
