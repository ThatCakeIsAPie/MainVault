# Tony Robbins

Tony Robbins is one of Lyle Cole's named influences, especially around identity, belief, state, and behavioral change.

## Relevance

The most important wiki connection is the identity-as-thermostat idea: behavior tends to return to a person's identity set point unless the identity itself changes. Lyle connected this with theological heart-change framing, making Robbins useful but not final authority in the framework.
