# Tony Robbins

Tony Robbins is one of Lyle Cole's named influences, especially around identity, belief, state, and behavioral change. A second, later cluster of material is his personal-finance / investing teaching (Money series and *The Holy Grail of Investing*), often delivered with co-author/investor Christopher Zook (CAZ Investments).

## Relevance

The most important early wiki connection is the identity-as-thermostat idea: behavior tends to return to a person's identity set point unless the identity itself changes. Lyle connected this with theological heart-change framing, making Robbins useful but not final authority in the framework.^[raw/transcripts/lyle-tony-robbins-identity-thermostat-2026-06-04.md]

On the finance side (Modern Wisdom interview with Chris Williamson, 2026-09-21), Robbins frames wealth around (1) Ray Dalio's "holy grail" of 8–12 non-correlated investments, (2) three money buckets (security / growth-risk / dream), and (3) a claimed retail-access shift into alternatives (SEC / DOL 401(k) pathways). Treat performance claims and regulatory details as contested until independently verified.^[raw/transcripts/youtube-DuRcrbP3kag-2026-09-21.md]
