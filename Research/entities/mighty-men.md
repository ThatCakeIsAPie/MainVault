## 2026-11-21 — The Most Mighty Principle (Keith A. Craft)

Published 3-page handout. Raw: [[raw/documents/mighty-men-2026-11-21-published-notes]]. PDF: `raw/assets/mighty-men/2026-11-21-published-notes.pdf`. ^[raw/documents/mighty-men-2026-11-21-published-notes.md]

The printed title is “The Most Mighty Principle for any Man that wants to be Most Mighty for God.”

Core points from the published notes (not Lyle's take):

- “Whatever is first place in your life, will direct how you live your life.”
- A principle is a foundational truth that guides how you Think, Be, and Do everything.
- Most people live by preferences (opinion-based) rather than principles (truth-based). The notes chain preference-based living through personality, stubborn opinions, likes and dislikes, a fixed mindset, false pride and a fragile ego, insecurity, then self-sabotage and low performance.
- “A Principle-based life removes the weakness and low level ROI of ‘preference-based’ living.”
- The Most Mighty Principle is Matthew 6:33 NKJV: seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added.
- “Whatever force that has FIRST place in your life, will drive every decision and shape your future.”
- “The most important thing you will ever do is also the most difficult thing you will ever do and that is to lead yourself well.”
- If you want to be more than “that,” put God first in every area of life, starting with money.
- “Men you are NOT MIGHTY if you don’t live the MOST MIGHTY PRINCIPLE of the Word of God.”

The notes also say the answer to “Steve Krofts question” is this principle — “Most Mighty…for God not Most Mighty for WHAT?!” The handout does not state what Kroft’s question was.
