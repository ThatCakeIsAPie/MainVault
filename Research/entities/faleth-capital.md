# Faleth Capital

Faleth Capital is Lyle Cole's family-office / private-index-fund architecture for building businesses that eliminate the wage/salary model and align operators, contributors, customers, and capital around value creation rather than fixed labor cost.

## Core Thesis

Faleth treats business as a discipleship and economic-governance vehicle, not merely a profit engine. The model assumes that compensation, equity, and governance should be tied to contribution, stewardship, and long-term alignment rather than job titles or speculative valuation.

## Structural Ideas

- Compensation should follow [[offshoots/contribution-over-wage-compensation]], not fixed wage/salary logic.
- Equity should be earned through real contribution and reinvestment, as in [[offshoots/earned-equity-through-contribution]].
- Governance should be handled by contributors bearing the consequences of the system, as in [[offshoots/self-governance-by-contributors]].
- The business should become a repeatable operating system / factory, tying to [[offshoots/factory-over-product-thinking]].

## Relationship to Lyle's Sequencing

Faleth is downstream of the current sequencing model. The practical path runs through active-income acceleration, [[ltd-amway]] leadership development, and then subsidiaries that can be plugged into the Faleth structure.
