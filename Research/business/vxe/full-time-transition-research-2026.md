# VXE Full-Time Transition Research — 5 Paths

**Objective**: Leave day job and reach $800–1,000+/week personal income via VXE (SDVOSB procurement & supply chain, McKinney facility for gov inspections + in-house repairs).

**Key Assets**:
- $400k deal at 50% margins ($200k gross profit)
- SDVOSB certification (major federal contracting advantage)
- Existing McKinney presence
- Goal: proper facility + hiring offset via grants

**Research Sources** (June 2026):
- VXE Corp: veteran-owned / SDVOSB procurement & supply chain (vxecorp.com)
- McKinney industrial rents: $14–19/SF NNN
- McKinney Innovation Fund: up to $200k–500k non-dilutive
- TWC Skills for Small Business: $2k per new hire training grant
- Texas Enterprise Fund: too large (25–75+ jobs)
- SBA SDVOSB set-asides + 7(a) loans
- McKinney Chapter 380 tax grants/abatements

---

## Path 1: Aggressive Local Grant + SDVOSB Facility Play (Fastest Scale)

**Core Idea**: Use McKinney Innovation Fund + Chapter 380 incentives + SDVOSB status to secure facility and first hires quickly, funded by the $400k deal profit.

**Timeline to $1k/week**: 4–7 months (once facility enables inspection/repair capacity and 2–3 additional contracts).
**Capital Required**: $80–150k (facility deposit + buildout + 2–3 hires). $200k from deal covers most.
**Grant Leverage**: McKinney Innovation Fund ($100–200k), TWC Skills ($2k/hire), possible Chapter 380 tax relief.
**Risks**: Grant approval timeline (2–4 months), competition for Innovation Fund, need to show job creation + capital investment.
**Dependencies**: Strong application positioning VXE as supply chain innovation for government.
**Next Actions**:
- Apply to McKinney Innovation Fund (pre-qualify via uniquemckinney.com)
- Contact McKinney EDC / MEDC for Chapter 380 discussion
- Get SDVOSB certification confirmation + start pursuing set-aside opportunities
- Model 3,000–5,000 SF facility at $15–18/SF

---

## Path 2: $400k Deal Bootstrap + Phased Facility (Lowest Risk)

**Core Idea**: Close the $400k deal first, use the $200k profit to self-fund a modest facility and 1–2 key hires without waiting on grants. Add grants later as icing.

**Timeline to $1k/week**: 3–6 months (fastest personal income path if deal closes cleanly).
**Capital Required**: $60–120k from deal profits. Very low external capital needed.
**Grant Leverage**: Apply for TWC Skills after first hires; McKinney incentives once cash-flow positive.
**Risks**: Deal execution risk is the single point of failure. Slower facility = slower capacity for inspections/repairs.
**Dependencies**: Reliable close of the $400k contract.
**Next Actions**:
- Confirm $400k deal timeline, payment schedule, and margin reality
- Identify minimum viable facility (even 2,000–3,000 SF) that enables in-house work
- Build 90-day cash flow model assuming only the one deal + existing pipeline
- Delay non-essential hires until deal cash hits

---

## Path 3: Hybrid Grant + SBA 7(a) Loan + Deal Profits

**Core Idea**: Combine the $400k deal profit + McKinney/TWC grants + an SBA 7(a) loan for the facility buildout. Uses SDVOSB status for both contracting preference and potentially better loan terms.

**Timeline to $1k/week**: 5–8 months.
**Capital Required**: $150–250k total (loan covers 50–70%). Deal profit + grants reduce personal exposure.
**Grant Leverage**: TWC Skills for hiring, McKinney Innovation Fund or Chapter 380 for facility.
**Risks**: Debt service on loan, grant + loan approval timelines, need strong personal credit or collateral.
**Dependencies**: Ability to qualify for 7(a) (veteran status helps).
**Next Actions**:
- Speak with SBA district office (Texas) about SDVOSB + 7(a) for facility
- Prepare grant + loan package showing the $400k deal as revenue anchor
- Model debt service against projected margins from inspections/repairs

---

## Path 4: Faleth Capital Internal Funding + Strategic Build (Aligned Capital)

**Core Idea**: Treat VXE as a Faleth Capital portfolio company. Use internal capital or structured investment from Faleth to fund the McKinney facility and hiring, with clear ROI and operator upside.

**Timeline to $1k/week**: 6–9 months (depends on internal decision speed).
**Capital Required**: $100–200k (Faleth-funded or co-invested). Repaid from VXE profits.
**Grant Leverage**: Still pursue McKinney/TWC grants to reduce Faleth capital outlay.
**Risks**: Mixing personal/business entities, need clear governance and exit/repayment terms.
**Dependencies**: Lyle's willingness to deploy Faleth capital here vs. other opportunities.
**Next Actions**:
- Draft simple internal investment memo (use Faleth philosophy: operator upside, aligned incentives)
- Model 18–24 month payback using facility-enabled higher-margin repair/inspection work
- Decide on structure (loan from Faleth, equity stake, or revenue share)

---

## Path 5: Minimalist / Remote-First Then Facility (Safest Personal Income Ramp)

**Core Idea**: Stay lean/remote as long as possible. Use the $400k deal profit purely for personal runway + minimal contractor support. Only move to McKinney facility once you have 2–3 reliable contracts and proven $1k+/week personal draw.

**Timeline to $1k/week**: 2–4 months for income (slowest for facility/scale).
**Capital Required**: <$30k initially (mostly marketing, tools, subcontractors). Facility later when cash flow supports it.
**Grant Leverage**: Minimal early; apply once you have traction and can show job creation.
**Risks**: Slower competitive advantage in inspections/repairs. May miss some gov contracts that require facility.
**Dependencies**: Strong ability to manage inspections/repairs via subcontractors initially.
**Next Actions**:
- Build 6-month personal runway model from the $400k deal
- Identify which parts of the work can stay remote or subcontracted
- Set clear trigger points for facility move (e.g., "$X monthly recurring revenue + 2 inspection contracts")
