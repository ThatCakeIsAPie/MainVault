Systems and duplication create leverage so that results are no longer limited by personal time or effort.

### Rationale

The highest-leverage activity in network marketing (and business in general) is building systems that others can run and duplicate. Once a system exists, income and impact scale without proportional increases in personal input. This is the opposite of linear effort models where output is directly tied to hours worked.

### Examples from Practice

- Leaders repeatedly contrast "95% of income comes from built systems and investments" versus trading time.
- Duplication is described as the difference between owning 100% of your own effort versus 1% of 100 people's effort.
- Successful builders focus on teaching the system rather than doing all the work themselves.
- "Where there is no duplication, there is no systemized income."
- The factory mental model makes the same point one layer upstream: the product generates revenue, but the factory generates products. The best operators build the repeatable system that creates outputs, leaders, and future capacity. ^[raw/transcripts/Trust_Effective_Effort_and_The_Factory.md]
