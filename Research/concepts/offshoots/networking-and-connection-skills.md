Networking and genuine connection are treated as learnable, teachable skills rather than innate talents.

### Rationale

The ability to connect with people, understand their pain, and build relationships is presented as a skill that can be practiced and improved, especially by starting with an "A and B list" of warm contacts. This lowers the barrier for new people and makes leadership development more accessible.

### Examples from Practice

- "The most important skillset is networking."
- "Networking is a learnable, teachable skill."
- Leaders recommend practicing connection skills on friends first, then expanding.
- Emphasis on asking coaches how to "increase my connection skills."
