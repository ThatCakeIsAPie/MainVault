# Intelligence as Compression and Correlation

> **Intelligence is compression and correlation.**

On 2026-11-23, Andrew Carr (@andrew_n_carr) wrote: "Ilya wasn't joking when he said intelligence is compression." The post names only "Ilya." Elon Musk replied: "It is compression and correlation." Lyle shared the reply. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2125175732758520056.md]

In the same thread, after someone wrote "knowledge != intelligence," Andrew replied: "I know! And that's why Ilya's point is controversial, because he's not arguing that knowing more is compression." ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2125175732758520056.md]
