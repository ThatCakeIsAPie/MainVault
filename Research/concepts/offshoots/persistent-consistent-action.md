Long-term success in this model requires persistent, consistent daily action over an extended period (typically framed as a 2–5 year plan).

### Rationale

The business rewards people who show up consistently even when results are slow. Small efforts practiced day in and day out compound into significant outcomes. This filters out those looking for quick wins and selects for those willing to treat it as a real profession.

### Examples from Practice

- Multiple leaders describe it as a "2-5 year plan of consistent, persistent action."
- Success is framed as "the sum of small efforts practiced day in, day out."
- People who fail often do so because they were not really trying consistently rather than lacking skill.
