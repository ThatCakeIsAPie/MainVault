# Ongoing Spirit-Filling and Gifts for the Common Good

The Holy Spirit is not a one-time deposit at conversion only. Ephesians 5:18 frames **be filled** as a present imperative—an ongoing command to keep receiving what you cannot supply yourself. Empowerment is **power for a purpose** (witness, service, battle), and spiritual gifts are distributed for the **common good**, not personal display.

### Rationale

Church notes from the 2026 "Spirit-Filled Life" series repeat the same mechanics: passive voice (you do not fill yourself), plural audience (this is for everyone), and a progression from Spirit **with** → **in** → **upon** so that overflow draws people to Christ in you, not to you. Anointing marks consecration for decisions, worship, spiritual warfare, prayer, witness, and gifts—but **fruit** signals maturity more than giftedness.

This connects to [[offshoots/faith-anchored-action-and-obedience]] (ask, believe, receive) and [[offshoots/character-assessment-through-fruit-inspection]] (fruit over flashy gifts).

### Examples from Practice

- "Be filled" means **keep being filled**—not a suggestion but a continuing imperative.
- Subsequent encounters with the Spirit after conversion: believe it is available, ask, receive (like refreshment in a race).
- 1 Corinthians 12:7: manifestations of the Spirit are given **for the common good**—not for the gifted person's ego.
- "The fruit is a sign of spiritual maturity, not the gifts."
- Mentally assenting to the Holy Spirit without engaging Him is a recurring failure mode; participation is required.
- The flesh **drives**; the Holy Spirit **leads**—including in business decisions and presence.

### Why It Matters

Without ongoing filling, people substitute externals (status, substances, control) for peace. Without the common-good frame, spiritual gifts become weird or divisive. The mature pattern is repeated reception plus fruit inspection.
