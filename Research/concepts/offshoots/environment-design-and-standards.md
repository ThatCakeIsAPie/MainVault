Your environment (the people and culture you surround yourself with) will either lift you or pull you down, so deliberately design it and hold high standards of excellence.

### Rationale

Conferences emphasize that the people in your environment will "rub off on you." This includes both positive leadership environments (like HOAL or Freedom Fighters) and the need to maintain personal standards in how you show up (dress, communication, commitment level). Lowering standards to avoid disappointment is a common trap.

### Examples from Practice

- "If you allow them to, the people in your environment will rub off on you."
- "Match their commitment level to what they are doing, not what they say."
- "Having standards of excellence, do you dress like a business owner?"
- Leaders stress guarding communication rhythms and seeking out environments that have already produced the results you want.
