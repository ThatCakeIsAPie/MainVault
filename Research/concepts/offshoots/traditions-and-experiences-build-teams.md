Traditions and shared experiences are what turn a group of people into a real team with heart, belonging, and long-term loyalty — not just an organization or structure.

### Rationale

Conferences and leadership events repeatedly highlight that while structure and compensation plans are important, what creates deep connection and staying power are the traditions, memories, struggles, and experiences shared together. People join for the business but stay for the team and the culture.

### Examples from Practice

- "Traditions connect people, create stability in the organization."
- "What he missed the most about playing sports was the team. The struggles, the laughs, the memories. It’s not the field experiences, it’s the locker room together."
- "Turns a group of people into a team. This is what creates the heart-strings."
- Leaders intentionally create traditions and cool experiences with their teams because that is what people ultimately value more than stuff or money.
