The model prioritizes building something that generates returns without requiring continuous personal time input.

### Rationale

Most income sources are linear — you trade time for money and the income stops when you stop working. This approach emphasizes creating infrastructure (teams, systems, customer bases) that can continue generating value with reduced ongoing involvement. The distinction between "killing it and eating it" versus "owning an asset that generates dividends" appears as a recurring mental model.

### Examples from Practice

- Leaders describe the goal as doing the work once to create infrastructure that generates income without having to rebuild it repeatedly.
- The contrast between employee-style effort and ownership-style returns is drawn frequently in teaching.
- Long-term participants often measure progress by how much of their income has become less dependent on their personal hours.
