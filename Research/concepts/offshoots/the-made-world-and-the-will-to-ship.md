# The Made World and the Will to Ship

Three quotes Lyle saved together on 2026-11-10. They say the same kind of thing in different words: the world is made by people, shipping is hard because order is rare, and you can poke life and change it.

## Lyle's take

He loves these quotes. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119894036316398055.md]

## The world is made

John Collison (@collision), May 2022: ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119894036316398055.md]

> As you become an adult, you realize that things around you weren't just always there; people made them happen. But only recently have I started to internalize how much tenacity *everything* requires. That hotel, that park, that railway. The world is a museum of passion projects.

The buildings, parks, and railways around you were not a given. Someone had to insist they exist.

## Shipping is a low-entropy fight

Michelle Pokrass (@michpokrass), July 2024: ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119894036316398055.md]

> shipping is so hard because it is a low entropy state. there are a million ways your launch can get derailed, but only a handful of ways that everything can line up in order to ship. the universe does not want you to ship, but you must do it anyway

This is the same idea from the other side. Making a thing real means holding a rare, orderly state long enough to finish. Most paths break. You ship anyway.

pranav (@prd_008) posted those two tweets together and wrote, "I think about these two tweets all the time." ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119894036316398055.md]

## You can poke life

A reply to that post, from Dermot McGrath (@dermotmcg), added a third quote. Carl Pei (@getpeid) had posted it in 2023, attributed to Steve Jobs: ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119894036316398055.md]

> The minute that you understand that you can poke life and actually something will, you know if you push in, something will pop out the other side, that you can change it, you can mold it. That's maybe the most important thing – is to shake off this erroneous notion that life is there and you're just gonna live in it, versus embrace it, change it, improve it, make your mark upon it.

Attribution is Pei's; it was not checked here against a primary interview transcript. The point sits next to Collison's line: life is not just a backdrop you live in. You can push on it.

## What this is not

These are not all Steve Jobs quotes. The two tweets in the original post are Collison's and Pokrass's.

A different Jobs-attributed line Lyle saved earlier — "great people don't compete" — is about staying in your own lane, not about making the world. That one is on [[offshoots/move-first-and-over-deliver]], with the same caveat that the attribution is unverified. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2108165794467074118.md]
