In evaluating potential partners, mentors, team members—or your own walk—prioritize observable **fruit** (character, relationships, results under pressure) over credentials, gifts, appearances, or self-reported spirituality. "Be a fruit inspector, check the fruit on the tree."

### Core Principle

If someone is not producing the results they claim or advise (e.g., not successful in business), their advice is unlikely to bear good fruit for you. Success in one domain does not automatically transfer; inspect the specific outcomes in the relevant area.

In church notes, **fruit is the metric Jesus gave for winning**—not giftedness you did not earn, not faked religiosity, and not personality rationalizing flesh. "The fruit is a sign of spiritual maturity, not the gifts." Proximity to you reveals fruit others already tasted: closest relationships are the honest mirror.

### Application in Business and Relationships

- The world is full of show and ego; surface-level signaling is common.
- Focus on heart and character rather than DEI checkboxes or polished presentations — proper character manifests across diverse people.
- Share your story selectively with those whose fruit you have inspected.
- In partnerships: "If you want to go platinum, start dressing like one" is secondary to actual behavior and results.
- Avoid committing to people whose advice lacks demonstrated success in the area they are guiding.
- Good fruit requires **integration** (same character in boardroom and home), is revealed **under pressure**, and is **experienced in relationships**—especially with people unlike you.
- Fruitful relationships: "You tell me you are so spiritual, but your relationships are a dumpster fire" is a valid inspection.
- If there is no breakthrough, the notes ask whether you have been **with Jesus**—fruitlessness traces to the vine, not only tactics.
- Faith That Works (James 2025): "Show me your fruit—show me the fruit of your relationships"; wisdom from heaven is full of mercy and good fruit.
- Values 301 Week 5: serving qualifications are **fruits of the Spirit**, not gift showmanship.
- Words-of-life MYA: those who speak life bear fruit; integration under pressure is the honest mirror.
