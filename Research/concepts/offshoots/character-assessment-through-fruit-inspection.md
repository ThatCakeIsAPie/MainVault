In evaluating potential partners, mentors, or team members, prioritize observable "fruit" (results, character, lifestyle) over credentials, appearances, or self-reported claims. "Be a fruit inspector, check the fruit on the tree."

### Core Principle
If someone is not producing the results they claim or advise (e.g., not successful in business), their advice is unlikely to bear good fruit for you. Success in one domain does not automatically transfer; inspect the specific outcomes in the relevant area.

### Application in Business and Relationships
- The world is full of show and ego; surface-level signaling is common.
- Focus on heart and character rather than DEI checkboxes or polished presentations — proper character manifests across diverse people.
- Share your story selectively with those whose fruit you have inspected.
- In partnerships: "If you want to go platinum, start dressing like one" is secondary to actual behavior and results.
- Avoid committing to people whose advice lacks demonstrated success in the area they are guiding.
