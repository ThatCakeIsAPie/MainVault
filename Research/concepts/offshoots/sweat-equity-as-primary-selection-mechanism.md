Because there is no financial buy-in or formal commitment required, consistent effort becomes the real filter that determines who succeeds.

### Rationale

In many business models, capital or credentials act as the primary gate. In this model, the absence of those requirements means that results are disproportionately driven by sustained personal investment of time and energy. This creates a self-selecting environment where those unwilling to put in the work naturally fall away.

The system is designed so that sweat equity is both the entry ticket and the ongoing differentiator.

### Examples from Practice

- Leaders repeatedly note that "there are no commitments, there is no financial equity — but because of that, there is sweat equity."
- The expectation is made explicit: sponsorship comes with the assumption that the new person will put in meaningful effort.
- This dynamic helps explain why some people with strong starts fade while others with slower starts eventually surpass them through consistency.
