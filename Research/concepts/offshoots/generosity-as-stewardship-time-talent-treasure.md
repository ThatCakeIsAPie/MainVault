# Generosity as Stewardship (Time, Talent, Treasure)

Everything belongs to God; believers are stewards. Generosity is not occasional overflow but **identity** ("I am generous"), expressed through time (seek first the Kingdom, refresh others), talent (gifts attached to the body), and treasure (tithe and offering as hard obedience backed by trust).

### Rationale

Values 301 and fall sermon series repeat the same barrier: **fear** (of not having enough, of being less generous than self-image). The antidote is trusting God as source, loving God more than money, and refreshing others so relational equity compounds. Generosity moves church vision forward and looks most like Jesus when giving.

### Examples from Practice

- "People don't feel what you do, they feel what you feel. When you are generous, they feel it."
- Three lanes: time (calendar guarder vs time giver), talent (body incomplete without your gifts), treasure (tithe = easy math, hard obedience).
- Proverbs 11:25 — whoever refreshes others will be refreshed.
- James thread: difference between "I will be generous" / "I have been generous" vs **"I am generous."**
- "If God can supply all my needs, I can be generous and add value to others."
- Psalm 112:5-6 — generous and just conduct tied to stability and being remembered.

### Why It Matters

Stinginess and fear shrink mission; generosity externalizes faith and funds souls-and-vision work (serve teams, MYA, the hurting). Connects to [[offshoots/faith-anchored-action-and-obedience]] and [[offshoots/purpose-and-vision-as-focusing-mechanisms]].
