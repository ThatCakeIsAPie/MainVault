# Goal, Current State, Path

Any useful attempt to improve a person, system, or organization must make three things legible:

1. **Goal:** What outcome, condition, or standard are we trying to reach?
2. **Current state:** Where are we actually now, without flattery or despair?
3. **Path:** What sequence of action can credibly move us across the gap?

> Here is the goal. Here is where we are. This is the path toward achieving it.

## Why all three are necessary

- **Goal without current state** produces fantasy. The destination may be attractive, but the plan has no honest starting coordinate.
- **Current state without goal** produces diagnosis without direction. It can become criticism, rumination, or sophisticated stagnation.
- **Goal and current state without a path** expose the gap but leave agency stranded. The result is often discouragement rather than movement.
- **Path without goal** produces activity theater. Progress cannot be judged because the intended destination is undefined.
- **Path without current state** prescribes steps for an imaginary person or system.

Together, the three form a minimal control loop: destination, position, trajectory. Action produces feedback, feedback updates the current state, and the path is recalibrated while the goal remains visible.

## Relationship to Lyle's existing architecture

This is the operational skeleton beneath [[queries/what-why-how-three-by-three-action-architecture]]. The **goal** clarifies the What. The **path** is the How. The honest **current state** tells us which part of the How matters now and whether the Why is producing action.

It also maps onto [[faleth/mindset/three-ps-motivation-2026]]:

- Push makes the current state costly enough to leave.
- Pull makes the goal attractive enough to pursue.
- Process makes the path credible enough to follow.

## Applications

### Coaching and performance

Name the standard, show the observed gap with evidence, then provide a development path. This makes feedback actionable rather than merely evaluative.

### Strategy

Define the target condition, establish a factual baseline, then sequence constraints and interventions. A strategic plan that skips the baseline is aspiration formatted as a document.

### Sales

Clarify the buyer's desired state, understand the present burden, and present the offer as the trusted path between them. This is the practical bridge between Push, Pull, and Process.

### Rejection

[[offshoots/rejection-that-preserves-agency]] works because the rejection identifies the standard, honestly locates the candidate relative to it, and leaves a credible route toward future eligibility.

## Guardrails

- The current state must be factual, not a weaponized interpretation.
- The goal must be specific enough to guide tradeoffs.
- The path must be credible from the actual starting point, not merely motivational.
- Reassess the path as evidence arrives; do not quietly move the goal to disguise failure.
- If the goal changes, name the change explicitly.

## Working rule

> Direction requires a destination. Progress requires a starting point. Agency requires a path between them.
