A clear vision and sense of purpose act as powerful filters that simplify decisions, priorities, reading lists, and relationships.

### Rationale

Without vision, people lack direction and become distracted by non-essential activities. Conferences and leadership events repeatedly emphasize that having "eyes straightforward" creates an aura and makes it easier to remove anything not aligned with your purpose. Purpose also shifts focus from profit to impact.

### Examples from Practice

- "If you don't have a vision, you don't have a sense of priorities... Those who have their eyes straightforward have an aura to them."
- "The more your focus is on impact, the less you care about profit. When you chase profit, you will lose purpose."
- HOAL and other major events are positioned as environments where purpose, cause, and mission are clarified and reinforced.
- Off-stage conversations and relationships often deliver more lasting impact than stage presentations.
