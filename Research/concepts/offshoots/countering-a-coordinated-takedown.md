# Countering a Coordinated Takedown

On November 9, 2026, Travis Kalanick (@travisk) posted twelve "Practical principles to counter a modern-day coup/psyop/struggle session." He framed it as writing to send to someone who may benefit. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119869150143091175.md]

This is advice for a public pile-on or a coordinated attempt to take someone down: own what you actually did, do not feed the mob half-truths, treat the event as the attackers' opportunity, keep a small loyal team, cut off people who will sacrifice you to protect themselves, do the hard preventive work you had been avoiding, and keep doing the work with your head up.

## Lyle's take

> Actually useful information regarding those types of situations. Unfortunate when stuff like that happens, but there are always going to be people who want to take you down, so this is legitimately decent advice.

## The twelve principles

Kalanick's wording, in full: ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2119869150143091175.md]

1. Own what you did but fiercely resist accommodating the mob, resist owning half-truths or actions/behaviors you didn’t do.
2. The op is not about your past actions, it’s about your enemies and this event as their opportunity
3. No matter how bad it gets, nothing is as bad as it seems.
4. Find the enemies at the very root of the op and find every creative problem-solving tool at your disposal to neutralize them.
5. People will throw their grandmother under the bus if they believe doing so will protect their reputation. They can become highly useful idiots in the op. Their power and access must be fiercely and quickly cut off.
6. Do not hesitate in cutting off anyone you suspect of disloyalty.
7. Build a team of close and loyal patriots and have one of them run a dedicated, well-resourced war room to put the rebellion to rest.
8. Use your patriots and regular meditation to buoy your psychological resistance to the op.
9. Your enemies aren’t as smart or hard working as you. Outsmart and outwork them as a way of life until the op is over.
10. Think about the op as if it were in the past. What are the difficult things you procrastinated on or avoided that would have stopped the op? Do them.
11. Never be ashamed of the op. Business as usual. Continue on with optimism and joy in your everyday work and its mission.
12. Download telegram and use it as your messaging application for all sensitive communications.

In the thread, people who otherwise agreed with the list objected to Telegram. Kalanick replied that he prefers it over Signal because of anti-screenshotting, that this blocks casual copying, and that Telegram secret chat is end-to-end encrypted. That is his preference. It was not checked here against how the two apps actually work.

## What this is not

This is not Faleth's internal conflict process. [[faleth/governance/conflict-escalation-trust-preservation-2026]] starts from trust by default and expands review only as harm grows. Kalanick is writing about an attack, including from people close to you, and he is much quicker to cut people off.
