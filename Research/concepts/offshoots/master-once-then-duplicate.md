True scale comes from mastering a skillset once and then teaching others to do it, rather than remaining the best individual performer forever.

### Rationale

You can be the best sponsor, the best volume builder, or the best at any single skill — but you will eventually hit a cap unless you duplicate yourself. The goal is to help others set the example themselves so you can move on to higher-leverage activities. This is the difference between linear growth and exponential team growth.

### Examples from Practice

- "You only have to get good once — enough to be able to duplicate it downline."
- "Train yourself out of a job."
- "You have to bring them in, train them how to think, how to speak, how to communicate upline, so they can learn how to communicate downline."
- Emphasis on teaching communication rhythms and thinking patterns rather than doing everything yourself.
