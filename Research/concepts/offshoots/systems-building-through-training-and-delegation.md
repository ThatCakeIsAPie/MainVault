True systems emerge not from doing tasks yourself but from investing in training others until they can execute independently at a high level. This is the mechanism that creates leverage and frees the leader's time.

### Key Insight
After training someone on a disliked task, realizing "a task that I didn't like to do has been taken off of my hands, but it is still getting done. THAT is the source of building a true system."

### How It Works
- Stand in the fire long enough to master the skill yourself.
- Develop the ability to train others effectively.
- The trained person now owns the outcome; you no longer need to check in constantly because it is their business too (in network marketing context).
- This compounds: each trained leader multiplies capacity without linear time input from the original builder.

### Contrast with Common Approaches
Most people attempt to build systems by documenting or automating their own repetitive work. The higher-leverage path is human development — creating other capable operators who replicate the process.

### Connection to Network Marketing
The model incentivizes exactly this: your income grows as your team grows and takes ownership. You cannot scale by doing everything yourself.
