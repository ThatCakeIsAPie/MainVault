# Offer Stacking

> **Bundle many cheap-to-serve perks into one subscription so the perceived value far exceeds the price.**

Named by @miroburn (*stackowanie ofert*) on a 2026-11-23 thread about Google AI Ultra. Lyle shared the follow-up post. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2124854140173955437.md]

## The idea

In online sales, offer stacking means packing a plan with extras — storage, a video subscription, credits, compute, branded tools — until the pile looks worth far more than the monthly price. The stack works when most of those extras are cheap to serve at the margin. That is the same utilization logic on [[offshoots/if-it-scales-its-free]]: once the infrastructure is already running, adding another perk can cost almost nothing, while the list still reads as huge value. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2124854140173955437.md]

The example here is Google's "Use Google AI Ultra benefits" page: one plan with a long list, including YouTube Premium Family, 20 or 30 TB of storage, Colab GPU time, Gemini, Flow, Antigravity, and more. miroburn had bought the highest Ultra and was especially struck that a GPU came in the price. Then he posted the list and wrote: in online sales, we call this offer stacking. ^[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2124854140173955437.md]

This also hits the worth wall in [[offshoots/marketing-first-principles]]: "that costs more than it's worth to me" is harder to say when the stack looks this large.
