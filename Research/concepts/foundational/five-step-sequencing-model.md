# Five-Step Sequencing Model

Lyle's framework for sequencing major life/business commitments rather than pursuing them simultaneously.

## Core Insight

Pursuing all major commitments at once fragments attention too severely. The sequencing logic is grounded in lived experience — Lyle ran the experiment and concluded it doesn't work.

## The Steps

1. **Foundation** — (details from Lyle's earlier context)
2. **Maximize active income** — currently here; focused on government contracts business
3. **Redirect bandwidth** — toward network marketing (LTD Amway)
4. **Launch subsidiaries** — Faleth Capital subsidiaries
5. **Scale** — (full ecosystem operation)

## Relationship to Faleth Capital

The sequencing model is the execution strategy for the [[foundational/faleth-capital-economic-philosophy]]. Each step builds the capital and capability base needed for the next. Network marketing sits in the middle as both income stream and leadership development pipeline — confirmed at Spring Forward 2026 LTD conference as the structural spine of the Faleth model.
