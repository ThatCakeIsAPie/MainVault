# Faleth Capital Economic Philosophy

The philosophical foundation underlying [[Faleth Capital]]'s structure.

## Core Principles

- **Revenue reflects value delivered** — not time spent, not credentials held, not position occupied
- **Building through downturns proves real value** — if you can't survive a contraction, you were riding momentum, not creating value
- **Faith and business are unified** — not separate domains. Biblical principles inform economic structure directly
- **Discipleship is the mission** — business ventures are vehicles, not ends in themselves

## Biblical Grounding

- Ecclesiastes on diversified income streams — "Ship your grain across the sea; after many days you may receive a return" (Eccl. 11:1-2)
- Talmudic one-third rule: split resources across land, business, and liquid capital
- Matthew 7:12 as operational principle — treat others as you'd want to be treated in every business interaction

## Economic Model

The Faleth architecture treats the family office as a private index fund where operators, investors, and customers all participate in upside. Labor costs flex with revenue (COF rate scales proportionally). No fixed payroll crisis. Anti-speculative by design — book value only, no market multiples.
