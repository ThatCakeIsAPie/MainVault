Network marketing, when approached seriously, functions primarily as a leadership development system rather than just a product distribution model.

### Rationale

The structure rewards people who can build and lead teams, develop others, and create repeatable systems. The compensation and recognition mechanisms are designed to incentivize leadership behaviors at scale. This makes it distinct from most traditional employment or small business models, where leadership development is often incidental rather than core to the activity.

The real product being developed is the leader, not just the volume of product moved.

### Examples from Practice

- Successful leaders consistently describe the business as a training ground for skills that transfer far beyond the company itself.
- The progression through pin levels correlates strongly with demonstrated ability to develop other leaders, not just personal sales volume.
- Many long-term participants report that the leadership, communication, and people-development skills gained were more valuable than the income itself.
