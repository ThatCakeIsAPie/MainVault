## Expanded Note Tweet

if you're about to run deepseek v4 flash 0731 on one dgx spark, let me save you 3 hours of head scratching.

pull IQ3_XXS. it's the only quant that loads fully on the gpu. everything bigger OOMs, because the 128gb box has to hold the model and the kv cache both, and once the model alone crosses ~116gb there's nothing left for it.

what fits and what dies is in the chart.

## Referenced-post context

The quoted post identifies DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 as a 284B-total / 13B-active MoE using a CUDA-enabled llama.cpp build for GB10. It reports this quantization ladder on a 128 GB DGX Spark:

- UD-IQ3_XXS: 104 GB — fully GPU-resident.
- IQ3_S: 116 GB — too tight.
- Q3_K_M: 128 GB — OOM.
- Q3_K_XL: 128 GB — OOM.
- Q8_K_XL: 162 GB — does not fit.

GGUF source named by the post: https://huggingface.co/unsloth/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731-GGUF

These are practitioner measurements for one runtime/configuration, not controlled independent benchmarks.
