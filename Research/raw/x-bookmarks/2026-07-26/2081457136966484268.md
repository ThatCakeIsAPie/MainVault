## Text

My father banned me from attending his funeral.

He told me six months before he died.

“I’m leaving you something more valuable than money. But if you come to my funeral, you’ll never get it.”

Everyone thought he was joking.

I didn’t.

When he passed away, my entire family called me selfish.

My mother cried.

My uncles called me ungrateful.

Even my younger sister stopped speaking to me.

The morning of the funeral, I stayed home.

I turned off my phone.

For three days, I was the villain in the family.

Then a lawyer called.

He handed me a sealed envelope written in my father’s handwriting.

Inside was a single sentence.

“Now you know who loves you for you, and who only loved the version of you that obeyed me.”

There was another page.
