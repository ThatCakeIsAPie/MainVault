RTX 5090
unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF
Qwen3.6-27B-Q6_K.gguf
→ https://t.co/DeoZRxpMsx

2x RTX 3090 / 2x RTX 5090 
unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF
Qwen3.6-27B-Q8_0.gguf
→ https://t.co/13lY17GUvu

1x DGX Spark / 1x RTX 6000 Pro
nvidia/Qwen3.6-27B-NVFP4
