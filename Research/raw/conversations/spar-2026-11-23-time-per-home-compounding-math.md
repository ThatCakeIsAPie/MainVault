# Spar distillate — Lyle's time-per-home compounding math (2026-11-23)

Source: Spar, from the 2026-11-23 conversation with Lyle. Meant to sit with `Business/Faleth Capital/One Billion Homes/One_Billion_Homes.md` section 6 (Speed). His illustrative example, not a forecast.

## Lyle's words

"If you put in $500k and get out $600k, and it takes you 12 months, that is 20% APR. If you can do the same, but in 1 month, that is the same math, but in 1 month instead, and compounds 12 times in the same time span as prior."

## Spar's arithmetic on his example

20% per build, all of it put back in:
- 1 build a year turns $500k into $600k.
- 2 a year: $720k.
- 4 a year: about $1.04M.
- 12 a year: about $4.46M (1.2 to the 12th power is about 8.92).

The margin per home and the price never change; only the time per home does.
