# Tristan Ghazal — public account of Amway termination after 20 years

## Evidence status

This note preserves **Tristan Ghazal's public account and quoted Instagram comment**. It does not establish that every allegation or quotation is independently true. No public Amway, LTD, Joe Markiewicz, Robert Routh, or Derly Treviño response was located during this intake. Distinguish:

- **Observed:** Tristan published the statements below publicly; the attached screenshot contains the quoted comment.
- **Claimed by Tristan:** the complaint was malicious/false; his upline worked against him; LTD did not help; private material was used against him; named people made the quoted statements.
- **Independently corroborated:** Amway publicly describes IBO arrangements as annually renewed contracts in its 2026 Business Reference Guide/Rules of Conduct materials.

## Lyle's firsthand context

Lyle says he has spoken with Tristan a number of times and that Tristan's concern about suppression of his speech predates this public termination account. Lyle's concern is closely aligned: if speaking freely and attempting to become more effective in business can place the entire business at risk of termination, then the participant's agency is structurally conditional.

This is useful corroboration of **the longevity of Tristan's concern**, not independent proof of the complaint details, attributed quotations, or Amway's motives.

## Public post — transcript

> Over the last few months, my 20‑year Amway journey came to a painful end.
>
> In late April, Robert Routh and Derly Treviño filed a complaint about my role with Globe Life Family Heritage, my day job; we lived solely on Amway 2017–2024. GLFH isn’t an MLM, and Robert was never impacted by my FH career; we hadn’t spoken since before I started. In my view the complaint was malicious and false, and it hurt many.
>
> Based on that complaint and the rumors, over a dozen IBOs connected to FH were suspended on hearsay, by email. Within weeks the others were reinstated. Ours was the only one that stayed suspended, told our case would go to formal mediation under Rule 11 over “other facts.”
>
> While still suspended, older 2023–2024 training talks and private venting messages I’d sent our team were submitted to Amway, forwarded from our chat. Nothing I said was untrue, just against newer QAS rules. I acknowledged crossing the line and apologized. Even so, Amway terminated our business, canceled mediation, and said we’d not be welcome to re‑register. Twenty years ended in one email. Our upline worked against us, LTD didn’t help, and IBOAI pushed mediation while others connected to FH got back in.
>
> Many have asked, “What happened?” At one point we were in the top 0.09% of IBOs in North America, every incentive and trip, part of our upline’s “inner circle.” When I refused to shut down my Spotify channel, I was removed from that circle; that upline fanned the rumors instead of checking.
>
> If an outside day job, old talks, and private venting—fueled by false complaints—can end things at that level, the rules have changed. You’re called an “independent business owner,” but you own none of it—the annual renewal makes sure of that.
>
> As for me and Jenny, this chapter is closed. Even if let back in, our reputation’s damaged, and it would come with an unspoken “be a good wittle boy” asterisk. We’re deeply proud of The Ghazal Team, and grateful for everyone who grew with us. We wish the best for those who stay in Amway, just be clear‑eyed and careful. Our focus now is Ghazal Protection Group (GLFH) and more when the time is right.
