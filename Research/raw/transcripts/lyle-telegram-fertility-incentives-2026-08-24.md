Something I've studied regarding the fertility crisis is that it actually all comes down purely to incentives. There is no incentive to have more kids, simply put.

I put out a reply to someone on X who was talking about housing being passive income for boomers being the issue, and this was my response:
The problem with that is that families who are more well off, on average, are also below the threshold.

There is no data to prove that money is actually the limiting factor here, as much as it might seem like it at first.

You would think not enough money would be the main factor given the state of the economy, but oddly enough it is not. The only groups that do are certain church going folk.

The real problem is that kids add no economic value to a family. When on the farm, they were free labor. Then, when we moved into cities and abolished child labor, kids consume until they move out. They only make parents' lives harder, economically speaking, not emotionally.
