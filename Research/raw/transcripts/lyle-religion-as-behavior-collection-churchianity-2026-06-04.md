# Lyle Cole — Religion as Behavior Collection and Church-ianity

Source: Conversation with Lyle Cole, 2026-06-04.

Lyle clarified that when he says "religion" in this context, he means a collection of behaviors rather than a transformed relationship with Christ. This is the distinction between being a follower of Christ and merely being "christian" in the cultural or behavioral sense.

He noted that modern Christianity is sometimes called "church-ianity" for this reason: people can perform church behaviors, adopt Christian labels, attend services, and follow visible norms while missing the heart-level relationship that should produce the fruit naturally.

Implication: in Lyle's theological framing, "religion" is not primarily a denominational category here; it is external compliance without inward transformation. Relationship with Christ is the root-level change from which congruent behavior flows.
