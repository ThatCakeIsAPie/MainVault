# Lyle Cole — Tony Robbins Identity Thermostat Note

Source: Conversation with Lyle Cole, 2026-06-04.

Lyle noted that the idea "identity shapes belief and action" is consistent with other material he has seen and heard, especially Tony Robbins' framing that people act to verify their identity.

Robbins' analogy: identity functions like a thermostat. If someone's identity is set to 78 degrees and they drift down to 60, the subconscious "turns on the heater" to bring them back up. If they drift up to 90, it "turns on the air conditioner" to pull them back down. Applied financially, someone who believes themselves to be broke may subconsciously self-sabotage when they begin doing well. Someone who believes themselves to be wealthy may feel compelled to work hard and restore congruence with that identity.
