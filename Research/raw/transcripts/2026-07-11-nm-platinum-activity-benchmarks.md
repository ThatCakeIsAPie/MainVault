# NM Platinum (7500PV) Activity & Structure Benchmarks

Reference numbers for what bare-bones platinum-level activity and org structure actually look like, based on direct field experience.

## Baseline Activity Required

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | 10 conversations |
| Daily | 3 "opening the doors" |
| Weekly | 4 booked MG1s (meet and greet, first meeting) |
| Monthly | 10 MG1s run |
| Monthly | 1–2 new sponsors |
| Annual | 12 new legs |
| Ongoing | 80 total monthly MG1s in depth (across the whole team, not just personally) |
| Team-wide | 150PV per seat average, 50 people total |

## Platinum Leg Structure

A platinum business is typically shaped like:

- One **4000PV** leg
- One **2500PV** leg
- One **1000PV** leg
- A handful of smaller **150–600PV** legs filling out the rest

## Depth-to-PV Correlation

Roughly, how deep a leg runs correlates to its PV size:

- **3 legs deep** → ~1000PV leg
- **5 legs deep** → ~2500–4000PV leg
- **7 legs deep** → ~7500PV leg

## The Sponsor Funnel

Out of every 4 people sponsored:

- **1** quits within 3 months
- **1** sticks around for the environment/culture, but doesn't build
- **1** incubates for an undefined amount of time
- **1** actually runs the business

Because of this ratio, it typically takes **12–15 total sponsors** to find the **3 core legs** that end up carrying the business.

## Timeline

- **Year 1:** Build the initial team.
- **Year 2:** That team scales and develops in depth.

_Total: roughly a 2-year arc from a standing start to a platinum-shaped organization, assuming the baseline activity above is sustained._
