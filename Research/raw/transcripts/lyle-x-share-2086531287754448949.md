## What was released

Unsloth published GGUF quantizations of MiniMax H3's pruned FL2VA denoiser and its Qwen3-VL-32B text encoder. The smallest published denoiser is Q2_K at 6.26 GiB. The smallest accompanying text encoder is Q2_K_M at 12.20 GiB.

The release covers the pruned first-and-last-frame H3 variant, which accepts text plus zero, one, or two images. It is not the full Ref2VA omni-reference stack.

## Evaluation of the 8 GB claim

“Fits 8 GB” is defensible only in the operational sense that a compatible runtime can place one component or part of a component on the GPU while offloading the remainder to system RAM or CPU. It is not true if “fits” means the complete model remains resident in 8 GB VRAM.

The two core GGUF files alone total 18.46 GiB, before the video VAE, audio VAE, activations, latent tensors, CUDA workspace, and application overhead. Even the 6.26 GiB denoiser leaves only 1.74 GiB of nominal space on an 8 GB card before runtime buffers. The text encoder cannot fit by itself at 12.20 GiB and therefore must be run through CPU/system-memory offload or partial placement.

## Lyle hardware implication

For Lyle's GTX 1070 Ti, the GGUF release changes H3 from “essentially unavailable” to “possibly runnable as an offloaded experiment.” It does not establish acceptable speed. Pascal compatibility, low-bit kernel performance, system RAM, storage bandwidth, output resolution, frame count, and activation memory remain decisive. The post and model card provide no GTX 1070 Ti timing or peak-memory benchmark.
