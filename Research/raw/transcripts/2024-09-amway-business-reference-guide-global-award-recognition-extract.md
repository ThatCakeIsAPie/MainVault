# Amway Business Reference Guide — Global Award Recognition extract

## Provenance

Lyle supplied the U.S. English **Amway Business Reference Guide**, which states on its cover that it is current as of **September 2024**. Both Telegram uploads were byte-identical; one canonical PDF was retained.

Original: [[Amway-Business-Reference-Guide-USEN-2024-09.pdf]]

Full searchable extraction: [[2024-09-amway-business-reference-guide-full-text]]

This extract focuses on pages A-12 and A-13 (PDF pages 18–19), which define Diamond and the Global Award Recognition levels.

## Diamond / Founders Diamond

The guide states:

- **Diamond:** six or more registered, internationally registered, or foster-registered sales groups; three must be North American; each qualifies at the 25% Performance Bonus level for at least six months of the Performance Year.
- **Founders Diamond:** Emerald Bonus qualification plus six or more sales groups qualifying at the 25% Performance Bonus level for all 12 months; Volume Equivalency may also qualify a group.

## Global Award Recognition table

For Executive Diamond and above, qualification combines **Founders Platinum (FP) Groups** for width with **Qualification Credits (QC)** for depth.

| Award level | Minimum FP Groups | Qualification Credits | Maximum QC per group tier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Executive Diamond | 6+ | 10 | Up to 6 |
| Founders Executive Diamond | 6+ | 16 | Up to 6 |
| Double Diamond | 8+ | 25 | Up to 9 |
| Founders Double Diamond | 8+ | 34 | Up to 9 |
| **Triple Diamond** | **10+** | **43** | **Up to 9** |
| **Founders Triple Diamond** | **10+** | **52** | **Up to 9** |
| Crown | 12+ | 64 | Up to 9 |
| Founders Crown | 12+ | 76 | Up to 9 |
| Crown Ambassador | 14+ | 88 | Up to 12 |
| Founders Crown Ambassador | 14+ | 100 | Up to 12 |

## What width and depth mean

The guide explicitly distinguishes the two:

- **Width:** sponsored Founders Platinum groups. Internationally sponsored groups and groups without a Founders Platinum do not count toward this width measure.
- **Depth:** Qualification Credits determined by the QC schedule and its per-group caps.
- A Founders Platinum can contribute Qualification Credits subject to the schedule.
- For a Founders Platinum to contribute more than one QC, the relevant multiple business must be an Emerald Bonus Recipient in its local market.
- Qualification Credits from Founders Platinums are limited to three per sales group.
- Award qualification is determined by the required combination of FP Groups and QCs, not by counting one Platinum as one QC throughout the organization.

## Correction to prior conversational shorthand

The prior phrase “10 Platinum legs wide with 52 Platinums in depth” compressed two different units incorrectly.

The official September 2024 U.S. formulation is:

- **Triple Diamond:** 10+ FP Groups **and 43 QCs**.
- **Founders Triple Diamond:** 10+ FP Groups **and 52 QCs**.

Therefore, **52 is a Qualification Credit requirement for Founders Triple Diamond, not a requirement for 52 Platinum organizations**. Multiplying 52 by the 7500PV Platinum heuristic is not supported by this guide.
