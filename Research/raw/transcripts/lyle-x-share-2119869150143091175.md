> Had a little time on my hands this morning to do some writing… send along to someone you know who may benefit
> ---
>
> “Practical principles to counter a modern-day coup/psyop/struggle session”
>
> 1) Own what you did but fiercely resist accommodating the mob, resist owning half-truths or actions/behaviors you didn’t do.
>
> 2) The op is not about your past actions, it’s about your enemies and this event as their opportunity
>
> 3) No matter how bad it gets, nothing is as bad as it seems.
>
> 4) Find the enemies at the very root of the op and find every creative problem-solving tool at your disposal to neutralize them.
>
> 5) People will throw their grandmother under the bus if they believe doing so will protect their reputation. They can become highly useful idiots in the op. Their power and access must be fiercely and quickly cut off.
>
> 6) Do not hesitate in cutting off anyone you suspect of disloyalty.
>
> 7) Build a team of close and loyal patriots and have one of them run a dedicated, well-resourced war room to put the rebellion to rest.
>
> 8) Use your patriots and regular meditation to buoy your psychological resistance to the op.
>
> 9) Your enemies aren’t as smart or hard working as you. Outsmart and outwork them as a way of life until the op is over.
>
> 10) Think about the op as if it were in the past. What are the difficult things you procrastinated on or avoided that would have stopped the op? Do them.
>
> 11) Never be ashamed of the op. Business as usual. Continue on with optimism and joy in your everyday work and its mission.
>
> 12) Download telegram and use it as your messaging application for all sensitive communications.
