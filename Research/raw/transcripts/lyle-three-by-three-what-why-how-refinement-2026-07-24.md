# Lyle's 3×3 What–Why–How Refinement

> I think I refined the 3 Ps far more now.
>
> It basically goes like this:
>
> What = Why x How
> In order to get what you want out of life, you need to figure out why you want it and how you will get it.
>
> Why:
> Push - Pull - Process
> What are you dead set on not happening?
> What do you want to happen?
> What can you do that motivates you just by doing it?
>
> What:
> Time - Talent - Treasure
> We all have our three resources. Each one is exchangeable for the others, though with different reactions.
> To get more treasure, you need to trade either time or talent or both. Most people trade time, but rarely have the talent to trade.
> How to get the talent to trade? You must first trade time or treasure or both. We already discussed you dont have treasure, so you need to spend the time to get the talent.
> Once you have both talent and treasure, you can trade to get back your time.
>
> Master those, you will get what you want out of life.

## Editorial note

Lyle confirmed in the same conversation: **“The what is the ultimate outcome. The how and why get the what.”** The repeated “What:” before Time–Talent–Treasure in the original message was therefore a heading slip; Time–Talent–Treasure is the **How**.
