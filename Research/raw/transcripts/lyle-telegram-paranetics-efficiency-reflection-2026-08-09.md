## Clean interpretation

Lyle sees legitimate ambiguity in the word “efficiency,” because the answer depends on the measurement boundary and operating condition. His concern is that this legitimate complexity can become a convenient way for a seller to avoid presenting decision-grade data.

The technology may have real potential, but novelty alone is not the question. Circumferential, rim-driven, and hub-style motors already offer several of the same packaging and torque advantages. ParaNetics must therefore be compared against credible alternatives using normalized performance data rather than against a vague category called “traditional motors.”
