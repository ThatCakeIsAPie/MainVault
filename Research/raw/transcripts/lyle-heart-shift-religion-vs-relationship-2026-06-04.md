# Lyle Cole — Heart Shift, Religion vs Relationship with Christ

Source: Conversation with Lyle Cole, 2026-06-04.

Lyle connected the identity-belief-action principle to the difference between mere religion and having a relationship with Christ. The key distinction is a heart shift: behavior is downstream from the heart, not the root cause. He cited the biblical phrase, "Out of the abundance of the heart, the mouth speaks," as a concise statement of the mechanism.

Implication: durable transformation cannot be reduced to behavioral compliance. If the heart/identity remains unchanged, outward behavior may be temporary, performative, or brittle. When the heart changes, speech, choices, discipline, and action begin to flow from a different internal source.
