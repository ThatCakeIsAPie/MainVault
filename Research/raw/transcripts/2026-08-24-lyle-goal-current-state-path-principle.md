## Immediate synthesis

The Valve rejection letter is one application of a general achievement loop:

1. State the goal or standard.
2. Describe the present state honestly.
3. Define a credible path across the gap.

The model applies to coaching, hiring, performance management, strategy, sales, personal development, and institutional communication.
