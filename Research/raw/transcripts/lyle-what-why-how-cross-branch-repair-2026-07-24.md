# Cross-Branch Repair in the What–Why–How Pyramid

## Conversation context

Delta observed that the branch containing the failure need not contain the remedy. For example, an intolerable Process is diagnosed under Why but may be repaired through How: greater Talent can make the work easier, Treasure can purchase tools or delegation, and Time can be redesigned or protected.

Lyle confirmed:

> Love it. Also love your point on the cross repair between the two sections, i didnt even consider that, but it makes total sense.

## Distilled claim

The pyramid's branches are diagnostically distinct but operationally coupled. The failed node locates the symptom; the repair may come from another node. Why governs the allocation and persistence of Time, Talent, and Treasure, while those resources can redesign the motivational experience of Push, Pull, and Process.
