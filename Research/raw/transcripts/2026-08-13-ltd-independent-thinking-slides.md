## Slide 1 — Independent Thinking

Title: INDEPENDENT THINKING
Subtitle: YOUR MIND IS YOUR GREATEST ASSET. USE IT.
Citation: Romans 12:2 — Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.

THE STATS DON'T LIE:
- 90% of people never step outside the influence of their close circle. Source: Harvard Business Review
- 85% of financial success is due to mindset and persuasion, not IQ. Source: Carnegie Institute of Technology
- 70% of people say fear of judgment holds them back from pursuing opportunities. Source: American Psychological Association
- 95% of people overestimate the risks and underestimate the rewards of taking action. Source: University of California, Berkeley

HOW FAMILY & FRIENDS TYPICALLY DISCOURAGE:
- "That's too risky." They project their fear onto your future.
- "Get a real job." They value security over freedom and don't understand the vision.
- "What will people think?" They live for approval, not purpose.
- "You could fail." They focus on worst-case scenarios instead of potential.
- "Just stay where you are." They fear change more than they want success.

POOR: PASSING OVER OPPORTUNITIES REPEATEDLY
- Opportunity rarely knocks twice.
- The same mindset that keeps you safe will keep you stuck.
- Every time you say "not now," someone else says "I'm ready."
- Your future self is watching the decisions you make today.

DON'T BE POOR WITH OPPORTUNITY. THINK. DECIDE. ACT.

Footer:
- Independent thinking isn't about rejecting people — it's about respecting your purpose.
- Think for yourself. Pray for wisdom. Move on opportunity.
- Your mind. Your decisions. Your destiny.

## Slide 2 — Active Income vs Ongoing Income

Subtitle: DIFFERENT MINDSET. DIFFERENT RESULTS.
Center: CASHFLOW QUADRANT — E Employee / B Business Owner / S Self Employed / I Investor

E EMPLOYEE: You earn money by working for others. Trades time for money. Limited control over income, hours, and job security.

S SELF EMPLOYED: You make money by working for yourself. Owns a job, not a system. High personal responsibility and limited time freedom.

B BUSINESS OWNER: You own a system that makes money for you. Leverages people, leadership, and systems. Income grows even when you're not working in it.

I INVESTOR: Your money (or other people's money) works for you. Focuses on assets, cash flow, and compounding. Builds long-term wealth and financial freedom.

Stat strip (treat as persuasion props, not independently verified here):
- 90% of people are stuck in the E quadrant. Source: Rich Dad Poor Dad
- 79% of millionaires are business owners or investors. Source: The Business Insider
- 80% of wealth is built through ownership, not income. Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics
- 65% of the next generation will do something different. Source: Deloitte Survey

Footer: SHIFT YOUR MINDSET. CHOOSE YOUR QUADRANT. CHANGE YOUR FUTURE. Stop trading time. Start building freedom.

## Slide 3 — Let's Think Together

Subtitle: THE NUMBERS DON'T LIE. THE CHOICE IS OURS.

- Median U.S. household income: $83,730 per year. Latest official nationwide figure available as of mid-2025.
- More than 90% of retirees receive income from Social Security. As of 2025, average SS retirement benefit for a retired worker is approximately $2,071 per month / about $24,850 per year.
- About 6% of 401(k) participants took hardship withdrawals in 2025 — the highest level Vanguard has reported. Pre-2020: around 2%. 2024: about 5%. 2025: 6% (highest Vanguard has ever recorded). That's a 200% increase compared to the pre-pandemic norm.
- Median retirement savings for households ages 65+: about $185,000.
- Only about 54% receive income from IRAs.
- Previous generations could count on retiring at 65. Today's workers are planning for 67. Tomorrow's workers may face an even later retirement age.

THE QUESTION ISN'T WHETHER YOU SHOULD KEEP YOUR JOB — IT'S WHETHER YOU'RE BUILDING INCOME AND ASSETS OUTSIDE OF IT SO YOUR FUTURE ISN'T DETERMINED SOLELY BY POLICY CHANGES.

Footer: THE BEST TIME TO PREPARE WAS YESTERDAY. THE NEXT BEST TIME IS TODAY. PLAN / BUILD / FREEDOM.
