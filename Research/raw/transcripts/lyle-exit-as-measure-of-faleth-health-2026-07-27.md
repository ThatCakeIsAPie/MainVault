## Distilled Delta

The rewritten Faleth documents already encode the anti-dependency safeguard through their overarching objective: build people until they no longer need a leader, another participant, or Faleth itself.

The three-income-stream architecture supports that end by reducing dependence on any single stream:

1. Faleth contribution income supports current life.
2. Network-marketing income develops and can become a self-replicating engine.
3. Bucket One investment returns create an independent personal floor.

As those streams mature, Faleth should become less necessary rather than more controlling.

## Exit as Health Signal

Actual use of the exit door is not necessarily organizational failure. It can indicate that:

- alignment changed honestly rather than being performed;
- the person developed enough capability and security to choose freely;
- earned value is genuinely portable rather than rhetorically owned;
- the Peaceful Fork works under real pressure;
- the institution values productive independence over retention statistics.

Some people will naturally drift out as their direction changes. Others may want to take a team, business, capability, or other value they built and continue independently. If the separation preserves what was earned and produces healthy independent builders, it may be evidence that Faleth fulfilled its purpose.

The useful distinction is between **healthy exit** and **preventable leakage**. Exit caused by maturing independence or changed alignment can validate the model. Exit caused by exploitation, opacity, broken promises, avoidable conflict, or economic failure reveals a defect. Raw retention therefore cannot serve as the health metric; the quality and cause of exits matter.
