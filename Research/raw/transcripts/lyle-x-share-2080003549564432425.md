## Distilled claim

Time, Talent, and Treasure form a resource-conversion system. When one resource is scarce, intentionally combine the other two to acquire it: business converts time and talent into treasure; education converts time and treasure into talent; investment, delegation, and systems convert talent and treasure into recovered time.
