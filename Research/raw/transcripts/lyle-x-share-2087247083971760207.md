## Why this hits

This is external product validation of the same first-principles bet Lyle already runs as **Delta on Hermes over Telegram**:

1. **Messaging is the OS surface** — not another dashboard app.
2. **Bots are teammates**, not chat toys — they should move work in real tools.
3. **Simple surface, serious infrastructure** — the product people bounce from is usually a clever chat wrapper without durable identity, memory, coordination, or tool depth.
4. **One ask replaces a pile of prompts** when the agent holds context, feedback, and coordination.

## Evidence limits

- Vendor-adjacent endorsement (Cursor Field CTO + xAI product launch), not independent third-party measurement.
- “Stopped opening most apps” is a subjective workflow claim.
- Tool sign-in / finished-work claims are product positioning; trust requires watching failure modes: auth scope, hallucination-as-done, review burden, cost, and multi-bot coordination breakage.
