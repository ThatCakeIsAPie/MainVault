## Clarification

For converting the daily activity standard into an annual workload, Lyle selected a **sustainable operating year of five active days per week for 50 weeks**.

## Canonical delta

- Personal sponsoring target: **12 people per baseline year**, with **16** as the stretch year.
- Expected 12-person cohort distribution: three quit, three stick without seriously building, three do something intermittently, and three run hard.
- Expected Platinum result: one of the three hard runners eventually produces a Platinum organization.
- The person who reaches Platinum may be deeper in the sponsored group; the personally sponsored frontline individual does not have to be the one who personally qualifies.
- Working conversion: **one eventual Platinum-bearing sponsored group per 12 personal sponsors**.
