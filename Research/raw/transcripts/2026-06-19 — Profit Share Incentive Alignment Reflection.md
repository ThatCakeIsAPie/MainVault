# 2026-06-19 — Profit Share Incentive Alignment Reflection

## Context

Lyle was reflecting on why more companies do not use profit sharing, especially when incentive alignment seems like one of the fastest ways to make people care about company performance.

## Transcript Excerpt

> Right, exactly. Now, I do think, because from a lot of business owners I have talked to, it does sound like the major reason primarily has been it's just the accounting is just so confusing, it's really hard to make sure you do it right and do it fairly, to prevent the free rider problem.
>
> But if you're willing to actually go through with that, it's like the quickest way you can actually boost productivity in a workforce, because you're giving them a reason to care about the company. If the company does well, you do well. It's a very direct correlation. It works really, really, really well. Why would you not do that?
>
> I feel like that's where my mindset is really on it.

## Source Thesis

Profit share is underused not because the incentive logic is weak, but because the accounting and fairness mechanics are hard. If a company is willing to solve those mechanics, profit share can become a direct productivity unlock by connecting company success to worker upside.
