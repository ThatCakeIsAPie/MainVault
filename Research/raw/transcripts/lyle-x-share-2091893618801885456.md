## Official product spec (not the tweet)

Docs: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/desktop#hud-mode

- Toggle: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+H or the titlebar button.
- Behavior: chrome-free always-on-top floating bar; main Desktop window steps aside; live conversation + composer remain.
- Context rule: **where the bar is parked is the referent**. "this", "here", and "that page" resolve to the app/screen underneath the HUD.
- Move: press-and-hold the composer, then drag. No titlebar.
- Resize: bottom-right corner.
- Snap to pointer: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+G (global).
- Exit: same toggle or the bar exit button; session intact.

Distinct sibling: **Quick Entry** (Settings → Advanced; default Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Space) is a global fire-off composer that does not require the main window. HUD is the persistent overlay with visual context. Voice still works in HUD (composer has dictation / wake word / spoken replies per third-party walkthrough).

## Independent operator walkthrough (corroboration)

Tonbi's AI Garage, 2026-08-17, 16:53: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fb-kiqgjTdk

Reported behaviors:

- HUD can identify and summarize the app/page underneath (Chrome/X, TradingView, Spotify, Steam, GitHub, Notepad, SSH terminal).
- Computer-use from HUD can drive apps with no MCP (Clipchamp import; Spotify track change).
- After app switches, the operator must tell it the surface changed; no session restart required.
- HUD response pane is small — long jobs belong in full Desktop.
- Move by grabbing the thin outline; easy to miss.

Treat as operator-reported, not Nous-certified.
