# Lyle's What–Why–How Pyramid Refinement

> You can actually think of it more like a pyramid instead of an equation.
>
> What is on top.
> Why and how are just below it.
> The 3 Ps sits below why, the 3 Ts sit below how.

## Structure

```text
                 WHAT
          ┌────────┴────────┐
         WHY               HOW
   ┌──────┼──────┐    ┌─────┼─────┐
  Push   Pull  Process  Time Talent Treasure
```

## Distilled claim

The model is fundamentally hierarchical rather than algebraic. The ultimate What sits at the apex. Why and How directly support it. Push–Pull–Process decomposes Why; Time–Talent–Treasure decomposes How. The pyramid preserves the What-first planning sequence while showing the foundations required to realize it.
