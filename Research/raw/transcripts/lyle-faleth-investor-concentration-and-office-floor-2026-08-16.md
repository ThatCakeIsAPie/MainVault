# Lyle Telegram — Faleth investor concentration and office pool floor

Shared 2026-08-16 on Telegram as a contemplated Faleth system change.

## Exact source turn

Something im considering changing in the Faleth system is our rules on investors. I already have a rule where investors can't hold more than 10% of the total pool, but I also want to include a rule where the office can have no less than 25% of the total pool, just for durability purposes, really. I don't want investors to have too much soft leverage over us. The idea is that I would rather implement essentially price controls for investors. Because we price on book value rather than market value, something that could be worth much higher is only priced at its book value. That discrepancy is very attractive for investors, because it means the value per dollar of investment is so much greater, but we have stricter rules of how much any one investor can actually own of the pool. As a result of this, from how I have studied the incentives and tried to incentivize, we shouldn't be hurting on capital, but we will be struggling significantly in producing the kinds of returns they expect because we simply don't have enough good people and good businesses fast enough.

## Follow-up turn (same session)

It may be a mechanic that never actually made it into the documents, despite my conversation including it when I was writing the frameworks.
