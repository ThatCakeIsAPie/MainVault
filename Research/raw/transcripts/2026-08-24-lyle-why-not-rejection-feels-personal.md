# Lyle refinement — why unexplained rejection feels personal

Date: 2026-08-24
Context: Further refinement after the CrubTV / Valve rejection letter.

> I think the reason why it hits so hard is simply because most never say "why not", they just say "no", and that is what makes the rejection feel personal.

## Immediate synthesis

An unexplained no creates a causal vacuum. The rejected person has to invent an explanation, and people often fill missing causal information with an identity-level judgment: “There is something wrong with me.”

A truthful “why not” relocates the rejection from the person's worth to the observable present gap between the candidate and the standard. It does not remove disappointment; it makes the decision legible, bounded, and potentially actionable.
