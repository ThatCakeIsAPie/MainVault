## Evidence limits

This is one user's measured-account report, not an official published entitlement or independent benchmark. The result is specific to Grok 4.5 on **Low**, a weekly SuperGrok period converted into four weeks, and the author's inferred Cursor allowance. Token accounting may differ across products because of caching, hidden reasoning, tool calls, context reuse, mode, promotional terms, and provider-side metering.

Most importantly, this compares **raw measured token ceilings**, not accepted work. Cursor bundles repository context, editing, agent orchestration, and code execution; SuperGrok provides a different interaction surface. A token in each product is not necessarily an equal unit of completed work.

## Durable takeaway

Subscription procurement should compare two layers separately:

1. **Capacity:** measured usable tokens, requests, cooldowns, reset period, and concurrency.
2. **Productivity:** accepted work per dollar after tools, context, retries, latency, and human review.

SuperGrok may offer the larger raw Grok 4.5 allowance under the measured conditions, while Cursor may still produce more completed coding work per token because the harness supplies leverage. Buy measured outcomes, not the number printed largest on the buffet sign.
