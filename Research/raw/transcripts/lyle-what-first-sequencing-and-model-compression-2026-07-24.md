# Lyle's What-First Sequencing and Deliberate Compression Refinement

> I say the what first because if you dont first define that, nothing else matters. "Why do I want... what?" Or "how do I get... something?"
>
> If you define the what, then you need to figure out why you want it. If you figure out why, then the how usually shows itself, or at least is addressable.
>
> And exactly, they almost always trade a ratio of the two, not just purely one or the other to get the third, but to cover every minor basis in a simple formula just leads to bloat.

## Distilled claims

1. **What is logically first.** Why and How are incomplete questions until they refer to a defined outcome.
2. **The sequence is generative:** define What, discover Why, then address How. A sufficiently understood Why often reveals or constrains the How.
3. **The resource equations are deliberately compressed.** Real conversions use varying ratios of Time, Talent, and Treasure; the model names the dominant relationship without bloating into false precision.
