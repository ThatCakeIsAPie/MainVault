## Linked resource — Hugging Face model card (fetched 2026-10-26, separate from the post)

Source: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/MotionBricks (README) · Project: https://nvlabs.github.io/motionbricks/ · Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24833 · Code: https://github.com/NVlabs/motionbricks

Key facts from the card:
- Generates real-time motion for humanoid robots and human characters from recent poses + target poses (in-betweening).
- Two weight families (SafeTensors): **SOMA** (human motion on the SOMA skeleton) and **Unitree G1** (humanoid robot). Not interchangeable. Each model uses three networks together.
- Smart Primitives turn target poses into reusable building blocks for navigation and object interaction without retraining; software includes SOMA and G1 demos built with Newton and Warp, plus tools to make your own primitives and scenes.
- The MotionBricks software ships ready-to-use ONNX models; TensorRT demo runtime requires an NVIDIA GPU. Weights are not Transformers `from_pretrained` models; no training pipeline or full training data included.
- Generates motion, not a physical-robot controller (hardware needs a separate tracking controller and safety validation).
- Terms: weights under NVIDIA Open Model Agreement ("permits commercial use and redistribution of modified models, subject to its terms"); code Apache 2.0; bundled BONES motion samples under NVIDIA Sample Data Evaluation License (internal evaluation/testing only).
- HF repo created 2026-10-23.
