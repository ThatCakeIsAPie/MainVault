## Company context

Safe Superintelligence Inc. describes itself as a “straight-shot SSI lab” with one goal and one product: safe superintelligence. It says safety and capabilities are developed together, with safety intended to remain ahead of capability. The company was founded in 2024 by Ilya Sutskever, Daniel Gross, and Daniel Levy. Gross left in June 2025; Sutskever became CEO and Levy president.

Sutskever previously co-founded OpenAI, led its superalignment work, and co-created AlexNet, a foundational breakthrough demonstrating the effectiveness of deep neural networks trained with GPUs.

SSI intentionally avoids ordinary product releases and short-term revenue cycles. Its stated structure is meant to insulate research and safety work from commercial pressure.

## Why the NVIDIA announcement matters

- NVIDIA is not merely supplying chips; it is making a substantial equity investment and giving SSI access to the Vera Rubin platform.
- TechCrunch reported that the investment is in the multiple billions; Bloomberg and Reuters reported approximately $5 billion. SSI itself did not disclose the amount in the post.
- SSI says the partnership will expand its compute by 10× within twelve months.
- NVIDIA said it received rare access to SSI’s closely guarded research and considers that research sufficiently promising to scale.
- The companies will collaborate on current and future compute platforms, giving NVIDIA insight into the requirements of frontier AI research.
- SSI previously raised $1 billion in 2024 at a reported $5 billion valuation and $2 billion in 2025 at a reported $32 billion valuation, despite releasing no public product.

## Evidence boundary

The announcement is a major signal of technical and strategic confidence, not public proof that SSI has solved superintelligence or alignment. Its research remains secret, no model or benchmark has been released, and the investment amount is reported rather than disclosed by SSI.
