# Biography Operating System

**Purpose**: Turn biographies into compressed, reusable strategic intelligence for Faleth Capital.  
**Core Thesis**: Personal experience is too narrow. Hundreds of lives = a council of ghosts you can consult.

## Why This Exists

Your lived experience is one dataset.  
Biographies give you thousands.  
The goal is not admiration or trivia — it is **judgment compression**.

This system turns reading into an asset that compounds for Faleth.

## The Extraction Schema

Every biography is reduced to a standardized, queryable format.

### Core Fields (required)

| Field | Question it answers | Why it matters for Faleth |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Core Drive** | What animated this person? | Identifies the fuel that sustained them through decades |
| **Arena** | Where did they fight? | Maps the domain and constraints they operated in |
| **Constraints** | What limits shaped their choices? | Forces realism about resources, enemies, timing |
| **Repeated Patterns** | What did they do again and again? | Reveals their actual operating system |
| **Failure Modes** | Where did their strengths become liabilities? | Prevents repeating expensive mistakes |
| **Decision Models** | What principles can be stolen? | Turns history into portable tactics |
| **Faleth Relevance** | What directly applies to building Faleth? | Filters noise into actionable insight |
| **Key Scenes & Quotes** | What moments are worth remembering? | Creates emotional anchors and language |

### Optional High-Leverage Fields

- **Mentorship Compression**: What did they learn from predecessors that saved them time?
- **Agency Preservation**: How did they protect (or destroy) the agency of others?
- **Timing Mastery**: When did they move early, late, or perfectly?
- **Betrayal Handling**: How did they respond to disloyalty?
- **Legacy Architecture**: What systems outlived them?

## Usage Modes

| Mode | When to use | Output |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Council Query** | Facing a hard decision | “What would X, Y, and Z have noticed here?” |
| **Pattern Mining** | Looking for recurring traps | List of failure modes across 20 leaders |
| **Fuel Calibration** | Motivation or purpose drift | Which drives actually sustained people for 30+ years |
| **Model Stealing** | Building a new system | Extracted decision frameworks |
| **Range Expansion** | Feeling myopic | Read 3 biographies from wildly different arenas |

## Integration with Faleth

- Every extracted note is tagged `#faleth-relevant`
- Key models are linked into [[Faleth Capital Constitution]], [[The Contribution Framework]], and GovCon automation work
- GBrain can answer questions like:
  - “Which leaders preserved contributor agency best?”
  - “What patterns show up in people who built multi-generational institutions?”
  - “What did empire builders do when capital was scarce?”

## Reading Protocol (recommended)

1. Read the biography normally.
2. Within 48 hours, extract into the schema.
3. Write the **Faleth Relevance** section last — this is where the real work happens.
4. Link the note to at least 3 existing Faleth concepts.
5. Add 1–3 high-signal quotes or scenes.
