# Hundreds for Team and Customers

Lyle's operating phrase:

> Want a team? Interview 100 people. Want a customer base? Tell 100 people about your products. If either isn't enough, do 100 more.

## Core synthesis

Two parallel 100s. Same volume logic. Different conversation type.

- **Team 100:** interviews.
- **Customer 100:** product conversations.
- **Keep rate:** about 10–20 out of each 100.
- **Remedy:** another 100 of the same unit. Do not invent a new personality, a new pitch, or a new identity first.

The unit is 100 of the *right* conversation, not 100 random contacts.

## Expected keep

| Unit | Action | Expected keep |
|---|---|---|
| Team | Interview 100 | Recruit 10–20 |
| Customers | Tell 100 about products | Keep 10–20 recurring customers |

These are planning heuristics for courage and volume, not measured conversion rates and not Amway income claims.

## Duplication sketch (linear group adds)

If you have **10 people on your team personally**:

- Together they do about **100 interviews a month**.
- That adds about **10 people a month**.
- Done for a year, about **100 people on the team**.

Read this as shared monthly interview volume producing linear adds, not as every new person instantly compounding the same 100. Ten personals carrying the 100s is the factory; headcount without interviews is decoration.

## Diagnostic

| Symptom | Inspect first | Likely constraint | Repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| No team | Interview count in 100s | Volume never started | Interview 100 |
| No customer base | Product-conversation count in 100s | Volume never started | Tell 100 about products |
| Did 100, kept fewer than ~10 | Quality, hunger, follow-up (WKRP) | Wrong unit, thin follow-up, or neediness | Another 100, then inspect skill/people/posture |
| 10 personals, not ~100 interviews/month together | Duplicated activity, not roster size | Team exists as names, not as interviewers | Duplicate the 100s, not pep talks |

## Compression boundary

Do **not** collapse this into the Platinum funnel math.

- Marketplace contacts, opens, MG1s, and personal sponsors remain the measured pipe in [[ltd-amway/playbooks/recruiting-and-pipeline-playbook]] and the one-in-twelve Platinum cohort.
- “Interview 100” is higher-intent than “talk to 100 people in the wild.”
- 10–20 keep is rhetoric-grade planning courage, in the same family as [[concepts/offshoots/pipeline-abundance-through-repetition]], not a replacement for channel-specific tracking.
- Year-100 is **10 personals × shared monthly interviews → linear adds**, not exponential compounding of every signup.
- Customers come from product value. Recruiting is not the product. IDS if earnings appear.
