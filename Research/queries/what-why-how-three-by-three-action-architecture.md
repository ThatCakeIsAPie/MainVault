# What–Why–How: A 3×3 Architecture for Directed Action

## Core synthesis

A desired outcome is produced by joining a sufficiently strong reason to act with a workable means of acting:

**What = Why × How**

- **What** names the chosen result: the state to create, preserve, become, or obtain.
- **Why** explains the motivational energy that makes sustained action possible.
- **How** identifies the resources available for constructing and executing a path.

The architecture contains two linked triads:

| Layer | Triad | Governing question |
|---|---|---|
| Why | Push · Pull · Process | Why will I start, aim, and persist? |
| How | Time · Talent · Treasure | What can I invest or convert to make the result possible? |

The **What** is not a third triad. It is the selected destination against which both triads are organized.

## Why: Push, Pull, Process

- **Push:** What am I dead set on preventing, escaping, or never experiencing again?
- **Pull:** What do I positively want to create, obtain, serve, or become?
- **Process:** What activity is rewarding or motivating in the doing, before the result arrives?

Push supplies urgency. Pull supplies direction. Process supplies endurance.

## How: Time, Talent, Treasure

- **Time + Talent → Treasure:** apply hours and capability to valuable problems.
- **Time + Treasure → Talent:** invest practice, instruction, tools, and access into capability.
- **Talent + Treasure → recovered Time:** use judgment, delegation, automation, and investments to reduce dependence on one's direct hours.

The conversion rates are not equal or guaranteed. Time can be wasted, talent can be mispriced, and treasure can be misallocated. Judgment, trust, market demand, health, relationships, circumstance, and other people's agency affect every conversion.

## The action loop

1. **Choose the What.** Define the actual outcome rather than a vague desire.
2. **Strengthen the Why.** Identify the Push, Pull, and Process that can sustain action.
3. **Inventory the How.** Determine the present amounts of Time, Talent, and Treasure.
4. **Find the constraint.** Identify which resource or motivational component is weakest.
5. **Convert deliberately.** Trade the resources currently available for the one presently scarce.
6. **Execute and inspect.** Test whether the conversion produces movement toward the What.
7. **Compound agency.** Reinvest gains so less direct Time is required and greater capacity becomes available for purpose.

## Important boundary

This is best understood as an architecture of **controllable action**, not a promise that desire plus effort guarantees every outcome. Some Whats depend on chance, physical reality, institutions, timing, markets, or the voluntary choices of other people.

The model's defensible claim is:

> Mastering your motives and stewarding your convertible resources sharply increases your capacity to pursue chosen outcomes without surrendering agency.

## Current-season example

- **What:** contract cash lands and the VXE fulfillment wave succeeds without collapsing family priorities or long-term agency.
- **Why:** avoid returning to low-agency dependence (Push); build Faleth's family-office mission and enable others' dreams (Pull); enjoy operating, solving, systematizing, and compressing workflows (Process).
- **How:** invest present Time and operating Talent into VXE delivery to generate Treasure; later place Talent and Treasure into people and systems that recover Time.
