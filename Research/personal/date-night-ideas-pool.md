# Date Night Ideas Pool

A reusable pool for Delta to draw from when Lyle asks for a date-night idea. Source thread: [[raw/transcripts/lyle-x-share-2083652162450538507]].

## How to choose

When suggesting an idea, match it to the actual constraint instead of blindly selecting “dinner, but elsewhere”—humanity has suffered enough.

Consider:

- **Energy:** drained, normal, adventurous
- **Location:** at home, nearby, destination
- **Budget:** free, inexpensive, flexible
- **Time:** 30 minutes, 1–2 hours, half-day
- **Need:** laughter, novelty, connection, creativity, nostalgia, or decompression

## Quick-match menu

### Drained / almost no planning

- Sunset walk with coffee
- Quick dessert-only market run
- Sit in the car and build a shared playlist
- Walk without phones
- Spend ten extra minutes talking in the car before going inside
- Sit somewhere pleasant and ban discussion of chores, bills, and logistics

### Playful / laugh together

- Mini-golf
- Bowling
- Arcade night
- Bar trivia
- Stand-up comedy
- Card game where the loser plans the next date
- Paint terrible portraits of each other
- Blind snack taste test

### Adventurous / break routine

- Ice skating
- Indoor rock climbing
- Escape room
- Axe throwing
- Dance class
- Try a sport neither has played

### At home / no babysitter / no screens

- Paint terrible portraits
- Blind snack taste test
- Invent and name cocktails or mocktails
- Home spa night
- Build an overcomplicated kit together
- Cook a dish from a country both want to visit
- Write letters to open in ten years

### Create something lasting

- Print photos and make an album
- Pottery or ceramics class
- Cook a destination-inspired meal
- Write ten-year letters
- Start a scrapbook of the last five years
- Build a shared bucket list

### Reconnect / have a real conversation

- Recreate the first date
- Ask ten questions never asked before
- Build a shared bucket list
- Plan an elaborate imaginary dream vacation
- Share something each person misses from the early relationship
- Each play one song from the year the couple met
- Share favorite memories from the past year

### Cheap but memorable

- Sunset walk with homemade coffee
- Dream-house drive through expensive neighborhoods
- Bookstore date: choose a book for each other
- Dessert-only market run
- Shared playlist in the car
- Phone-free walk
- Dream-vacation planning at home
