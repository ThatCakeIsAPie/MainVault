# 2026-07-09 — Faleth Document Review and Sanctioning Architecture

## Context

Full review of the nine-document suite ([[Faleth Capital Constitution]], the seven frameworks, and [[Beyond the Wage]]). The session resolved several v1 critiques via clarification, produced one major design addition (a graduated-sanctions and involuntary-removal architecture), rejected one proposed mechanism (the QC bounty), and consolidated a patch-ready ledger for the v2 cut. Two decisions remain open with Lyle.

## Canonical Clarifications (resolved this session)

**One-Third mapping.** The pool's 50/50 liquid-to-deployed ratio *is* the business third and the liquid third held in equal measure; Bucket One is the individual's land third, completing the Talmudic structure outside the pool. The 50/50 rule is therefore a derived equality, not an arbitrary parameter. No structural change needed — keep the derivation visible in Constitution 1.3.

**Mint-and-burn accounting.** Intended mechanics confirmed: quarterly profit share sits as a distributions-payable (liability). Cash-out extinguishes it (assets down, liabilities down, equity unchanged). Minting converts the payable into contributed equity (liabilities down, equity up, shares up proportionally at pre-mint book value). Book value per share is exactly invariant under both branches — the invariance claim in [[The Equity Framework]] Section 6 is sound; only the language needs fixing (see ledger rows 7–8).

**Floor mechanics.** The Minimum Pay Guarantee is a recoverable draw against commission — a well-established, generally lawful structure, not a prohibited clawback. Recovery reaches only down to the floor, never below it, keeping weekly take-home at or above the floor. Residuals: hours are still recorded for non-exempt W-2 participants (FLSA recordkeeping and overtime computation — the no-time-clock *culture* survives; the records requirement does not go away); the floor should index to local minimum wage × realistic hours, not cost of living alone; and the 100%-recovery band above the floor is a dead zone in the action-income feedback loop — a partial recovery rate (e.g. 50%) is a legitimate recalibration parameter, not a flaw fix.

**Soft advance = non-pursuit with persistence.** Balances are never pursued after departure; they persist and resume if the person returns. Superior to forgiveness: the only exploit is one-shot and bounded by the four-week cap (a known underwriting cost), and no formal discharge event ever occurs (no forgiven-debt income oddity for the departed). Language to add: the advance is *recoverable solely against future contribution earnings and is never a personal debt* — this makes non-pursuit structural rather than discretionary.

**Class A = stewardship, not economics.** Church-elder model confirmed: pure governance, no financial stake; the book economically belongs to Class B collectively. Legal term of art to hand attorneys: **steward-ownership** (non-economic voting class + economic non-voting class). "Sold upon departure" is stale v1 language — replace with the elder-emeritus structure already implicit in Constitution 3.2 / 3.3 / 4.1: recognition is permanent, *active* status carries the vote and duties, seats lapse (not sold) on stepping back, reactivation mirrors the returning-participant rule, and the Peaceful Fork remains the economic exit. "Active" needs one defining sentence.

**Class C** is the stale v1 term for what became MCAs. Last hideout: the Layer 3b paragraph of [[The Contribution Framework]].

## New Design Decisions

**Keep Governance 6.2 — relocate the sanctioning function.** The prohibition on Leads unilaterally removing participants is anti-boss and anti-coup: whoever can unilaterally remove you from the pool is your boss, and an elected Lead who can prune the electorate can survive any confidence vote. First live instance of the fence-rediscovery pattern — the fence was load-bearing. Do not delete; relocate.

**Involuntary removal: transpose Constitution 3.2 downward.** The suite's only expulsion procedure existed at the top (Class A revocation); the shop floor had none. New architecture: the Lead documents and initiates (prosecutor and friction-remover — honoring the pruning instinct); the *body* decides — team supermajority at scale, Lead-plus-owner concurrence at founding scale. Earned stakes (Class B shares, earned pay) travel with the removed regardless; inviolable. Principle grounding: no-trapping cuts both ways — a team that cannot stop associating with an adversarial member is trapped by him. Removal is the fork's recognition of failed voluntary alignment initiated from the other side, so due process supplies what voluntariness otherwise would. Pattern match: Matthew 18 — private word, then witnesses, then the assembly; decided by the body, never by one man.

**Graduated sanctions ladder** (installs Ostrom's one missing principle): conversation → documented warning → reopened-unit economics → temporary suspension by team vote → removal procedure. Middle rungs are peer-administered, sized to the violation, and documented so escalation is legible.

**Warranty comebacks: completion frame, not punishment.** A unit's value is provisional until it survives the warranty window; a comeback means the unit was never finished. The comeback *reopens the unit*: everyone paid on it participates in making it right in proportion — the tech redoes the repair, QC re-inspects free. A single comeback is economics with no moral freight; a comeback *rate* is a conversation, then the ladder. If the earner has left, the non-pursuit grace applies (same DNA as the floor: pay provisionally, true up against realized value). Distinction to write down: honest defect (pure economics) vs. **falsified completion** (a trust breach against the log the entire pay system rests on — escalates up the ladder fast).

**QC bounty rejected.** Paying the catcher the failer's points pays QC to *want* defects — income maximized when the shop is at its worst — and turns every marginal call into a two-party pay dispute landing on the Lead. Deeper problem is empirical: punitive per-defect docking drives concealment (Deming; aviation and medicine moved to blameless reporting because punishment suppresses the signal that kills). Replacement: symmetric downside via the reopened unit — nobody profits from defects existing; the collective layer already prices warranty cost into net profit and Layer 3a.

## Open Decisions (Lyle)

2. **Ratify warranty mechanics.** Completion frame proposed; not yet settled.
3. **Floor opt-out ↔ classification.** Minimum wage is non-waivable for employees; opt-out can only exist for non-employees → ABC-test prong B risk for anyone doing the core work. This is the framed question for the employment attorney (engage alongside securities counsel).
4. Pre-existing: Class A share allocation method upon admission (Constitution 3.1 note).

## Standing Insights

- The suite is **deduced, not accreted** — derived from four axioms: pay for value not time; exit as the accountability mechanism; book value as the standard; equip to independence. Consequence: coherence is near-guaranteed, and risk relocates from the joints to the axioms. The remaining risk is empirical — do people self-govern shared pools honestly; does transparency plus exit replace supervision; does a modest floor actually switch off survival mode — and only FRR can test it. Proof-over-argument is the last validation instrument available.
- Model consensus is correlated evidence: same corpora, same agreeableness gradient, and coherence is the one property fully verifiable from text alone. Some of the detected coherence was installed with model help. Five models ≈ five colleagues from one graduate program. The uncorrelated evaluators are adversarial humans, starting with the first technicians.
- One-delta designs (NM = marketing delta; co-ops = ownership delta) suffer organ-transplant rejection from the retained chassis. Ground-up design escapes the rejection but pays in live fence rediscovery — accreted systems encode fixes to problems nobody remembers. **Quarterly recalibration is the fence-discovery engine**; treat it as the most precious mechanism in year one. Governance 6.2 was rediscovery #1, done cheaply in conversation.
- The shared pool is simultaneously the improvement on NM and the removal of NM's immune system (isolation — you can't fire a downline, but the network routes around damage). In a pool, toxicity is contagious instead of containable — which is why removal must exist *somewhere*, and never in one hand.
- Ostrom, *Governing the Commons* → reading list. The framework independently reinvents most of her documented design principles for durable self-governing commons; graduated sanctions was the one gap, now closing.
- Recruiting: warm market first, then the content/podcast funnel. The open documents are an *owned* pre-contact filter — the dojo intake unavailable inside the upline funnel already exists here. Caveat: the comp model attracts the overlap of believers ∩ builders, smaller than either circle; matters once strangers arrive via content.
