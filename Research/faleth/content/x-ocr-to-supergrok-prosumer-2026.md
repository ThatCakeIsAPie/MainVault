# X Original Content Rewards to SuperGrok Prosumer (2026)

## Thesis

> **X can fund SuperGrok only after it pays you for original Home Timeline posts that Premium users actually stop on. Replies build the room. They do not pay the bill.**

Lyle's ask (2026-08-14): treat SuperGrok Heavy (~$300/mo) as prosumer inventory. Use X payouts to make the subscription free, then grow it toward ~$500/mo. Use the For You action-weight model. Grow skill by producing what he consumes.

That is a valid **asset** goal. It is not this season's cash plan. VXE showing-up stays the apex.

## Baseline (do not skip)

@LyleBCole as of 2026-08-14: **22 followers**, 57 following, 365 posts, **not Premium/verified**. Bio already verb-shaped: helping hungry people build businesses that return control of finances, time, mind, and agency.

From 22 followers, the first paid gate is **X Premium**, not Heavy. The first payout gate is **Original Content Rewards**, not the old ads split.

## Question the $300 requirement

Official grok.com ladder on 2026-08-14 showed **SuperGrok $30** and **SuperGrok Plus $100**. Heavy still exists in the compare table and is widely listed at **$300/mo**. Third-party $99 Heavy promos are not an official x.ai price.

Prosumer does not require the top SKU on day one. Buy the cheapest Grok tier that actually grows the skill you are publishing. Upgrade when a real job (multi-agent, rate limits, Grok Bot weekly allowance) is blocking output. Do not buy Heavy to feel like Jon's screenshot.

## Platform rules that change the play

As of 2026-08-14, X help:

- **Creator Revenue Sharing is closed to new enrollments** (2026-08-07) and retires **2026-09-07**.
- The live path is **Original Content Rewards**.
- Eligibility: 18+, US (available), account in good standing, Premium / Premium+ / Premium Business, **500 verified followers**, **500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days**, and **replies are excluded** from that impressions test.
- Payouts: every two weeks, **$30 minimum**, based on **qualified impressions** = unique Premium-user Home Timeline views where at least 50% of the post is visible.
- Ineligible: copied/minimally modified content, exclusive monetization-coaching, **automated posting**, engagement solicitation ("like/follow/repost this"), Community-Noted posts.
- Admission is not automatic after the numbers. Application + review.

Rate per qualified impression is **unpublished**. The July 2026 ~$3.5k / 12M sample is one creator's old ads-share screenshot, not an OCR forecast.

## Prosumer, applied here

LTD language: stop being only a consumer; be a **producing consumer**. Buy from a system that can pay you, and produce what you consume.

Applied to Grok + X:

1. Consume algorithm notes, agent-ops threads, and your own vault work.
2. Produce **original** posts that teach the same thing in your voice.
3. Let X pay for the originals if/when OCR accepts you.
4. Reinvest payout into the Grok tier you actually use.

This grows **Talent**. It is not VXE cash timing. It does not replace showing up for contracts.

## Two ledgers (do not mash them)

| Ledger | Job | Counts for OCR money? | For You job |
|--------|-----|----------------------|-------------|
| **Replies** | Find the room; prove judgment; earn follows | **No** (eligibility excludes reply impressions) | High if the reply is useful; stranger replies still will not ride into a random For You |
| **Originals** | Qualified Home Timeline impressions | **Yes** | Copy-link +20, mutual reply on an original +20, quote/DM +5, like +0.5. Same-author tax if you dump |

Write originals people **export**. Farm likes and you are decorating.

## Operating cadence (30 minutes, VXE first)

Hard cap: **one original + 8 to 12 replies** on a workday. Zero on a VXE hell day. The 2026 Ideas note's "3–10 posts/day" is too many for a 22-follower account under the same-author haircut.

### Daily

1. **15 min replies** in 4 rooms only: Grok/agent operators, trades/ops owners, faith-and-work builders, GovCon/process operators. Make the thread more useful. No applause. No "great post."
2. **10 min one original** from today's real work: a discovery, a decision, an artifact, or a past-you vs now. Source the vault, not the timeline.
3. **5 min** log: which reply earned a profile visit or a real conversation. That is market research.

### Weekly

- **1 thread or X Article** that teaches one system you actually ran (Hermes/vault, named-role agents, process vs pain, sanitized GovCon workflow).
- **1 artifact post** with a real screenshot or numbered list from work. Jon's Mary/Atlas pattern is the form, not the costume.
- **0 posts about how to get monetized.** That category is ineligible on purpose.

### Spacing

- One original per reader-day. If you post a thread, that is the day's original.
- Recycle by writing the next true thing. Posts die at **48 hours**.
- Never ask for likes, follows, or reposts.

## Content lanes (what to publish)

Publish principles and sanitized workflows. Keep VXE/LibreTech client names, dollar specifics, and proposal text private.

Default mix:

- 50% natural extensions of what @LyleBCole already says (usefulness over hustle slogans, process, faith-and-agency, telling people what they need to hear).
- 30% new territory you already have in the vault (named-role agent teams, owner-manages-the-manager, Hermes as OS, public-learning flywheel itself).
- 20% same-day reflections from work.

First sentence must earn a stop. Specific number, specific failure, specific tool. No "here are 3 lessons."

## Scoreboard (in order)

1. **VXE cash timing** untouched. If X ate a fulfillment day, the tactic failed.
2. **Premium on.** You cannot enter OCR unverified.
3. **500 verified followers.** From 22, this is the real Phase 1 KPI. Count verified, not total.
4. **500k verified Home Timeline impressions / 90 days on originals.** Analytics, not vibes.
5. **OCR application accepted.** Then Stripe / identity if not already done.
6. **Two consecutive payouts ≥ the Grok tier you actually use.** Only then is the subscription "free."
7. **$500/mo** is a later asset target, not a 90-day promise.

## Phase math (honest)

- **Days 1–7:** Buy Premium if missing. Tighten bio/header. Turn on 2FA. Screenshot follower count and any analytics you have. Do not buy Heavy.
- **Days 8–45:** Room-building. Target 100+ verified followers from people in the four rooms. Still one original most days.
- **Days 46–120:** Originals-first. Aim at 500 verified followers + a visible climb in verified HT impressions.
- **After both OCR gates:** apply. Keep publishing. Do not change the voice to chase payout.

$500/mo from X is possible for accounts that already hold Premium attention. It is not a schedule you can put on a calendar from 22 followers. Treat it as **optional dividend** on a flywheel you would run anyway.

## What not to do

- 100-reply cargo cult. One creator's 10k-follower story is not a law.
- Auto-posters, reply bots, engagement pods. OCR bans the category.
- Posting about payout hacking.
- Making "X creator" the identity. The identity is already in the bio.
- Un-holding the automation-services bridge because a plumber's Grok Bot dashboard looked like a company.
