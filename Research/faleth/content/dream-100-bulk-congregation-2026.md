# Dream 100 Bulk Congregation (2026)

## Thesis

> **Do not build the room from zero if a room already exists. Name the people who already hold your avatar. Become someone they would platform, not a smaller copy of them.**

Lyle (2026-08-14) stacked three teachers on purpose:

- **Darren Hardy** (*The Entrepreneur Roller Coaster*): sell in bulk. One congregation beats a hundred one-off pitches.
- **Russell Brunson** (*Traffic Secrets* / *DotCom Secrets*): go where the avatar already gathers. The **Dream 100** is the list of people who already have that room. Get on their radar. Direct a portion of their traffic to you.
- The Hormozi test, in Lyle's words: if the topic is business, the goal is not to *be* Alex Hormozi. It is to be someone Hormozi would **want to talk about**.

That is complementary distribution. It is not genre cloning.

## First-principles split

| Move | Job | Failure |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Congregation** | Speak where the avatar already pays attention | Cold-posting into the void and calling it "brand" |
| **Dream 100** | A living list of people who hold that congregation | A vanity list of celebrities you will never reach this quarter |
| **Collaborator** | Add a missing piece they do not have | Become their intern or their cover band |
| **Bulk** | One quote, guest, or share moves many avatars at once | 100 DMs that all say "love to collab" |

Hardy bulk and Brunson Dream 100 are the same machine. The list is who owns the bulk.

## What Brunson actually requires

The Dream 100 is a **working file**, not a mood.

1. Write the avatar in one sentence (who, pain, outcome).
2. List up to 100 people and properties that already serve that avatar. Podcasts, X accounts, newsletters, churches, operator Discords, YouTube shows.
3. Rank by *overlap with your avatar*, not follower count. A 4k trades owner can be a better Dream 100 than a 2M generalist.
4. Work the list with value they can export. On X that is a quoteable original plus a useful reply. Offline that is a guest, a JV, a stage, a warm intro.
5. Track who actually moved traffic. Promote those names. Retire the ones who never will.

**100 is the catalog.** The season's **working set is 8.** From 22 followers, 100 outreach attempts is theater. Eight named operators, worked weekly, is a system.

## Hormozi test

Ask of every draft: would this person **quote me**, or would they scroll past a worse version of themselves?

If the answer is "I sound like them," delete it. If the answer is "I finished a sentence they started, from a life they do not live," keep it.

Lyle's missing pieces they do not have: Faleth process, owner-manages-the-manager, sanitized GovCon, faith-and-agency, Hermes as an operating system. Those are collaboration bait. "3 lessons on mindset" is not.
