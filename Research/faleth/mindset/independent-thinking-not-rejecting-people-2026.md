# Independent thinking is respecting purpose, not rejecting people

LTD slide drop (2026-08-13). Room photos. Romans 12:2 as the banner.

## What the room was selling

Three beats:

1. **Mind as asset.** Most people stay inside the close circle, fear judgment, and overestimate risk. Family often projects their fear as love.
2. **POOR = Passing Over Opportunities Repeatedly.** The safety mindset that keeps you employed can also keep you stuck.
3. **Cashflow quadrant.** E and S trade time. B and I own systems and assets. The closer: keep the job if you must, but do not let the job be the only future.

## What is actually durable for Lyle

The usable core is not the stat sheet. Those numbers are persuasion furniture. Verify before quoting as fact.

The durable frame:

- Independent thinking is **renewing the mind**, not scorning the people who love you.
- Discouragement from family is often **their fear wearing your name**. Diagnose it. Do not marry it.
- The sin named POOR is delay dressed as prudence.
- The quadrant question is **Who owns the residual?** Time sold vs a system that works when you are not in the chair.
- Slide 3's actual question is the honest one: not "quit your job tonight," but "is anything compounding outside it?"

## Map to the pyramid

- **What:** agency that outlives a paycheck. God → wife → kids → business still sits above the quadrant.
- **Why / Pull:** purpose and people, not approval. "What will people think?" is a Why leak.
- **Why / Push:** a future determined only by policy, hardship withdrawals, and someone else's schedule.
- **How:** systems and duplication (B), then assets (I). VXE cash timing is How for this season, not identity as Employee.

## Anti-capture

Do not let this slide deck turn into contempt for work, family, or a W-2 that is currently feeding the house. LTD equips character. It does not replace showing up for VXE.

Think. Pray. Decide. Act. Then measure fruit, not applause.
