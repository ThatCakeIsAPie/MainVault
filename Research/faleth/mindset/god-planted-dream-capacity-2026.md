# God Would Not Plant an Unreachable Dream

## Claim

**God would not plant a dream in a person incapable of reaching it.** Capacity is implied by the calling; the work is to align effort, character, and obedience with the planted vision—not to negotiate the dream down to current comfort.

## Distillation

| Layer | Implication |
|-------|-------------|
| Identity | The dream is evidence of capacity under formation, not proof of immediate readiness |
| Discipline | Effort and standards are how capacity catches the vision |
| Faith | Obedience walks before full proof; capacity grows in the walk |

## Faleth / Lyle (2026)

- Anchors **faith-anchored action** during post-layoff cash-timing: GovCon + VXE pipeline as the “planted” operational dream, not side-hustle identity theater.
- Does **not** license delusion: “capable of reaching it” includes mentorship, systems, and sweat—not vibes alone.
