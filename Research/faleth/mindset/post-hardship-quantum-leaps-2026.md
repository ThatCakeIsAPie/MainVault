# Post-Hardship Quantum Leaps (2026)

## Claim

After prolonged hardship and uncertainty, multi-domain “quantum leaps” often follow — **if** you do not abandon yourself mid-tunnel.

## Distillation

1. **Hardship is not the end state** if identity and process survive it.
2. **Leaps cluster** across life areas once the bottleneck (cash, status, health, belief) shifts — not always linear progress each week.
3. **Only requirement stated:** do not give up on yourself (self-trust + continued action).
