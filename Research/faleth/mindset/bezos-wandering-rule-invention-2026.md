# Bezos Wandering Rule — Invention vs Efficiency

Jeff Bezos frames **efficiency** and **real invention** as opposing modes. When the path is known, move in a straight line. When the problem is still being discovered, you need **permission to wander** — lateral thinking, not incremental polish.

## Core quotes (clip via @aerockrose, ~Jun 26 2026)

- "When I sit down to work on a problem, I know I don't know where I'm going."
- "Efficiency and invention are sort of at odds."
- "Real invention, not incremental improvement… requires wandering."
- "You have to give yourself permission to wander."
- Many people treat wandering as inefficient; teams often kill invention by demanding a straight line too early.

## Operating rule

| Mode | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Efficiency** | Playbook exists — execution, predictable meetings, known KPIs |
| **Wandering** | Problem discovery — invention, lateral moves, unclear ETA |

## Faleth / Lyle application (2026)

- **Bridge income** (automation services): efficiency — scoped deliverables, proof, billing.
- **GovCon pipeline + Faleth shape**: mix — qualify/score efficiently, but **invention** on architecture, agent governance, and agency-respecting operating models needs wander-time without fake roadmaps.
- Aligns with **process** motivation (systems bedrock) without forcing charisma-theater or premature certainty.
