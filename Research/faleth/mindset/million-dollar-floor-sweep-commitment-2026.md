# Million-Dollar Floor Sweep Commitment

## Claim

Terry Crews describes beating depression by **imagining he was paid a million dollars to sweep a warehouse floor**—then sweeping as if that were true. Full presence and excellence applied to the task in front of you rewires energy and breaks collapse loops.

## Core mechanism

1. **Reframe the task’s dignity** — act as if the work is high-stakes paid excellence.
2. **Games for the mind** — deliberate mental frames when motivation is gone.
3. **Process over mood** — standards first; feeling follows motion ([[faleth/mindset/volume-based-standards-with-detached-outcomes-2026]]).

## Faleth / Lyle (2026)

- Use on **hell-month fulfillment**, boring proposal ops, and unemployment admin: do the unglamorous work as if it were a million-dollar contract.
- Complements “do it for fun” ([[faleth/mindset/do-it-for-fun-anxiety-reframe-2026]]): fun lowers anxiety; **floor-sweep** raises standards when energy is flat.
- Not hustle porn: temporary frame to restore agency, not permanent self-exploitation.
