# Heart, Intuition, Guts Over Analysis

## Claim

Jeff Bezos: **best decisions in life and business came from heart, intuition, and guts—not analysis.** Spreadsheets and models inform; they do not replace judgment under uncertainty.

## Distillation

| Mode | Role |
|------|------|
| Analysis | Clear known variables, kill dumb options, post-mortems |
| Intuition / guts | Commit when data is incomplete and the path is still inventing itself |
| Heart | Values-aligned bets that survive long grind |

## Link to Wandering Rule

When the problem is not fully known, **straight-line efficiency fails** ([[faleth/mindset/bezos-wandering-rule-invention-2026]]). Intuition is how you navigate wander-time without fake precision.

## Faleth / Lyle (2026)

- GovCon + Faleth architecture need both: **score deals** with analysis; **choose what to build and who to partner with** with guts and values.
- Guardrail: intuition after reps and mentorship, not vibes without fruit inspection ([[concepts/offshoots/character-assessment-through-fruit-inspection]]).
