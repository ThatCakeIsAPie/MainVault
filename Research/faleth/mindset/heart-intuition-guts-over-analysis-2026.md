# Heart, Intuition, Guts Over Analysis

## Claim

Jeff Bezos: **best decisions in life and business came from heart, intuition, and guts—not analysis.** Spreadsheets and models inform; they do not replace judgment under uncertainty.

## Distillation

| Mode | Role |
|------|------|
| Analysis | Clear known variables, kill dumb options, post-mortems |
| Intuition / guts | Commit when data is incomplete and the path is still inventing itself |
| Heart | Values-aligned bets that survive long grind |

## Link to Wandering Rule

When the problem is not fully known, **straight-line efficiency fails** ([[faleth/mindset/bezos-wandering-rule-invention-2026]]). Intuition is how you navigate wander-time without fake precision.

## Faleth / Lyle (2026)

- GovCon + Faleth architecture need both: **score deals** with analysis; **choose what to build and who to partner with** with guts and values.
- Guardrail: intuition after reps and mentorship, not vibes without fruit inspection ([[concepts/offshoots/character-assessment-through-fruit-inspection]]).

## Jensen Huang: intelligence after technical skill becomes abundant

In a 2026 interview clip, Jensen Huang argues that technical problem-solving is becoming more commodity-like as AI absorbs programming and similar work. His future definition of smart sits at the intersection of technical competence, human empathy, and the ability to infer what is unspoken or see around corners. He describes the resulting “vibe” as compressed data analysis, first principles, life experience, wisdom, and sensitivity to other people—not mystical instinct detached from evidence. [[raw/x-bookmarks/26-08-09/2086434570031993112]]

That sharpens the guardrail on intuition: high-value judgment is **analysis compressed through experience and empathy**, used to pre-empt problems before they become legible in a spreadsheet. As routine technical production gets cheaper, partner selection, customer understanding, leadership, and anticipatory judgment become more—not less—important for [[faleth-capital]].
