# Don't Be a Career (Steve Jobs)

## Claim

**Don’t be a career.** Identity and life trajectory should not collapse into a job title, ladder, or institutional path. Build work that serves a larger mission; refuse to *become* the role.

## Distillation

| Trap | Alternative |
|------|-------------|
| Career as self | Mission / craft / contribution as self |
| Ladder optimization | Asset and system building ([[concepts/offshoots/asset-creation-over-linear-income]]) |
| Resume theater | Observable fruit and shipped work |

## Faleth / Lyle (2026)

- Post-layoff: unemployment and bridge income are **instruments**, not identity. Primary play remains VXE/GovCon cash timing + Faleth architecture.
- Reinforces [[faleth/mindset/make-more-than-you-take-2026]] and anti-zero-leverage sequencing ([[concepts/foundational/five-step-sequencing-model]]).
- Guardrail: “don’t be a career” is not anti-work; it is anti-**identity capture** by W-2 or status games.
