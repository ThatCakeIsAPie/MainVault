# Self-Writing Vault as an Operating Loop (2026)

## Principle

A durable knowledge system should reduce the willpower required to capture, connect, retrieve, and revisit thinking. The bookmarked post points to a referenced X article describing a practical loop: one low-friction inlet, immutable raw material, automated filing and backlinks, context loaded into future agent sessions, and periodic synthesis.

The durable idea is not “let an agent reorganize everything.” It is **separation of planes**:

1. **Capture plane:** voice or text lands in one predictable inlet.
2. **Evidence plane:** raw material remains immutable and inspectable.
3. **Processing plane:** agents classify, link, and distill without rewriting history.
4. **Recall plane:** relevant context is loaded into work rather than left in storage.
5. **Review plane:** recurring synthesis exposes themes, contradictions, and unfinished commitments.

## Useful metrics

- Raw items successfully processed without manual babysitting.
- Link density and useful retrieval—not note count vanity.
- Time from capture to searchable distillation.
- Number of stale or orphaned notes.
- Whether weekly synthesis changes a decision or action.

## Lyle / Delta implication

This validates the current filesystem-first architecture: Obsidian is the inspectable source of truth; raw captures remain raw; GBrain provides retrieval and graph structure; recurring jobs perform ingestion and synthesis. The improvement target is not another app. It is fewer failed handoffs and less babysitting—because apparently collecting 2,400 beautiful files and never reopening them is not, in fact, a second brain.

## Agent histories as behavioral evidence

Coding-agent sessions can be a high-signal record of actual intent, constraints, corrections, abandoned approaches, and decisions made under work pressure. They often reveal more operational truth than retrospective summaries because the trace is produced while trying to accomplish something.[2]

That does **not** make agent logs an unfiltered psychological ground truth. People still perform for themselves, prompts omit offline context, repository work overrepresents technical concerns, and logs can contain secrets or third-party material. Treat histories as evidence to distill—with consent, privacy boundaries, provenance, and explicit uncertainty—not as permission for an agent to diagnose its operator from a terminal transcript.

## Guardrails

- Never let an automated distillation overwrite the raw source.
- Require provenance and deterministic ledgers for recurring ingestion.
- Measure retrieval usefulness, not graph aesthetics alone.
- Keep automation quiet when nothing changed and explicit when processing failed.
