# Lyle refinement — identity not on externals (2026-06-28)

**Context:** Follow-up to [[research/faleth/mindset/mindset-as-infrastructure-mental-diet-2026]] / GRITCULT X share thread. Delta had emphasized not tying identity to this week's scoreboard.

## Lyle (verbatim sense)

Don't tie identity to anything external. In a weird way it requires a **healthy degree of delusion**: you need to know you are going to win — and even if you didn't win *this time*, you will win.

As a Christian, this is what we mean by identity rooted in **Christ**, not of this world. All we need is God's love and faith in His plan for us and our lives; we just have to be **obedient**.

**Operate rule:** Absolutely strive — but don't let identity be taken away from Christ. If that external thing becomes your new god and not God, you are in trouble.

**Resonant shorthand (same thread):** *Work like it all depends on you. Pray like it all depends on God.*

## Mechanism (distilled)

| Failure mode | Symptom | Root |
|--------------|---------|------|
| Scoreboard identity | Weekly metrics define self-worth | External thing worshipped as validator |
| Founder self-pity | "I am failing" after variance | Same — outcome became lord |
| Healthy conviction | Strive hard after a loss | Identity anchored upstream (Christ / calling) |
