### 8. Thread — The GovCon capture OS wedge (outline)
Status: draft
Category: new territory
Source: GovCon Proposal Automation Operating System.md full spec

```text
1/ Building an internal proposal automation OS for small government contractors.

Not as a SaaS play first. As the actual workflow we use on VXE/LibreTech/Faleth work.

2/ The repetitive front half kills small teams:
- SAM.gov monitoring + fit scoring
- RFP/PDF intake and requirement extraction
- Compliance matrix generation
- Vendor outreach drafting
- First-draft response + human review queue

3/ The moat isn’t generic AI writing.
It’s company-specific past performance memory, vendor network, compliance discipline, pricing judgment, and—most importantly—an evidence locker that survives bid protests and “shadow AI” evaluation questions.

4/ MVP gates we’re enforcing:
- Named human reviewer on every material step
- Model routing logged (cheap for triage, premium for narrative)
- Source URL + retrieval timestamp + assumption trail on every output
- Autonomous submission disabled until the manual process is mapped and proven
- CUI/government data handling tracked at every layer

5/ Why now: the market is validating the wedge (Polsia TenderField/ContractPulse signals, SAM.gov Smart Contracting, etc.). But most tools are still selling “it just works.” We’re selling “here’s the audit trail when it doesn’t.”

6/ Next step: map the current manual solicitation workflow end-to-end, identify the first automatable bottleneck, and instrument the evidence locker before we touch drafting.

Real work > demo theater.
```

### 9. What I learned today
Status: draft
Category: natural extension
Source: 2026-06-24 daily debrief synthesis + GovCon OS iteration

```text
What I learned today:

Agentic automation only becomes an operating system when you design the governance layer first—permissions, observability, named human handoff, budget logs, and refusal-to-proceed defaults.

Everything else is just a more expensive way to make the same mistakes faster.

The boring parts (evidence lockers, model routing policies, CUI flags) are the actual product.
```
