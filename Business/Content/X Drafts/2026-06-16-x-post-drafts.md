### 8. Thread — GovCon Proposal Automation OS (current version)
Status: draft
Category: new territory
Source: Ideas/GovCon Proposal Automation + debrief

```text
1/ Building the GovCon Proposal Automation OS.

The problem: Small contractors can't afford to submit 3x more proposals without exploding AI spend or quality.

The realization: Route cheap models to the boring 80% (triage, outlines, compliance matrices, past-performance mapping). Reserve frontier models for final narrative polish only.

2/ Current flow:
- Opportunity Radar → fit score
- Bid/no-bid triage (cheap model)
- Outline + compliance matrix (cheap)
- Evidence locker with source citations
- Human review gate
- Frontier polish only on the final 20%

3/ Result so far: Higher throughput, lower hallucination risk in compliance sections, and the expensive model only touches what actually needs it.

This is infrastructure thinking, not demo thinking.
```
