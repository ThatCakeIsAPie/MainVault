### 8. Thread — GovCon Opportunity Radar before proposal automation
Status: draft
Category: new territory
Source: GovCon Proposal Automation OS + VXE consultant notes + Last30Days GovCon report

```text
1/ I’m building toward a GovCon Opportunity Radar before trying to automate full proposals.

The problem: small contractors do not just need prettier proposal drafts.

They need better timing, better qualification, and better evidence before they decide to bid.

2/ The first wedge is not "AI writes the proposal."

The first wedge is:
- monitor the right PSC/NAICS areas
- pull historical awards
- identify incumbents
- notice recompete patterns
- score fit/risk
- create the next human action

3/ A consultant recently made a simple point that stuck with me:

A lot of contractors are not even using USASpending well.

That means the opportunity is not always exotic AI.
Sometimes the opportunity is disciplined research plus automation.

4/ The workflow I want first:

- opportunity comes in
- system extracts agency, deadline, PSC, set-aside, place of performance
- checks award history
- suggests similar keywords/contracts
- drafts outreach notes
- builds a bid/no-bid checklist

Human still owns judgment.

5/ The moat is not generic AI writing.

The moat is company-specific memory:
- past performance
- vendor network
- relationship notes
- pricing judgment
- compliance discipline
- clean source trails

That is an operating system, not a chatbot.
```

### 9. What I learned today — Capital confidence and bounded asks
Status: draft
Category: direct reflection/update
Source: `Faleth Capital - Investable Wedge and Capital Confidence.md` + VXE capital/location note

```text
What I learned today:

A vague raise forces people to judge the whole dream.
A bounded ask lets them evaluate one operating constraint.

"Do you believe in Faleth?" is too abstract.

"Would this specific capital structure remove this specific bottleneck with this downside protection?" is useful.

The second question is where real conversations can start.
```
