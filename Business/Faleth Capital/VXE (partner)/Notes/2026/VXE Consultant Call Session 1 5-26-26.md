## Session 1 Opener
Instead of looking at contracts that are already out there, knowing the people behind the solicitations to get 6 months ahead of the game.
The Mindset Shift "By the time a solicitation hits SAM.gov, you're already losing."
The Hunter- Gets in 6 to 18 months early. Shapes requirements. Builds relationships before the RFP exists.
The Farmer- Shows up at the finish line. Competes on price. Wonders why they keep losing.
When you help word the RFP, when they vote on the awardee, they hear your wording and pick you
If you lose a bid, no worries. Call the CO, say "I see you putting this contract out every 6 months, how is it going?"
Usaspending.com covers everything the government has ever bought
RFI, Request for Information, they are asking "I want to buy something, but I dont know what we need". You get the opportunity to tell them what to buy.
As a hunter, you have to have a plan of attack. You dont just kill it, you have to skin it too
1. Where does our pipeline come from today?
2. What does Len do that no one else on this team currently does to source opportunity?
3. If Len was unreachable for the next 90 days, what would happen to our pipeline?
Being able to talk to the CO, and tell them why we will win it.
You want to build a relationship with the CO and tell them you want to be on their shortlist for their needs
First, write an email. Second, phone call. Third, LinkedIn message.
Doing business with the government, everything is documented. Usaspending.
The goal is to own opportunities from start to finish and to build out a case for it, get recurring contracts because you are keeping up with them, not just hitting enter on a contract
