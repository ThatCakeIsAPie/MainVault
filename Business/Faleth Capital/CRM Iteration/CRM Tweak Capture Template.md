# CRM Tweak Capture Template

Use this if you want a structured capture. Otherwise, just message Delta with `CRM tweak:` and dump the thought.

```text
CRM tweak:
Screen:
What I saw:
What I expected:
How bad: low / medium / high / blocking
Evidence:
```

## Examples

```text
CRM bug:
Screen: Ticket detail on mobile
What I saw: payout preview numbers feel attached to the wrong person
What I expected: each worker's points and dollars are visually grouped together
How bad: medium
Evidence: screenshot attached
```

```text
CRM feature:
Screen: Bounty board
What I saw: I want workers to understand what they get paid for the next node before reserving it
What I expected: show node-specific pay clearly on the card
How bad: high
Evidence:
```
