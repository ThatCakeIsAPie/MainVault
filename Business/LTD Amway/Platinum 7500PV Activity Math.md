# Platinum / 7500PV Activity Math

Lyle's rough activity standard for reaching Platinum / 7500PV.

## Activity standard

| Cadence | Metric |
|---|---:|
| Daily | 10 conversations |
| Daily | 3 opening-the-door conversations |
| Weekly | 4 booked MG1s (Meet and Greet 1) |
| Monthly | 10 MG1s ran |
| Monthly | 1–2 sponsors |
| Annual | 12 legs |
| Monthly in depth | 80 total MG1s |

## Interpretation

This is the level of activity required to hit Platinum: not a motivational slogan, but a throughput model. The core bottleneck is not desire; it is maintaining daily conversation volume, converting enough of those into opened doors and booked MG1s, and eventually creating enough depth that activity is no longer only personal effort.

## Delta note

Treat this as a **process scoreboard**: conversations → opened doors → booked MG1s → ran MG1s → sponsors → legs → depth. If a month is off pace, diagnose the stage conversion instead of morally prosecuting the human operator. Revolutionary concept, I know: inspect the pipe before yelling at the water.
