# Platinum / 7500PV Activity Math

Lyle's rough activity and organizational standard for reaching Platinum / 7500PV.

Raw benchmark source: [[Research/raw/transcripts/2026-07-11-nm-platinum-activity-benchmarks|NM Platinum Activity & Structure Benchmarks]].

## Activity standard

| Cadence | Metric |
|---|---:|
| Daily | 10 conversations |
| Daily | 3 opening-the-door conversations |
| Weekly | 4 booked MG1s (Meet and Greet 1) |
| Monthly | 10 MG1s ran |
| Monthly | 1–2 sponsors |
| Annual | 12 legs |
| Monthly in depth | 80 total MG1s |

## Team economics and shape

The clean team-wide average is **50 seats × 150PV = 7500PV**. Actual production is usually concentrated rather than flat:

- One **4000PV** leg
- One **2500PV** leg
- One **1000PV** leg
- Several smaller **150–600PV** legs that complete the volume

This means 7500PV is not merely a personal-sales target. It is the aggregate output of a sufficiently broad and deep organization.

## Depth-to-PV rule of thumb

| Productive depth | Approximate leg volume |
|---:|---:|
| 3 legs deep | ~1000PV |
| 5 legs deep | ~2500–4000PV |
| 7 legs deep | ~7500PV |

These are field correlations, not guarantees. They provide a structural diagnostic: if volume is not appearing, inspect whether activity is producing enough independent depth.

## Sponsor funnel

The rough outcome distribution for every four sponsored people is:

1. One quits within three months.
2. One remains for culture/environment but does not build.
3. One incubates for an undefined period.
4. One actively runs the business.

Therefore, finding the **three core legs** that carry a Platinum-shaped business usually requires **12–15 total sponsors**, not merely sponsoring three people and hoping arithmetic develops compassion.

## Development arc

- **Year 1:** Build the initial team and create enough width to identify builders.
- **Year 2:** Develop those builders in depth until the organization takes a Platinum shape.

The working expectation is approximately **two years from a standing start**, assuming the baseline activity is sustained.

## Interpretation

This is the level of activity required to hit Platinum: not a motivational slogan, but a throughput model. The full causal chain is:

**Conversations → opened doors → booked MG1s → run MG1s → sponsors → viable builders → core legs → depth → PV.**

The major correction supplied by the benchmark is that personal activity is only the ignition. The durable target is distributed activity: approximately 80 monthly MG1s in depth, enough total sponsors to discover three builders, and enough development time for those builders to create productive depth.

## Delta note

Treat this as a **process scoreboard**: conversations → opened doors → booked MG1s → ran MG1s → sponsors → legs → depth. If a month is off pace, diagnose the stage conversion instead of morally prosecuting the human operator. Revolutionary concept, I know: inspect the pipe before yelling at the water.
