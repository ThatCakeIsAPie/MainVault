# Founders Triple Diamond Work Requirement Model

## Destination

The September 2024 U.S. Amway Business Reference Guide defines:

- **Triple Diamond:** 10+ Founders Platinum groups and 43 Qualification Credits.
- **Founders Triple Diamond:** 10+ Founders Platinum groups and 52 Qualification Credits.

This operating model translates the **10-group width requirement** into Lyle's personal activity. It does not pretend that personal sponsoring alone automatically produces the 52 QCs; the depth/QC engine must be tracked separately.

## User-confirmed sponsor cohort

For each **12 people personally sponsored in one year**, expect the cohort to mature approximately as follows:

| Outcome | People | Share of sponsored cohort |
|---|---:|---:|
| Quit | 3 | 25% |
| Stick around but do not seriously build | 3 | 25% |
| Do something, but without sustained hard-running activity | 3 | 25% |
| Run hard | 3 | 25% |
| **Eventually produce a Platinum organization** | **1 of the 3 hard runners** | **8.33% of all sponsors** |

The Platinum-producing person may appear **in depth**. The personally sponsored individual does not have to qualify personally; the relevant width outcome is a distinct sponsored group containing a Founders Platinum.

Canonical planning conversion:

> **12 personal sponsors → three hard runners → one eventual Platinum-bearing group.**

This is Lyle's field-planning heuristic, not an Amway guarantee or a statistically validated corporate conversion rate.

## Baseline sustainable operating year

Assumption confirmed by Lyle: **five active days per week for 50 weeks**, producing 250 operating days.

| Funnel stage | Cadence | Annual requirement | Implied stage conversion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conversations | 10 per active day | **2,500** | — |
| Opening-the-door conversations | 3 per active day | **750** | 30% of conversations |
| MG1s booked | 4 per week | **200** | 26.7% of opened doors |
| MG1s run | 10 per month | **120** | 60% of bookings |
| Personally sponsored | 1 per month | **12** | 10% of run MG1s |
| Run hard | cohort outcome | **3** | 25% of sponsors |
| Eventual Platinum-bearing groups | cohort outcome | **1** | 33.3% of hard runners; 8.33% of sponsors |

Therefore, one expected Platinum-bearing width group represents approximately:

- **2,500 conversations**
- **750 opened doors**
- **200 MG1 bookings**
- **120 MG1s run**
- **12 personally sponsored people**
- **Three hard runners**
- **One eventual Platinum-bearing group**

## Stretch year: 16 personal sponsors

Holding the same funnel conversion rates, a 16-sponsor year requires approximately:

| Funnel stage | Exact scaled pace | Rounded operating target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conversations | 13.33 per active day | **14/day** |
| Opening doors | 4 per active day | **4/day** |
| MG1s booked | 5.33 per week | **6/week** |
| MG1s run | 13.33 per month | **14/month** |
| Personally sponsored | 1.33 per month | **16/year** |
| Expected eventual Platinum-bearing groups | 1.33 per cohort-year | **roughly 1–2** |

A stretch year does not change the one-in-twelve planning conversion; it moves more people through the funnel.

## Total personal work for the 10-group width floor

At one expected Platinum-bearing group per 12 personal sponsors, the expected personal input for ten width groups is:

| Cumulative input/outcome | Ten-group working total |
|---|---:|
| Conversations | **25,000** |
| Opening doors | **7,500** |
| MG1s booked | **2,000** |
| MG1s run | **1,200** |
| Personally sponsored | **120** |
| Quit | ~30 |
| Stick without seriously building | ~30 |
| Do something intermittently | ~30 |
| Run hard | ~30 |
| Eventual Platinum-bearing groups | **10** |

## Time horizon

- At **12 sponsors per year**, 120 sponsors require **10 sponsor-years**.
- At **16 sponsors per year**, 120 sponsors require **7.5 sponsor-years**.
- If a cohort requires approximately two additional years to mature into Platinum, the crude calendar horizons are approximately **12 years baseline** or **9.5 years stretch** from a standing start.

Those calendar figures are pipeline estimates, not promises. Cohorts overlap; individual groups can mature faster, slower, or never. The purpose of the model is to make the required work visible before the outcome arrives.

## Two distinct engines

### 1. Personal width engine

Track:

- Conversations
- Opened doors
- MG1s booked and run
- Personal sponsors
- Annual sponsor cohort distribution
- Distinct sponsored groups that eventually contain a Founders Platinum

### 2. Organizational depth engine

Track separately:

- Hard runners in depth
- New Platinum and Founders Platinum qualifications
- Qualification Credits by group
- QC concentration and per-group caps
- Progress toward 43 QC / 52 QC

Do not substitute sponsor count or Platinum count for Qualification Credits. Width and depth interact, but they are not the same unit—apparently arithmetic retains its tiresome insistence on dimensional consistency.

## Operating scoreboard

### Baseline weekly scoreboard

- **50 conversations**
- **15 opened doors**
- **4 MG1s booked**
- Approximately **2–3 MG1s run**
- Average **0.24 sponsors**, resolving to approximately one sponsor per month

### Baseline annual covenant

- **250 active workdays**
- **2,500 conversations**
- **750 opened doors**
- **200 booked MG1s**
- **120 run MG1s**
- **12 personal sponsors**
- Expected cohort: 3 quit / 3 stick / 3 do something / 3 run hard / 1 eventual Platinum-bearing group

The scoreboard judges execution at the funnel stage Lyle controls. It does not morally prosecute him for the maturation schedule of other adults—a feature that should perhaps have been standard equipment on humans.
