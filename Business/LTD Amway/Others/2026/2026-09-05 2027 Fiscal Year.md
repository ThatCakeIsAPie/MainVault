3 elements of breakthrough-
- State
	- How you feel determines how you perform.
	- Your emotional condition.
	- How you show up to everything.
	- What you internalize you externalize.
- Story
	- The meaning you give determines your future.
	- What you tell yourself determines your reality.
- Strategy
	- Actions that create results
	- Once your story and state are in line, your strategy becomes more effective.
2 out of three are mental.
The challenge is maintaining long enough for the strategy to work.
