A: It starts in your mindset. It is easy to have a positive mental attitude when you hit your goals. But what happens when you do not? Anything happens, good. When people quit because you blow them out, good. When you get laid off 15 times, good. "Stephen Curry knows he is still Stephen Curry when he misses a shot." You can't look at you right now and think "when I get to X, Y, Z spot, then ill be a certain way" wrong, you bounce back, because that is who you are.

A: Are you accountable to it? Are you the example? You got to talk about it. People are gonna do what you talk about. If you talk about doing 150PV, people do 150PV. Go help them sell products. You are in a business to go serve people. What is stopping them? Sometimes, the way they think about some things. The biggest pins are the biggest servers, the ones who help people overcome their challenges and win. People sell out to your standards because you served them. You can't control your team, but you can control your volume. Are you a team player, or are you not? Do you believe you are going to go 7500 one day? Then you will figure it out.
