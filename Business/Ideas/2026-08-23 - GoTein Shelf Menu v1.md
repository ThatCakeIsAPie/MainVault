## Named so far (Lyle)

### Bulk breakfast
- **Sausage egg burrito**
- **Sausage egg skillet** — 0.25 lb sausage, 5 large eggs, cheese mixed in.

Planning on pork sausage: sausage ~15g, eggs ~32g, 1 oz cheddar ~7g → **~53g / ~840 kcal**. Clears the floor. Two ounces of cheese puts it near 60g.

### Bulk lunch
- **Chicken rice cheese burrito**
- **Chicken rice bowl** — 6 oz chicken on brown rice, mozzarella melted on top.

If 6 oz is **cooked** (plated): chicken alone ~53g. Rice + 1.5 oz mozz → **~67g / ~630 kcal**.
If 6 oz is **raw**: chicken ~38g; rice + mozz still clears **~53g**. Lock cooked vs raw on the first spec sheet.

### Bulk dinner
- **Steak and chicken burrito** — 0.25 lb steak, chicken to 50g meat, brown rice, cheese.
- **Steak and chicken bowl** — same mix as the dinner burrito, prepped like the lunch bowl (rice well, cheese melted).

Same protein well, two vessels. That is the kitchen winning.

Steak burrito planning (0.25 lb **raw** steak):
- Steak ~25g protein, ~2.8 oz cooked.
- Chicken to 50g meat: about another quarter pound raw / ~2.9 oz cooked.
- Meat cost at $8/lb steak and $2.50/lb chicken: about **$2.61** before rice and cheese.
- Bowl with 200g brown rice + cheddar prints around **65g**.
