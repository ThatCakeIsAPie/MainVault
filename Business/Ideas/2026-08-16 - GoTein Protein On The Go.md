## Idea

Protein, on the go.

A fast-food-shaped place whose only job is high protein. Not “healthy.” Not “fit lifestyle.” Protein as the product.

Working dose targets:

- Shake: about 50g protein
- Most meals: about 100g protein
- Pickup meal prep for later storage, same protein standard per meal

## Why Lyle Thinks It Exists

Fitness-adjacent restaurants already exist. He has not seen a place whose focus is simply providing high-protein meals at those doses, with both eat-now and take-home inventory.

## Core Wedge (as stated)

The menu is not a wellness brand. The menu is a protein delivery system that happens to taste like food.

Two loops in one kitchen:

1. Immediate: counter / drive-style shakes and plates
2. Deferred: meal prep pickup that stores later

## Open Questions
