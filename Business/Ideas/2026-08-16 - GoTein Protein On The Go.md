## Idea

Protein, on the go.

A quick-stop whose only job is high protein. Not “healthy.” Not “fit lifestyle.” Protein as the product. Every item is built for that demographic.

Working dose targets:

- Shake / grab breakfast: about 50g protein
- Hero eat-now meals: about 100g protein
- Grab-and-go shelf (hot and cold): same honesty per item, including an $8 / 50g breakfast burrito that reheats in a microwave or on a stove

## Why Lyle Thinks It Exists

Meal-prep delivery already exists and is usually expensive per gram. Fast food already has decent protein options, but those places do not advertise to the protein customer. That customer goes there because it is what they can find.

The gap is a place that is obviously for them: every SKU geared to protein, plus a hot and cold grab-and-go meal-prep shelf. Pay and leave. No engineering a Chipotle order. No subscription box markup.

## Core Wedge (as stated)

Quick stop. Default protein. Shelf plus counter.

Two loops in one kitchen:

1. Immediate: counter shakes and hot plates
2. Deferred: sealed hot/cold grab-and-go that stores and reheats later
