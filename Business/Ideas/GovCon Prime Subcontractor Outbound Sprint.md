# GovCon Prime Subcontractor Outbound Sprint

Created: 2026-06-08
Tags: #business-idea #govcon #outbound #subcontracting #vxe #libretech

## Core Decision

After the Shopify/domain transfer is handled, the highest-leverage next move is **not vague GovCon research**. It is a narrow subcontractor outbound sprint.

Goal:

```text
Find 20 likely prime contractors → identify the right contact → send a useful capability/capacity intro → ask for subcontractor/vendor onboarding or a short fit call.
```

This supports Louie's goal: build past performance/history by subcontracting under companies already winning government work.

## Why This Is High Leverage

VXE/LibreTech are early-stage in GovCon credibility. Direct prime bids without past performance can be hard. Subcontracting can create:

- past performance history
- relationships with primes
- agency/category familiarity
- referenceable work
- subcontractor/vendor onboarding records
- insight into what buyers and primes actually need

This also matches the VXE consultant note:

> By the time a solicitation hits SAM.gov, you're already losing.

The point is to get upstream of active opportunities by building relationships with primes and agency-adjacent players before the RFP is the only thing left.

## First-Principles Target

The real job is not "do outbound."

The real job is:

```text
Make it easy for a prime contractor to remember us as useful capacity for work they already need to staff, deliver, or de-risk.
```

## Sources / Directories to Use

Start with official/direct sources:

1. SBA Directory of Federal Government Prime Contractors with Subcontracting Plans
2. SBA SUBNet subcontracting opportunities
3. SBA Small Business Search
4. USASpending.gov recipient/award history
5. Agency prime contractor lists, especially:
   - DHS prime contractor list
   - DLA subcontracting resources
   - GSA subcontracting directory
   - DOT subcontracting directory if relevant

## Targeting Criteria

Prefer primes that match at least 3 of these:

- under or around $50M annual revenue, if discoverable
- active federal contract history in target agencies/categories
- has subcontracting plan or small-business liaison contact
- works in categories VXE/LibreTech can support
- shows recurring awards, not one-off weird awards
- likely needs flexible subcontractor capacity
- has current or recent awards near CECOM, Tobyhanna, DHS, DLA, GSA, DoD, or adjacent technical/repair/logistics work

## 2-Hour Sprint Plan

### Step 1 — Define the offer in one sentence

Template:

```text
We help [prime type] deliver [specific contract support] by providing [capability] with fast, flexible subcontractor capacity.
```

Do not start outbound until this is clear enough to put in an email subject line.

### Step 2 — Build a 20-prime target list

Fields:

- Company
- Website
- Agency/customer history
- Relevant award or contract category
- Why they might need us
- Contact name/title
- Contact email/contact form
- Source link
- Outreach status
- Next follow-up date

### Step 3 — Send 5 extremely targeted emails

Do not blast 50 generic emails. Send 5 emails where the company-specific reason is obvious.

### Step 4 — Call if phone number exists

Order:

1. Email
2. Phone call
3. LinkedIn message

This matches the VXE consultant note.

### Step 5 — Log every response

Every response teaches what the market actually cares about.

## Outreach Email Template

Subject options:

```text
Subcontractor capacity for [agency/category] work
Potential subcontracting fit for [Company] federal work
Supporting [Company]'s [agency/category] contracts
```

Email:

```text
Hi [Name],

I saw that [Company] has supported [agency/program/category] work, including [specific source-backed clue].

My brother and I are building subcontractor capacity around [specific capability/category]. We are looking to support primes that need reliable small-business/vendor capacity for [specific work type].

The reason I am reaching out is simple: if [Company] ever needs additional capacity on [agency/category] work, I would like to understand your subcontractor/vendor onboarding process and whether there may be a fit.

Would you be the right person to ask, or is there a supplier diversity / small business liaison contact I should speak with?

Best,
Lyle Cole
[phone]
[website/capability statement if available]
```

## Phone Script

```text
Hi, this is Lyle Cole. I am reaching out because I saw [Company] has federal work around [agency/category], and we are building subcontractor capacity in that area.

I am not calling about a specific solicitation. I am trying to understand whether you have a subcontractor/vendor onboarding process and who handles potential small-business subcontracting relationships.

Who would be the right person to speak with?
```

## What Not To Do

- Do not lead with "we are new and need past performance."
- Do not ask them to mentor us.
- Do not send generic capability spam.
- Do not chase active RFPs only.
- Do not overbuild automation before proving the manual target list and outreach script.

## KPI for Today

Minimum useful output:

```text
20 target primes identified
5 targeted emails sent or ready to send
1 reusable outreach template refined
1 list of missing capability-statement info
```

If that is done, the day was productive.
