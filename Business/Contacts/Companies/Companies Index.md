# Companies Index

Companies are the parent entities in the contact system. Each organization gets one folder containing its canonical company profile and every individual contact associated with it.

## Folder Pattern

`Business/Contacts/Companies/Company Name/`

- `Company Name.md` — canonical organization profile
- `First Last — Company Name.md` — individual contact
- `Second Person — Company Name.md` — additional contact at the same organization

Copy [[Company Profile Template]] and [[Contact Template]] when creating a new company folder.

## Company Directory

- Add companies here as `[[Company Name]]`.

## Query Uses

- Which companies are potential vendors for a capability?
- Which companies may buy a particular offering?
- Who do we know at a specific company?
- Which company has the strongest relationship coverage?
- Which companies have multiple contacts but no active next step?
- Which companies serve a particular geography, industry, NAICS code, or PSC?
