# Business Card Inbox

Drop business-card scans or photographs in this `_Cards` folder. Use a filename that matches the canonical contact note:

`First Last — Company — Business Card.jpg`

## Unprocessed Cards

Add an entry here when a card image arrives but its contact note has not yet been completed.

- None

## Processing Standard

For each card:

1. Preserve the original image.
2. Create or locate the company's folder under `Business/Contacts/Companies/`.
3. If needed, create the canonical company profile from [[Company Profile Template]].
4. Transcribe card-stated information into an individual note copied from [[Contact Template]] inside that company folder.
5. Put company-wide research in the company profile and person-specific research in the individual's note.
6. Distinguish confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unresolved questions.
7. Assess vendor, customer, and strategic fit.
8. Cross-link the company profile, individual contact, [[Companies Index]], and [[Contacts Index]].
9. Remove the card from **Unprocessed Cards** only after the note is researched and verified.
