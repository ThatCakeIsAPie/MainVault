# Business Card Inbox

Drop business-card scans or photographs in this `_Cards` folder. Use a filename that matches the canonical contact note:

`First Last — Company — Business Card.jpg`

## Unprocessed Cards

Add an entry here when a card image arrives but its contact note has not yet been completed.

- None

## Processing Standard

For each card:

1. Preserve the original image.
2. Transcribe card-stated information into a note copied from [[Contact Template]].
3. Research the person and company using attributable public sources.
4. Distinguish confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unresolved questions.
5. Assess vendor, customer, and strategic fit.
6. Link the finished note from [[Contacts Index]].
7. Remove the card from **Unprocessed Cards** only after the note is researched and verified.
