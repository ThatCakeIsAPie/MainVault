# The Spirit-Filled Life
## Experiencing the Power of the Holy Spirit in your Everyday Life
Ephesians 5:17-18 Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the Lord's will is. Be filled with the spirit...
We can sometimes try to understand God's will, and shelve what we already know is His will
Be filled means keep being filled
Another aspect of the spirit is power. Not power to excite you, but *power for a purpose*
Acts 1:3-9 John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit
Jesus said "I am going to give you power" and people still asked what time to expect for the King to come back
*The last thing Jesus said to the disciples is "you will receive power when the holy spirit comes upon you, and you will be my witnesses"*
The word "with" = "Para"
"The wicked flee when a holy person pursues"
 God doesn't play fair. Cause if you are running from the Lord, He will give you a kid who loves Him.
 "In" = "En"
 All you have to have is the faith of a child and you are saved. He sees you, He fills you.
 Christ doesn't stop just by saving you.
 "Upon/on" = "Epi"
