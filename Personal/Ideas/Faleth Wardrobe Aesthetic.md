# Faleth Wardrobe Aesthetic

Sparked by Boot Barn green Hayw boots (12 EE, BBXSP26L5-2, $599.99) fitting the look he wants for a new wardrobe.

2026-08-27: full board set re-dropped — "outfit ideas that I want to go with." Boards saved under `Personal/Ideas/assets/faleth-wardrobe/`.

## Through-line (from the boards)

- Deep forest green as the signature color
- Gold metal accents (watch, buttons, chain, clover pin)
- Warm neutrals: cream, tan, khaki, brown, cognac
- Understated wealth, quality over trends, legacy over everything
- Texan roots worn with pride
- Same man across gym → casual → boardroom → ranch
- Hats and boots as load-bearing western identifiers (not costume)
