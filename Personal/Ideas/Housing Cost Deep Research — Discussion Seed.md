# Housing Cost Deep Research — Discussion Seed

Brought in by Lyle (2026-08-24) as **basis for discussion**, not as a plan. Full text lives in the Hermes Ideas cache path above.

## Why it is in the Ideas net

Sits next to the fertility thread: the original X spark blamed housing (boomer passive income) for the fertility problem; Lyle’s model says incentives/economic value of kids, not money/housing as the load-bearing limiter. This report is the housing-side stack.

## Report claims (compressed, not endorsed)

- High U.S. housing cost = supply deficit × selective demand × construction/finance costs × slow-adjusting institutions — not one single “shortage” story.
- ~95% FHFA house-price rise 2016Q1→2026Q1 (nominal); much of it post-2020. Mortgage rates ~6.65% (Aug 2026) vs ~2.9% early 2021 → ~54% higher P&I on same principal.
- Post-2008 construction collapse still compounds in the stock; Freddie Mac ~3.7M unit shortage estimate (model-dependent).
- Zoning/NIMBY matter unevenly by place; SF Fed work warns supply-elasticity measures don’t cleanly explain cross-metro price/quantity the way the simple model predicts; income growth strongly tied to price growth.
- Demand is geographically selective (high-income metros, migration, cheap 2020–21 credit).
- High rates suppress both affordability and supply (lock-in + developer finance).
- Investors/foreign buyers secondary nationally, can matter locally.
- Framing for Faleth in the doc: housing as **jobs-access** (rent/mortgage + transport + time), not stand-alone real estate — jobs–housing–transport platform.
