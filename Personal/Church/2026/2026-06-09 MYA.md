# Summer Kickoff
## Control
It can feel like you are not in control, but trying to take control
We love to be in control, and it has never been easier to feel like you are in control of your life
In the young adult season, it's easy to feel:
- Confused
	- "What am I supposed to do with my life?"
- Insecure
	- "Am I falling behind?"
		- It is common to feel like you are behind, but the root of that statement is always comparison
- Lost
	- "This isn't how I thought it would go."
- Stuck
	- "Is this all there is?"
		- You can get stuck in a routine, but eventually look up and think "Is this it?"
Underneath all of them, is the struggle to control circumstances and forces we can't see.
Proverbs 3:5-6 Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight
*If you are walking with Jesus, you are right where you need to be*
We can forget the power in it, that there is an actual God and He loves us
