# Summer Kickoff
## Control
It can feel like you are not in control, but trying to take control
We love to be in control, and it has never been easier to feel like you are in control of your life
In the young adult season, it's easy to feel:
- Confused
	- "What am I supposed to do with my life?"
- Insecure
	- "Am I falling behind?"
		- It is common to feel like you are behind, but the root of that statement is always comparison
- Lost
	- "This isn't how I thought it would go."
- Stuck
	- "Is this all there is?"
		- You can get stuck in a routine, but eventually look up and think "Is this it?"
Underneath all of them, is the struggle to control circumstances and forces we can't see.
Proverbs 3:5-6 Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight
*If you are walking with Jesus, you are right where you need to be*
We can forget the power in it, that there is an actual God and He loves us
Matthew 19:16-22 "If you want to enter life, keep the commandments." "What do I still lack?" "Sell all you have, give it to the poor, and come follow me" The man walked away sad and did not do that, as he had great wealth
Maybe you grew up in an environment where it was all about what you did. And that in order to be loved, it was tied to what you do.
The young man was really asking "God, what do you want from me?"
The issue with the young man was not about wealth, but surrender, the posture of your heart
What does God want from me?
1. Your Heart
	1. He doesn't want good deeds, to keep the commandments, He wants your heart. He doesn't want a piece of your heart, he wants your entire life.
	2. We sometimes only present the best with God, throwing things in drawers and in the closet, presenting yourself like you have it all together, but you can't help but feel the guilt.
	3. When we walk up to God, we want to be great. But God doesn't just want that, He wants it all. He wants to go into the closet, under the bed, He wants it all.
	4. "If you love me, you will obey all my commandments"
	5. The danger of growing up thinking you are loved because of what you do, and doing that with God. God loves you right where you are at.
	6. The young ruler wasn't concerned about money, but he had made it his god in his life. Jesus was pressing in on him to get after his heart.
	7. We often all put things in front of God and treat that as our source
2. Your Devotion
	1. God doesn't want to be part of your life, but Lord of your life
	2. The God we serve wants to be authority of your life
	3. We often want to make it happen on our own
	4. We often look towards other things to be satisfied, but wonder why we end up feeling unfulfilled
	5. If you have felt distant from God, the lie is that God doesn't want to be in relationship with you, but He wants to be there, He is standing with open arms for you
3. Your Trust
	1. Do I really trust God?
	2. It is not a trust of just how good your life is going, but do you trust God to be there with you even when you are going through it?
	3. He knew from a young age, that he is saved and is going to heaven, but grew distant from God. He got exposed to a lot of stuff like substances come into his life, he was feeling far from God.
