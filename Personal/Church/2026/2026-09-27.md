## Greater
### Week One
Your goals for this series:
1. Grow in your understanding of the bible... because we want you to know God
2. Recognize the God who is GREATER
3. Experience God's GREATNESS in your life
God is bigger than what you are facing, what you lost, what you are carrying.
1 John 4:4 He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world
The bible is not a museum of what God used to do. It is the stories of the same God who has the ability to still work in your life.
He doesn't even have a requirement for you to have it all figured out
Ephesians 3:20 Now to him who is able to do immeasurably more...
It is too easy to see these stories and think "this is for someone else"
People tend to believe God has this buffet of goodness, but they are only for someone else.
The stories are in there so you can experience God's greatness
Exodus 3:8 God promises to bring Israel out of Egypt into the Promised Land
Numbers 13 They sent 12 spies into the land - only two (Joshua and Caleb) said they could take the land
At 13, he knew he had a call on his life, but he didn't know if his local church would have any hope
Deuteronomy 34 Moses and the generation of those who complained in the wilderness are dead.
Joshua 1:7-9 God tells Joshua over and over, "Be strong and courageous..." He tells Joshua to meditate on the book of the Law both day and night.... do everything written in it... so that you will prosper and be successful.
Joshua 3:15-16 As they were ready to cross the Jordan River, as soon as their feet touched the water's edge, the water from upstream stopped flowing.
If you believe God will deal with it and you won't have to do it afraid, you haven't read the bible.
There are a bunch of people who have had God's possessions, but have never been possessed by Him.
Jericho is the first city in the Promised Land, the oldest city on earth, and the first city with fortified walls and a tower.
The peptalk is great up until you see the walls, and it gets real.
The Lord tells Joshua He's delivered Jericho into their hands.
You have all these plans, but He isn't asking you to plan it, but to submit and walk it through. He is ordering your steps.
He said to march around the city one time per day for six days with the armed men, the priests, and the ark. He didn't say "Here is a catapult to take it down."
*You aren't going to pragmatically solve the biggest problems in your life.*
On the seventh day, march around the city seven times. When the trumpets blow have the whole army give a loud shout.
That entire plan would be problematic for Christians nowadays.
Joshua 6:20 When that happened, the wall collapsed and the men took the city.
