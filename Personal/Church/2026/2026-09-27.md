## Greater
### Week One
Your goals for this series:
1. Grow in your understanding of the bible... because we want you to know God
2. Recognize the God who is GREATER
3. Experience God's GREATNESS in your life
God is bigger than what you are facing, what you lost, what you are carrying.
1 John 4:4 He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world
The bible is not a museum of what God used to do. It is the stories of the same God who has the ability to still work in your life.
He doesn't even have a requirement for you to have it all figured out
Ephesians 3:20 Now to him who is able to do immeasurably more...
It is too easy to see these stories and think "this is for someone else"
People tend to believe God has this buffet of goodness, but they are only for someone else.
The stories are in there so you can experience God's greatness
Exodus 3:8 God promises to bring Israel out of Egypt into the Promised Land
Numbers 13 They sent 12 spies into the land - only two (Joshua and Caleb) said they could take the land
At 13, he knew he had a call on his life, but he didn't know if his local church would have any hope
Deuteronomy 34 Moses and the generation of those who complained in the wilderness are dead.
Joshua 1:7-9 God tells Joshua over and over, "Be strong and courageous..." He tells Joshua to meditate on the book of the Law both day and night.... do everything written in it... so that you will prosper and be successful.
Joshua 3:15-16 As they were ready to cross the Jordan River, as soon as their feet touched the water's edge, the water from upstream stopped flowing.
If you believe God will deal with it and you won't have to do it afraid, you haven't read the bible.
There are a bunch of people who have had God's possessions, but have never been possessed by Him.
Jericho is the first city in the Promised Land, the oldest city on earth, and the first city with fortified walls and a tower.
The peptalk is great up until you see the walls, and it gets real.
The Lord tells Joshua He's delivered Jericho into their hands.
You have all these plans, but He isn't asking you to plan it, but to submit and walk it through. He is ordering your steps.
He said to march around the city one time per day for six days with the armed men, the priests, and the ark. He didn't say "Here is a catapult to take it down."
*You aren't going to pragmatically solve the biggest problems in your life.*
On the seventh day, march around the city seven times. When the trumpets blow have the whole army give a loud shout.
That entire plan would be problematic for Christians nowadays.
Joshua 6:20 When that happened, the wall collapsed and the men took the city.
You can read it and think "this is crazy", but don't miss the message. The message isn't "Your walls will always fall exactly when or how you want them to." The message is "Don't let the size of the walls in front of you make you question the size or greatness of God."
Can you still believe in God's plan on the 6th day? The 7th day?
Wherever you are at in your journey, have you succumbed to those walls? Or are you still marching?
- 1. God had to prepare them to possess the land.
	- A lot of preparation and time in the process, but it changes when you stand and look at that wall.
	- You do know that you prayed for this and God answered this? You said you wanted to do something great and are available, but you face the wall. Possessing it is different. There are a lot of talkers, not a lot of people go "I've been prepared for this, I am going to possess it."
	- Yes, what you are bearing is heavy, but He gave it to you to possess.
	- You begin to wonder "am I adequate?" No, you aren't. But you are the right person.
	- Somebody else wasn't the one God chose. *When you are taking ground, it is different.*
	- God has prepared you and promised it to you, but now you are in the possession part.
What makes taking ground so difficult?
Taking ground comes with the intimidation of the walls.
Taking ground brings fear and the stories you tell yourself.
Did God see you through? Yes He did.
Why, after all of God's faithfulness, am I still afraid? Fear is not only a spirit, it is a spirit, but the intimidation of the enemy doesn't want you to take that ground.
Taking ground adds a whole different weight into our lives.
- 2. God gives promises, but that doesn't mean there won't be walls
	- What is your wall?
		- A marriage challenge?
		- Problems at work/career?
		- Health diagnosis?
		- One of your kids is struggling?
		- Fear over a financial situation?
	- The reason you have a different weight is because you decided to finally get on the field.
		- When you make that decision, you move from the suite to where you are going to bear some weight
- 3. Taking new ground requires a new level of faith.
	- A lot of people never get to the point where their walls fall, because they aren't willing to get to a new level of faith.
	- To have something you never had, requires you to do something you have never done.
	- It is not always information, but impartation.
	- Faith comes from years. Years of facing that fear, of pushing and taking ground.
	- He who is greater in me is greater than He who is in the world.
Sometimes, you have to do something that seems crazy, something you have never done before.
God never asked us to read the word and to bring our opinion to it. He said "just trust me, have faith in what I said. I feel like im nuts, going crazy." But you will start to see the faith.
