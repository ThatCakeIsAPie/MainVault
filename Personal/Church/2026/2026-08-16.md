## Habits of Emotionally Healthy People
Habits are scriptural
When we think about habits, we often think of bad habits
- Setting 5 alarms and treating them as suggestions
- Googling symptoms and deciding you have 3 days to live
- Saying "I'm not hungry" and then eating half of someone elses food
- Calling someone "bro" who is not in fact your bro
- Putting something somewhere "safe" and then wondering where it went
You can't put anything into practice to make God love you more
You can't "do" enough to clean yourself up to be perfect to a Good and Holy God
As He begins to sanctify us and renew us, we don't just have a cerebral life, we begin to live different. Put these into practice.
