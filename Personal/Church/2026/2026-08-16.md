## Stress
"This too shall pass"
The number one thing he is asked to talk about is stress, and it isn't even close
It is not a season when you have been stressed out your whole life
Let's talk about stress:
- Not all stress is bad.
	- There is no utopian world with no stress
	- We should be grateful for the challenges, because God puts it in our life to build us up
- What is stress?
- It's the body's response to a real or perceived threat
- There is a problem when you live under inordinate amounts of stress
- Why are we stressed out?
	- Life has challenging seasons
	- We enter new circumstances and transitions
	- We experience the fear that comes from unexpected adversity
- We can learn to manage this kind of stress better.
- *How you approach your season impacts your level of anxiety of this season*
Why are young people stressed? They are told "you can be anything"
I can be anything God desires for me to be, I can be great in that space, but I can't be anything.
You can't have it all, at least not now. We often don't understand that. You can't have all the news at once, cause then all the news become stress.
We aren't as good at multi tasking as we say we are.
When you have too many options, it can create too much stress
You can be high responsibility wired, and have all the decisions coming at us all at once
We can learn to manage this kind of stress better
There is a stress you can avoid- stress that comes from striving
Striving is living in a consistent state of doing whatever it takes to get more
More striving = more stress. Less striving = less stress.
Philippians 2:3 Do nothing out of selfish ambition, but for others
Jacob is an example of a striver. Jacob means "heel-grabber" that means "he deceives"
Genesis 25:26 his brother came out, with his hand grasping Esau's heel; so he was named Jacob. Jacob manipulated his brother
All of us who have a tendency to lean towards striving, hopefully we can get a little immunity from it
Striving is not motivation, but insecurity. They are often called "tryhards"
You just end up not being able to trust them because they have a striving spirit
Genesis 32:24-31 God dealt with the striving spirit in Jacob. It wasn't that they were actually wresting, but it was a theophany. You get a breakthrough in striving because you realize you ultimately have a problem with God.
Through his struggle, he had a limp. "You can always see those with a limp" those who rely only on their own ability
Strivers have more stress because they are over extended, they misdefine themselves, they want it fast. You are filled with comparison. They dont allow definition to come from wisdom, but the insecurity of striving.
We live in a microwave culture, but have a crockpot God. He works all that impurity and motive out, when He works you out of your future, you realize you were dumb for wanting that in your future.
They manipulate others, they blow past character-based warnings.
In God's Kingdom, the ends do not justify the means.
Jacob was the source of his own blessing until he wrestled with God
