# The Spirit-Filled Life
This week is more practically, how He comes into our lives and how we can follow Him with our lives
"All you need is the faith of a child"
Weeks 1 and 2 were:
Who the Holy Spirit Is
This week:
What the Holy Spirit Does
Our foundational passage is Ephesians 5:18
People think God says "Do a bunch of good things to come up to me" but in reality He says "I paid the price for you so you can come up to me"
The true meaning of "be filled" is actually to keep being filled
"Be filled" is not a suggestion, it is an ongoing command, an imperative
You can't fill yourself, you have to receive something from someone outside of you
It is 2nd person plural, it is for everyone
Everyone is stressed and tired today, but there is power. There is power when the Holy Spirit comes down upon you.
*The Holy Spirit is with us, we are empty -> He comes to live in us, He fills us -> Then He comes upon us and we are overflowing*
There is subsequent work of The Spirit in our lives
"Power for a Purpose"
There is always those people who are super spiritual, but do people want what you have? If you are empowered by the Spirit, people should be drawn not to you, but the Jesus inside of you. That is what it means when Jesus comes upon you
It is like kool-aid. It fills you, but it is also on you, it is sticky and people can see it.
The Anointing of the Holy Spirit
Anointing is connected to the pouring of oil "consecrated or made sacred; dedicated to God"
"Chrio" to anoint "Chrisma"...
2 Corinthians 1:21-22 He anointed us. He put His Spirit in our hearts as a deposit, guaranteeing what is to come.
The Holy Spirit is not actually oil, but He is like oil. Oil is separate from water.
We can think "I want to be like Moses or Elijah"
We have the whole Holy Spirit poured out on us, far more than any old Testament person ever did
When someone says something is "anointed", what do they mean?
You can have all the right sound equipment, the right projector, they can be talented, but you can tell they aren't connected to God.
There is this thing that is this intangible...
Has anyone ever prayed in an anointed way? Some people pray like there were the right words, but it wasn't connected to God. Like, you left with an impression of them, but not a hunger for God.
Anointing in the Old Testament:
The purpose was to dedicate the thing or person to God -> Jacob anointed the stone pillow he used at Bethel (Genesis 28:18) -> 
Anointing in the New Testament:
Jesus is the Messiah and the Christ. Both mean "Anointed One" -> God anointed us through His Spirit. 2 Corinthians 1:21 -> The sick should be prayed for and anointed with oil. James 5:14
The sooner you realize what you are comfortable with is not necessarily God's Will...
1 Thessalonians 1:5-8 "because our gospel came to you not simply with words but also with power, with the Holy Spirit and deep conviction. You know how we lived among you for your sake. You became imitators of us and of the Lord... We do not need to say anything about it." If you have to tell someone you are doing something great, you probably aren't working on anything great.
You should be so good that everyone sees it. Your work should be so good no one can ignore it.
What areas of our lives does the Holy Spirit anoint?
1. Anointed Decisions
	1. Romans 8:14 Led by the Spirit
	2. Have you ever said "God, I want you to anoint my business life. I want you to include the Holy Spirit in my presence and decision making."
	3. We can try and push harder against barriers, try and fix it
	4. The flesh drives, but the Holy Spirit leads
	5. "Never make a life-altering decision when you are not in a good emotional space"
	6. "Never make a decision under pressure" Every time we make it under external pressure, it is never good
2. Anointed Worship
	1. God is a continuationist. When you approach Him with a "going" mindset rather than a "been done" mindset, you approach everything differently.
	2. God doesn't need our approval. He existed before we did.
	3. In Revelation, it talks about incense rising out of bowls. It means that He listens when you praise Him. I don't worship Him because He needs it, it is a response from me to Him that changes me.
3. Anointed for Spiritual Battles
	1. There is a real enemy out there. Not everything is a spiritual battle, but everything happens in the spiritual before in the physical
	2. 2 Corinthians 10:4 "The weapons we fight with are not the weapon"
Think not just in the concept of the Holy Spirit, but what He wants to do in your life.
Mentally ascending to the truth of the Holy Spirit, but not actually engaging in it. You recognize it, but don't necessarily act on it.
Most of the time, God doesn't work the way of you waiting for Him to act.
If God has to usurp your will, where He is so hard-headed, that isn't good.
