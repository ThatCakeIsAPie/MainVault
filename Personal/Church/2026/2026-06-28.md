# The Spirit-Filled Life
This week is more practically, how He comes into our lives and how we can follow Him with our lives
"All you need is the faith of a child"
Weeks 1 and 2 were:
Who the Holy Spirit Is
This week:
What the Holy Spirit Does
Our foundational passage is Ephesians 5:18
People think God says "Do a bunch of good things to come up to me" but in reality He says "I paid the price for you so you can come up to me"
The true meaning of "be filled" is actually to keep being filled
"Be filled" is not a suggestion, it is an ongoing command, an imperative
You can't fill yourself, you have to receive something from someone outside of you
It is 2nd person plural, it is for everyone
Everyone is stressed and tired today, but there is power. There is power when the Holy Spirit comes down upon you.
*The Holy Spirit is with us, we are empty -> He comes to live in us, He fills us -> Then He comes upon us and we are overflowing*
There is subsequent work of The Spirit in our lives
"Power for a Purpose"
There is always those people who are super spiritual, but do people want what you have? If you are empowered by the Spirit, people should be drawn not to you, but the Jesus inside of you. That is what it means when Jesus comes upon you
It is like kool-aid. It fills you, but it is also on you, it is sticky and people can see it.
The Anointing of the Holy Spirit
Anointing is connected to the pouring of oil "consecrated or made sacred; dedicated to God"
"Chrio" to anoint "Chrisma"...
2 Corinthians 1:21-22 He anointed us. He put His Spirit in our hearts as a deposit, guaranteeing what is to come.
The Holy Spirit is not actually oil, but He is like oil. Oil is separate from water.
We can think "I want to be like Moses or Elijah"
We have the whole Holy Spirit poured out on us, far more than any old Testament person ever did
