We have moments where the Bible becomes real to us, not just something we read about, but the words jump off the page and become applicable in our very lives

James 1:22-25 Do not merely listen to the word, do what it says. Anyone who listens but does not do what it says is like someone who looks at his face in a mirror and forgets what he looks like.
Hearers vs Doers
Who do you want to be?
"What do you do?" is a very common question, but we have to be careful about tying this to your identity.
*The person you want to be is on the backside of conviction.*
*God changes your life one conviction at a time.*
**Convictions lived out over time form your character.**
One of the most common ways the Spirit talks to us is through conviction
What does the Holy Spirit convict us about?
1. Sin
2. Things that aren't beneficial for us
	1. There are things in our life that maybe aren't sin, but we still feel the Holy Spirit telling us to lay it down
3. Something God is asking us to do
When God convicts you to something, you have to grab it for yourself.
Conviction - A non-negotiable personal belief that influences choices or behaviors.
We all have that picture for our lives, but few of us embrace the procedure God uses, and that is conviction
Three convictions that can change your life:
- Integrity
	- 2 Timothy 2:21 If anyone cleanses himself from what is dishonorable, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart as holy, useful to the master of the house, ready for every good work
	- There is a difference in value between an uncrustable and a filet mignon
	- There is a difference in cost and a difference in value
	- If you want a filet mignon life, it is going to require a different level of conviction and integrity
	- There are no shortcuts for integrity in our lives
- Stewardship
	- Over 2,300 verses in the bible talk about stewardship
	- It isn't just about finances, but everything we have
	- Everything belongs to Him, but we get the opportunity to take care of it
	- Luke 16:10 One who is faithful in a very little is also faithful in much, and one who is dishonest in a very little is also dishonest in much.
	- He came to realize the things he was given to steward, he wasn't taking care of very well.
	- The things God has blessed you with is an opportunity to steward
	- There is a difference between your conviction and your personality
- Faith
	- It is usually not a circumstance or situation problem, it is usually a faith problem
	- "Does God have X for me?" That very question is a highlight of a lack of faith
	- "I can't see it" but a conviction of faith says "I can see it, even if I can't see it"
	- Hebrews 11:1 Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen
	- If you could see a path forward, you wouldn't need faith
	- Faith is only required when you can't see it
