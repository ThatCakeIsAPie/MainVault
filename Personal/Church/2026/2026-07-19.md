# Rooted
## When Scripture Becomes Personal
### Caleb Little
Young adults are the age group that has the highest tendency to get lost
Colossians 2:6-7 Just as you received Christ Jesus as Lord, continue to live your lives in Him, rooted and built up in Him
We have moments where the Bible becomes real to us, not just something we read about, but the words jump off the page and become applicable in our very lives
James 1:5 If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to you.
If we approach God with an open heart, He gives generously to all
He wants to be a man known for his character and integrity
The thing he looked up to the most about his father is what he does when no one is looking
"I want to leave a legacy that outlives me"
A general epistle: a letter written to the average believer. James is one of those letters.
James didn't initially believe, despite being surrounded by the works of God and seeing all the miracles. That changed later in his life.
James 1:22-25 Do not merely listen to the word, do what it says. Anyone who listens but does not do what it says is like someone who looks at his face in a mirror and forgets what he looks like.
Hearers vs Doers
Who do you want to be?
"What do you do?" is a very common question, but we have to be careful about tying this to your identity.
*The person you want to be is on the backside of conviction.*
*God changes your life one conviction at a time.*
**Convictions lived out over time form your character.**
