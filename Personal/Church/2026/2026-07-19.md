Hearers vs Doers
Who do you want to be?
"What do you do?" is a very common question, but we have to be careful about tying this to your identity.
*The person you want to be is on the backside of conviction.*
*God changes your life one conviction at a time.*
**Convictions lived out over time form your character.**
One of the most common ways the Spirit talks to us is through conviction
What does the Holy Spirit convict us about?
1. Sin
2. Things that aren't beneficial for us
	1. There are things in our life that maybe aren't sin, but we still feel the Holy Spirit telling us to lay it down
3. Something God is asking us to do
When God convicts you to something, you have to grab it for yourself.
Conviction - A non-negotiable personal belief that influences choices or behaviors.
We all have that picture for our lives, but few of us embrace the procedure God uses, and that is conviction
