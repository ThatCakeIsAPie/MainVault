# Memorial Day Weekend
Family Confict in Genesis
- Abraham and his wife
	- Marriage challenges (Genesis 12:10-20)
- Abraham and his nephew
	- Entitled, unwise and needs help (Genesis 13-14)
- Isaac with his sons, Jacob and Esau
	- Favoritism and manipulation (Genesis 27-33)
- Joseph and his brothers
	- Abuse and deception (Genesis 37-50)
God doesn't say "If you blow it, I don't know you". *God worked in and through their conflict*
He works through your conflict, not in the absence of it
Why do we have so much conflict? Conflict is in us.
It is not about not having conflict, it is about can you repair after ruptures?
Proverbs 6:16, 19 The last thing of the seven things God hates is a person who stirs up conflict in the community
Why do we have conflict and tension in our homes?
*All conflict is a collision of expectations*
***Personal note: This is why communication of expectations is so important***
- We all face pressure.
Those who study this have a phrase for this: mimetic exhaustion. The pressure to mimic what others you admire have.
There is your perception of the expectation of others
Technology has allowed people to live alone in the same facility, but you aren't working together through conflict
- We want to get rid of the pressure
We want to make sense of this, while wresting with our emotions, all while not seeing our circumstances right
Cognitive bias, the most common form is confirmation bias
Desirability bias, what we want to be true versus what is actually true
What if conflict was the catalyst to not to turn to "experts", but to allow God into our hearts?
Galatians 5:17 For the flesh desires what is contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit what is contrary to the flesh. They are in conflict with each other, so that you are not to do whatever you want.
"Die to the flesh so you can surrender to the spirit of God"
"God, what are you trying to do or move in me?"
"Put off the old self" The old self says "everyone should listen to me and things would be better", but the new self says "God, how can I be like you? How can I be slow to anger and quick to forgive?"
Ephesians 4:25-27 Each of you must put off falsehood and speak truthfully to your neighbor, *for we are all members of one body*. "In your anger do not sin": Do not let the sun go down while you are still angry, and do not give the devil a foothold
*You intimidate your kids more than you think. They are nervous to talk to you. Do not punish them for telling you, honor them for telling the truth. You will be amazed how the dynamics of your home will change.*
"Dad, it is hard to talk to you, because you are always right." "I want to talk to you and explain how im feeling, but I don't have the words you do." "Thank you for being honest with me and telling me how you truly feel."
Some of us are more emotional than others
We can blow up a relationship by taking a 2 and turning it into a 10
There is such a thing as emotional regulation, taking a breath, time and space to process before responding. You want to react this way, but you aren't called to react this way.
2 Timothy 2:23-25 Don't have anything to do with foolish and stupid arguments, because you know they produce quarrels. Opponents must be gently instructed, int he hope that God will grant them repentance leading them to a knowledge of the truth.
***We have to trust that it is God who works in them and grants them repentance***
It is energy, it is a "pay me now or pay me later". Show up, not with having all the answers, but the heart of trusting God.
How do we deal with conflict?
1. Start with our internal conflict
Why do we feel the pressures we feel? Where does our internal conflict come from?
- Heart -> Head -> Words
Are you the person who stirs up fires, or puts them out? Where is that pressure coming from?
Proverbs 15:18 The one who is patient calms a quarrel
2. Ask God to speak to you about the situation
When is the last time you said "God, you know the situation" "Will you help me to slow down to trust in Your plan, Your purpose, see it Your way?"
"Will you do only what you can do?"
Personal trainers say you need more time under tension, for your muscles to break down to be built up stronger. Your family is similar, you need to face the tension and fight through it.
3. Fight for unity
You came placed into a family, dependent on relationships
We have the most individualistic relationships, and we are the most loneliest generation ever. We are so fixated on "me" and "where I am going"
Focus on others
Prioritize small family moments of connection every day:
- Physical touch
- Meals
- Laughter
- Conversation
- Notes (a simple text to your spouse saying "im thinking of you, im praying for you")
	- Show them that you are not just an embodiment of the rules that are in their way
- Traditions
	- Have something silly, like Taco Tuesday. Think they don't care? Miss it one time.
Be proactive, and think "God, what are you doing in me?"
You have to be willing to do the work and surrender
