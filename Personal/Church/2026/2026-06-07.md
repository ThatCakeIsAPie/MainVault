How does the Holy Spirit make God personal?
- Advocate/Comforter/Counselor/Helper
	- "He will give you another advocate to help you and be with you forever." -John 14:16
	- If you don't come to Him, you will come to something, and you won't like who you become from it
- Teaches and guides us into all truth
	- "But the advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things." -John 14:26
	- "But when he, the Spirit of Truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth..." -John 16:13
- Reminds us of everything Jesus said
	- "...and will remind you of everything I have said to you." -John 14:26
	- It is amazing how much you can remember, it imprints on your soul
- Convicts the world of sin
	- "When he comes, he will prove the world to be in the wrong about sin and righteousness and judgement." -John 16:8
	- It isn't about performing to God, but when is the last time the Holy Spirit said "whoa... stop"
	- Have you ever been talking, but not communicating? He went down a weird path, and the Holy Spirit said "you need to make that right"
	- Don't let your weakness become an opportunity for the flesh
	- You don't have to run your life in the ditch, He is the bumps when you are driving, the vibration strips.
- Gives gifts for the common good
	- "there are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit distributes them... Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good." - 1 Corinthians 12:4, 7
	- "I haven't seen God heal the sick" have you laid hands on the sick?
	- I know the Holy Spirit is working in the earth right now
- Gives us supernatural power
	- Acts 1:8 "But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."
	- It isn't about being holier, it is about a purpose
