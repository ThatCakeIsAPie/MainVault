# Legacy
So many kids give their lives to Christ at camp
I can partner with God, with His desires
*People who make a difference think generationally*
Kids often say that achievement is their parents' biggest focus. Parents are afraid of their kid "falling behind" or not being successful, because what does that say about you?
Psalm 145:4 One generation commends your works to another; they tell of your mighty acts
When it comes to passing on to the next generation, there is some scripture about David.
2 Samuel 24:18-25 "I will not sacrifice to the Lord my God burnt offerings that cost me nothing"
We don't give oxen to God, but when you have a right heart before God, you aren't looking for a right offer, because Jesus paid it, so you say "here I am"
David couldn't build the temple, so his son Solomon did. 2 Chronicles 3:1 The threshing floor where David built the altar comes back as Solomon used it/built it again
One generation gives, the next generation builds, future generations can worship
You never arrive in the kingdom of God, we constantly have the perspective of giving
Ephesians 3:20-21 now to Him who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine. To Him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations
God fills the gaps when you have the right heart
Whether it is your marriage, or kids, you can't control people, you can only offer your own right heart
The church ministers from the cradle to the grave
We are all here today because the generation before us sacrificed somewhere that we now build on
**What if?** Those 2 words consume real estate in our minds.
*Are we allowing what if to keep us stuck in yesterday, or are we allowing it to inspire us about tomorrow?*
Some kids are living in an algorithm driven version of the world that has them discouraged
It takes a certain mindset in your perspective to make it clear that this is not about you, but them
"What does it look like to live generationally?" What if I took an intentional step? What if my step mattered more than I realized? What if the next generation went further because I went first?
It is not about what they do, but who you become. The next generation is looking to you to emulate.
Start something you've been postponing. Stop something you shouldn't be continuing. Strengthen something you know matters.
We are not just one domino. That one domino seems insignificant, but when it all adds up, it becomes significant.
David set in place something he didn't know would be significant, or turn into the temple.
What if we saw God wanting to move in all these different places?
You want to see your kid in the picture? It takes a kingdom vision to begin to be radically generous to people you will never meet or know.
People who think generationally, they don't always think about things that is tied to what they could quantify.
Psalm 102:18 Let this be written for a future generation, that a people not yet created may praise the Lord
We love an eternal investment
The best thing to ask is "God, are you pleased? I just want to please you, God."
