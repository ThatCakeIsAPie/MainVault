# Mother's Day
Mark 5:24-34
John 4:50 The man took Jesus at His word
God loves your child even more than you do
Sometimes we hear God, know His promises, but we have to actually respond in faith, take Him at His word
Just about the time you think you have got it, you don't got it

Why is it hard to take God at His word?
- We're tired of waiting
- We're afraid
- We listen to outside voices
- We have wounds from our past
- We feel far from God
- We have insecurities
- We feel like we don't measure up
Why can we take God at His word?
- Because God sees our pain
She had arthritis, at church, the pastor randomly said "I feel like someone has arthiritis, and God wants to heal you". She rose her hand, they prayed over her, and the next day, it was completely healed.
God doesn't just want to get you through it, but to build your faith along the way
Dont turn your pain into bitterness, but purpose
- Because God cares about our problems
- Because God calls us to persistent faith
The woman didn't do a fancy prayer, she just had a massive amount of faith
Dont isolate when you face a challenge, lean in
You dont need less faith in down times, you need more of it
The woman was marginalized in society, but Jesus accepted her
