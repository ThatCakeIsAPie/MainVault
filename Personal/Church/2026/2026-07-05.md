# Rooted
## When Scripture Becomes Personal
Colossians 2:6-7
We don't get to choose if pain comes inot our lives, but we do get to choose who we become in the process of our pain
James 1:2-4
The context of James, he is writing to those who are being killed and hunted down
When he says "consider it pure joy" he is saying "consider it done". It is not really a question, but a statement.
The trials don't feel joyful, but take the posture of "consider it done". This is supposed to be our response of many kinds. *Pure* joy, complete joy.
We think we are a bit more mature in our heads than we actually are.
Perseverance comes from the testing of our faith. James 1:3
Testing in the ancient world means more like what the process of purifying silver is like. This is the same thing as what James talks about.
*The goal isn't necessarily to "pass" the test. It's to be purified by it.*
***Personal note: I think this is fascinating, because it reminds me of Leviticus when God said to test Him on tithing. Some translations say try Him, some say test, I don't know what is right, because Jesus said "Do not test the Lord your God"***
*You may not like what comes out during a trial or a test, but that is the point. God is after our hearts.*
The pastor had God say "can you trust me, and consider these trials pure joy" and he said yes, and he instantly felt peace. When you realize that, you realize that God is God and we are not.
Respond not our way, but God's way
How we respond to trials:
- Our Way
	- Isolate
		- We try to do it our way, and we only end up on our own
	- Control
		- When you are isolated, you start to try and control everything, fix everything yourself. The root of it all is fear.
	- Negative Self-Talk
		- You start to speak all these lies over yourself that aren't true. But you have said it over and over again but it just isn't true.
		- Proverbs 23:7 For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he...
		- All the ways he did it on his own, he felt even more pain.
- God's Way
	- Lean In
		- Let people pray for you, encourage you
		- One thing you will tell yourself is "they won't understand the level of pain I am feeling". Lean in anyway.
	- Trust
		- I'm trusting even when I don't understand.
		- Saying "God, you are still good and I'm going to trust you."
	- Declare God's Truth
