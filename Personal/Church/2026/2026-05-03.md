# Let's Talk Family
Family is not a social construct, it is actually something that God initiated
Parenting is so personal to us, even moreso than money
Parenting truths are univeral truths, if you lead a team, if you are a grandparent...
## Parenting the heart
Not the external behaviors, though that doesn't mean letting them do what they want to do
The bible calls to clean our heart, renew our heart
It means to guide your child's inner life through correction and encouragement, shaping what they love and believe so their life follows God's way
You dont want a kid just good at hitting a ball, but not knowing God. The chances they become a pro ball player is low, but the chances they get married and start a family and need God's direction is high
The fact that they will have to interact with life is high
"Their coach is terrible" They will probably have a terrible boss. They will have a life where you wont be able to micromanage all their life circumstances.
Deuteronomy 6:5 Love the Lord your God with all your heart
Matthew 22:36-37 It is not just the external rules, but to change your "want to"
Having a kid is an important season, from birth to 7 years old.
Discipline is love, actually "they will be disciplined by those who love them, or those who do not"
Love comes from God
If your kid is throwing a tantrum, the point isnt to draw out anger, but quick repentance
It is cute when they are throwing a fit when they are young, but not when they are 15
When someone thinks the world revolves around them, they are not great spouses
You have to help them find their identity in Christ, not what a junior high kid says of their clothes
Your kids are struggling with their own security, when they push you away, they dont know how to manage their emotions. Press in. Hug them when they don't want to be hugged.
***Pick the right battles***
1 Samuel 25:1-33
- David was chosen by God to be king; Saul tries to kill David
- David and his men are on the run in the wilderness
Try to be a reciprocator in your relationships
- David and his men protect Nabal's shepherds and large herds
- David prepares his men for battle
- Nabal's wife apologizes
1 Samuel 25:32-33 May you be blessed for your good judgement and for keeping me from bloodshed
We all have a tendency to pick the wrong battles when tensions are high
Ephesians 6:4 Fathers, do not embitter your children, or they will become discouraged
What NOT to be motivated by:
- Position
- Embarrassment
- Frustration
- Anger
- Hypocrisy
Modeling is the highest form of teaching
Philippians 1:9-10 discern what is best
Discernment is the spirit-informed ability to choose what aligns with God's Will
One of the gifts is the discerning of spirits
When you get a gut feeling about your kid's spirit, you are usually right. When you have that concern, that radar, get into their heart. That is usually God talking to you. Lean on Him to pick the right battles.
If anything shows you dont got this, it is parenting
The battle of Midway was the first time we had intel on the enemy, and it turned the tide.
What are the right battles to focus on?
1. The Battle for my Marriage
God clearly says He hates divorce, but he loves divorcees
Not "lets stay together for our kids" But "you are to lay down your life for your wife", and ladies "respect your husband the way he loves you"
2. The Battle for the Truth of God's Word
Will you take on the battle of a coworker's family life, while they give you advice of what to do?
Will you listen to a book where you dont know the fruit of their advice?
The bible is your foundation: does He really say that?
If it doesn't align with God's word, it isn't true
3. The Battle to get over Hurts and Wounds
We are all doing the best we can with what we have, but there are hurts and pains, overreactions. Be humble.
The self awareness alone will heal the balm, but put on the armor and prepare.
When your kids tell the story, it will be revisionist history. But when you say "im sorry", the healing from those hurts, when you have self awareness to recognize the hurts going on, you kill the enemies ability to ise it
4. The Battle for the Big Rocks
Dont overdirect their fear that you cant pay for their larger expense
All of the things we do that are a big deal. The big thing now isnt a big deal later on in life. You are letting your whole week get destroyed by something no one will care about 5 years from now.
If you put in the sand first, you wont get the big rocks in. But if you put the big rocks in first, you can get the sand in too.
How do I know if I'm winning?
- Help them love the God you love
You want them to have such an impact from it, that they leave being fueled by Him.
- Help them understand their purpose
- Help them understand how to be a good steward and handle resources
- Help them with the process of dating and marriage
Relationship move faster than ever before
It takes a while in person, but now, there is the ability to accelerate the relationship
5. The Battle for Influence
Sometimes, it is like "id rather not know"
If you are influential in the process, I am winning.
6. The Spiritual Battle
The fight is not with your teenager, but the evil that wants to take their future away from them
Start praying for their spouses, even when they are born
God can direct their paths far more than you can. Ask God for their spiritual battle.
