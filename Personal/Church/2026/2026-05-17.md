# Let's Talk Family
## Pastor Jim Laffoon
### Resting Place: Is God at Home in your House?
Isaiah 66:1 "Heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool. Where is the house you will build for me? Where will my resting place be?"
What are you going to build just for God?
He loves buildings, but because people come to worship Him. If you turn the church into a concert hall, He won't be in there the same way.
### The Restoration of Fellowship with God
- The Garden (Genesis 2) "God creates us to live in His presence"
- The Fall (Genesis 3) "Sin brings separation between man and God"
- Sacred Spaces "God meets with some people in certain ways"
	- It was never God's plan to have spectators
- Our Hearts (John 14:23) "Jesus promises He and His Father will make their home in us"
- The Church (Eph. 2:19-21) "God's people. God's household. God's temple."
	- God is here because this is where we worship Him
He doesn't just have to be here, but He can be in the home too.
The home of Abinadab:
- 20 years with the ark
- No mention of impact
- 3 sons - 1 died
- Don't hear from them again
How do you have the Ark of the Covenant for 20 years, and there is no good news?
The home of Obed-Edom
- 3 months - he and his entire household were blessed
- Obed-Edom goes with the ark to Jerusalem and serves as a gatekeeper for the ark and a musician.
- His 8 sons and 62 grandsons all become "leaders", "strong", and "very capable men."
How can we have the same? He isn't just in your heart, but in your home.
Are you experiencing that presence? Are you in the living word?
David put the Ark in the middle of the town square, and it blessed his people.
If I wanted to experience God in my house, how would I live?
2 Kings 4: 8-17 One person wanted to remodel his house for God and His servants to be happy in it. They added a guest house to the top of their house that only God could stay in. "If we remodel our house a certain way, God might stay there." Elisha came and saw the room, no one had ever remodeled their home for him. He asked God "what can be done for her?" The woman could not have a baby, and Elisha said "in one year, you will have a baby."
If you wanted to create space for God in your home:
1. Don't Grieve the Holy Spirit
	1. Ephesians 4:26-32 Don't grieve the Holy Spirit. Let all bitterness and wrath and anger and clamor leave you.
	2. Never go to bed mad. If you let your anger build up, it only worsens. The Holy Spirit hates clamor, hates disunity. We don't want the Holy Spirit to back off, we want Him to lean in.
	3. He was condescending towards his wife, he felt grief in his soul, God called him and told him that was the last time he would ever do that, next time He would just judge him. He ran back to his wife and apologized.
2. Don't Worship idols
	1. Exodus 20:4-5 Don't worship idols, otherwise it will affect the lives of yourself, your family, and your kids' kids.
	2. If there is anything in your life you put in front of God, it will affect your life.
3. Don't Ignore Him
	1. Isaiah 30:15-18
	2. When you have the sinking feeling in your gut, you can ask the Holy Spirit what is wrong.
Three Do's
- Do Build Unity in Your Home
	- He commands a blessing in your home. Blessing starts between the spouse.
	- Malachi 2:13-15 And what was the one God seeking? Godly offspring. So guard yourselves in your spirit, and let none of you be faithless to the wife of your youth.
	- Never go to bed mad, and don't let a day go by where God's presence isn't in it. A portion of the Holy Spirit comes into your relationship.
	- They have a spiritual bond that is unbreakable, because they have built something in the spirit. They have prayed thousands of times. They got too close to the spirit to fight.
- Do Worship God
- Do Wait on the Lord
	- If you only have devotions until your need is met, you won't have much.
	- Revelation 3:20 "Behond, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and eat with him."
Jesus had a favorite place on earth: The house of Lazarus and his two sisters
The core of their marriage is not attraction, it is spiritual. It is thousands of hours over 50 years just praying together.
*Don't raise your kids religiously, but relationally*
