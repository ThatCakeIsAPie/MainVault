Everyone wants to be the one who stands against evil, but human nature and history tells us different.
SEALs start training believing they will pass, while they are nice and dry. But once conditions change, they quit.
People come out of hell week looking like they were run over by a train. On crutches, heads swollen, coughing blood, and that is just those not in the hospital.
Many people think they can be the guy who stands alone, apart from everyone else doing wrong.
You wont be judged for the strength of your opinions when you arrive at the gates.
2 timothy 4:7 He didnt just keep the faith, but fought the fight
John 20:29 i want a life that demonstrates what i believe
Be a person of deep conviction
If i can see the next step, of course i can be certain of it. Anyone can.
Men confuse conviction with stubbornness and hype.
You can be convinced and wrong.
Conviction has to be built on something beyond your opinion.
People should be able to look at your life, and it will show the evidence.
# Four Pillars of Conviction
1. Knowing what is true
Without it, you are just opinionated.
A foolish man uses everything he knows to back up his opinions. A wise man seeks truth.
Proverbs 9:10
Wisdom begins with humility. You have to accept you don't know everything.
God is the authority, He is the truth. Truth is not an entity, it is a person, and His name is Jesus Christ.
Wisdom requires pursuing truth.
You cannot grow in Godly convictions while your understanding stays the same.
What work have you out in? Wisdom requires the pursuit of truth.
Once you get a revelation of who God is, you get one of who you are.
If you had a revelation of the truth, you would be chasing it.
There is a reason you have to keep chasing your leaders, telling them "i did something stupid"
Knowing scripture is not the same as doing it
Classroom time is important
If you were trying to shoot, he might give you books and youtube videos, you could do that for weeks, but once you hit the range, you will learn more in one day than in all those weeks.
If you have felt in the same place, it is because you have declined opportunities to live out what you have heard.
James 3:17
God is a Great Master.
Those who are rulers over little will rule over much.
Wisdom is a resource. God gives freely to those who are inclined to Him.
If God gives you resources, and you do not use them properly, what would that say about Him if He gives you more?
2. Discernment knowing what is right.
Wisdom without discernment is recklessness.
Scripture seems to counter itself, but it is both extremes of a principal.
You have a right to self defense, but also to turn the other cheek. It says both.
We are called to be bold, but it also requires restraint.
It is not that the principles counteract themselves, but requires you to have maturity to discern which is right in that circumstance.
Dont be the guy who is like "ive been in church all my life" but isnt growing
Hebrews 5:14 But solid food is for the mature, who by constant use have trained themselves to distinguish good from evil
You dont develop it by hearing alone, but by applying what you have learned.
You want to discover the boundaries in training. If you dont know the edge, you dont know how close you can get.
Scripture and the Holy Spirit will guide you in how to apply scripture in the moment
It will not guide you in how to accomplish your goals.
If you want the Holy Spirit to help you, it will help you in growing the kingdom of God, not your personal goals.
If you align your goals with God's, the answers become obvious.
The mature have trained by constant use.
3. Purpose; know what is worth standing for
People will show multiple reasons to suffer.
Your willingness to pay any price is not enough
You must know whether what you are pursuing is worth the cost.
Acts 20:24 my only aim is to finish the race and complete the task the Lord Jesus has given me
Do you know what you are willing to die for?
There is nothing ethically or morally wrong with a castle. No one cares about your castle, it is for you. If you want to build one, you need to pay for every brick.
It is expensive to build a castle when it is all about you.
In human history, you will find this mentality, and people die for something that doesnt matter
There are also dragon slayers, those who say "im going to cure cancer", the worst response is "i hope you succeed", but it is all up from there. All the way up to how people will leave what they are doing to help you.
If you are not willing to die in an instant, why would you spend the rest of your life dying for that thing? 40-60 years?
"Im not doing some cool business" Paul was a tent maker. There is only one way that works, and everything is about serving the kingdom of God.
Until you do this, you will not live with conviction. "Oh, this looks successful! This must be my purpose!" "Oh, success is getting further and further away, it must not be my purpose"
You are going to have to decide what you are going to die for.
There is obstacles between you and success
4. Courage - The willingness to act
Without the courage to act, conviction is nothing but a feeling
It is worth nothing unless you are willing to act on it
None of these things matter if you are not going to act
The three thrown in the fire told the king to throw them in the fire. "Even if God doesnt save us from it, we will die for Him."
Even if He doesnt save you from it, He is still God. If you cannot live that way, you cannot have conviction.
You have got to be willing to give up being ok, let go of the old you, you will not see the promised land of your life.

Obedience and small things. You need to be obedient in the small things, even if they seem like a big thing for you at the moment.
Let go of fear. Fear is not from God. Fear is from the deceiver.
*Release your desire for guaranteed outcomes*
A man of conviction doesnt need anyone to tell him how to live his life
Conviction is not on how loudly you preach, but in how you live
Decide you will be someone, be certain

Question for your group: 
Where in your life do you know what is right, but struggle to act on it and what is holding you back?
