# Joshua/Jericho - Part 2
There is a difference in a faith that is just set free and a faith when you decide to possess
When you are on the journey to possess, you face new kinds of challenges
There is always a group of people in every generation who say "I want everything"
What are my goals for you in this series?
1. Grow in your understanding of the bible... because I want you to know God
2. Recognize the God who is GREATER
3. Experience God's GREATNESS in your life
1 John 4:4 ...He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world
The same God in these stories is the one who is working in your life. It isn't dependent on us, but who He is.
Week one: God is greater than what stands before us.
Week two: God is greater than what limits us.
We like an open space, we like to not be limited. The question isn't if we are limited in our day and age, but if we are limited in how we can show up for our kids.
The question is "is there enough of me to go around for everybody"
"I know that God has given me this big vision, and if I had this, then I could get to that."
"If God's resources were unlimited, what would you do?" We superimpose our limitations onto God's plan for our life.
You are limited, you are human. But there is this God who wants to walk with you, who is unlimited.
There is a part of the group with Joshua who said "but there are these giants", while Caleb said "if God says it is ours for the taking, then it is our duty to take it"
He wrote in his bible "God, help me to not wander."
When God is moving, you can't hide it
Joshua 9 One group tricks Israel and makes a treaty
Joshua 10:1-5 The wicked king of Jerusalem hears about the treaty and rallies four other kings to attack Israel and their new ally.
Joshua 10:6 Do not abandon your servants. Come up to us quickly and save us! Help us, because all the Amorite kings from the hill country have come against us!
People who walk by possessing faith are not necessarily people who are never afraid, never perfect, but they keep moving forward.
Joshua 10:7-9 Joshua hears from the Lord "do not be afraid of them; I have given them into your hand."
He sometimes gets upset with himself, after all God has done, why is he giving fear a place?
Fear is a spirit, it can just sometimes come on you.
This story represents your own limitations, what is limiting you. They aren't about walls or time, walls have different emotion. The limitations make what feels necessary, you feel insufficient. Walls create limitation. Limitation creates anxiety and urgency.
Joshua 10:12-14 "Sun, stand still over Gideon, and you, moon, over the Valley of Aijalon" God doesn't correct him when he prayed a prayer to stop time.
"We shouldn't be getting people's hopes up" "When I'm down, pray for hope" Religious people will question the prayer, God doesn't.
Joshua 10:14 There hasn't been a day like it, when God listened to a human being.
Most Christians believe we are in a statistical danger that we believe in God too much.
"I am resting, I don't want to burn out" "Just try, we will let you know if you are in danger."
Statistical evidence shows you are more likely to pray too small of prayers.
God doesn't give us everything we want, but God gives us what we need to complete the assignment.
It is not our plan we are asking Him to bless.
The world tells us "we can do anything. You are amazing, trust yourself, find yourself. You, you, you." It's not true. We are limited by a ton of things. What do we do when we feel limited?
When you are possessing, you will hit barriers, but when you cross that barrier, you will ask "do I have what I need to make this happen."
- 1. Don't let fear stop you from moving forward.
- 2. Don't let your limitations convince you God won't provide what you need.
God would routinely tell him "what have I not provided to you today that you need?"
God loves the fact that you are so dependent on Him. The miracle of Jericho was great, but yesterday's miracles are not what will win today's battles.
A limited person is not powerless. People think that with limits means that is your life now, your life will never change, but confuse limits with powerlessness.
It is more blessed to give than receive.
We mostly hit our limitations when we feel like we have to figure out a solution to the problem.
Being generous allows God room to move in your life, you become a conduit. When you become that conduit, that is when generosity becomes transformational in their life.
God uses people sometimes as a conduit, God wants to show us that goodness all the time.
What do we do with a "sun stand still" prayer?
- 3. Pray a "Sun-stand-still" prayer.
The old testament is telling us something, there was another sun-stand-still moment. The sun didn't stop moving, but it stopped shining when Jesus was on the cross.
We don't have to be Joshua, we have Jesus Christ who bore our pain, who gives us the message it isn't about your ability to provide. Jesus makes intercession for us when we pray.
