# Unshakable Faith
The type of faith that cannot be bought. Men who will face the impossible.
We serve an impossible God, with Him, nothing is impossible. The "Impossible LIfe" is not impossible to us, but to the world.
Daniel 3:16 "We do not need to serve you under these circumstances. If you throw us in the fire, our God will save us. Even if He does not, we will not worship you or the golden statue you threw up."
It is not just "God will save us", but also "even if He does not"
God doesn't make bargains, He makes promises. He says "I'll be here, I'll pick you up".
Faith does not need a contract
The three men knew God could save them, but their obedience was not conditional on their deliverance
God doesn't make deals, your obedience cannot be conditional, you cannot say "ill obey as long as you protect me"
Know who you will be at the end
That is the difference between ordinary faith and "even if" faith.
Ordinary, conditional faith says "I trust God as long as my business is healthy, the outcomes make sense." Even-if faith says "even if the business fails, God is still good and He has a great plan. Even if I don't understand and it costs me everything, I will not bow."
3 Keys to Unshakable Faith:
1. Know God
You cannot have unshakable conviction based on something that is constantly changing
You want to have that heroic conviction, it cannot be based on your own abilities
There are tough guys who maybe dont break for a while, but they all break
The only way to have that ultimate conviction is to have that faith that is unbrakable
2 Timothy 1:12 That is why I suffer as I do. But I am not ashamed in Whom I believe.
Paul is not saying I am suffering, but that is ok because I will be ok in the end. He is saying even if in the end, it isn't, he is saying it is ok because of Whom he belives.
If we could have it our way, if He showed us the movie, we would say God is a liar because we are like "there is no way my circumstances could end up there, God must be mistaken"
If you have ever had certainty that is not from God, and your circumstances your certainty goes out the window. It will not go away if it is in God.
Daniel 11:32 There is a direct connection between knowing God and living courageously
It says that those who know God will live courageously
Peace doesn't come from knowing what will happen, but what you will do
The three didn't say "God will save us from the fire", but "He is good, no matter what"
God is not going to tie your shoes for you
If something does happen, just accept it. Get some perspective, your life is great. Your circumstance is not unrecoverable.
2. Know who you are
Romans 8:16 The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God
God doesn't have grandchildren or step-children, we are all children of God
Romans 8:17
With the three in Daniel, they were talking to the most powerful man in the world, but the king had no power over their authority
Understand Who gave you your identity
Your business can be taken away, your house, your job, anything can be taken away from you. But if your identity is in God, than it cannot ever be taken away.
You are called to have this type of faith.
When you know who you are, other people's opinions stop mattering. You aren't afraid of failure.
If you are constantly bargaining with certainty, it will only get harder
"I've never lost a fight to an angry person, because I know what they will do"
When you bargain with certainty, you keep diminishing your position as it gets harder, and eventually you are in shackles.
*The other side is "i don't care if I fail" not to be arrogant, but because you know who God is and who you are.*
If you aren't willing to go into the fire, you will back down.
Resilience is more than the ability to endure suffering
This isn't a SEAL discipleship program, not everyone is willing to pay that price, and it isn't always required either
If you want to do anything in life, you are going to have to learn how to suffer. Life either gives you pain, or you choose to accept it.
True resilience is the ability to trust God in the fire
"Tough guys" without true resilience will get angry and blame you for their downfall
3. Live your conviction
You can be successful, but success isn't conviction
You are successful, but you didn't have faith through the whole thing
Faith without works is dead. Works without faith is vanity.
Whatever it is you would like to achieve, and you didn't trust God in it, it will eat you up from the inside out.
You don't want to get to the end of the road, and watch the trophy turn to ash, because it wasn't how God called you to live.
True faith produces obedience that is not dependent on the outcome
Difference between faith and opinion is that faith will cost you something.
The men in the fire didn't know God would rescue them, but they knew that they would not bow.
We don't need any more Christians with Jesus preferences. A preference is something you hold until it costs them something. Conviction is something you will hold onto even if it costs you everything.
We need mighty men with conviction.
Conviction is not the absence of fear, but conviction and faith in spite of fear
When you know who God is, you know who you are. When you live with truth, you don't get conviction in 5 minutes, it is a lot of bricks get stacked up for living in truth for a long time.
When you know who you are, you develop conviction. When your faith is stronger than your circumstances, you are unshakable.
