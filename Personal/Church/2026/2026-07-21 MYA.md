## Expectancy
*God meets us at the level of our expectation*
*The expectation you bring to God is the level of your preparation*
*Expectation without preparation is just a good intention*
The preparedness you come to is how God meets us in our midst
Our expectations can become increasingly vague
*You prepare for the things you care about. Job interviews, client meetings, etc. It is tied to your passion and care for how you lead your life.*
If it is something you are passionate about, it isn't as hard for you
If you feel like it is hard to hear God in your life, it usually comes from a motivation, an expectancy that God isn't going to move in your life
