# Summer Nights
## Expectancy
*God meets us at the level of our expectation*
*The expectation you bring to God is the level of your preparation*
*Expectation without preparation is just a good intention*
The preparedness you come to is how God meets us in our midst
Our expectations can become increasingly vague
*You prepare for the things you care about. Job interviews, client meetings, etc. It is tied to your passion and care for how you lead your life.*
If it is something you are passionate about, it isn't as hard for you
If you feel like it is hard to hear God in your life, it usually comes from a motivation, an expectancy that God isn't going to move in your life
### What are you expecting?
Joshua 3:5 Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do amazing things among you
If you read in Exodus and Deuteronomy, they look around for where God is taking them. They lose faith, they complain and curse God. God says "because you have not been faithful, you will not see the promise land"
*You can be set free from bondage, and not be stepping into the promises God has for you*
They were standing on the edge of the bank, and they can see the promise land God has for them. The bible says the river was at flood stage, so they can't get across.
Joshua 3:5-8 "When you reach the edge of the waters, go stand in it." the water upstream stopped flowing, and the people holding the Ark never stepped foot in water, but dry ground
Consecration - setting yourself apart for God
There are times we are called to set ourselves apart.
Many have an opportunity to participate in what God is calling us to do. Preparation = participation
If God has a miracle for you, He always ensures you have a part in making it happen. He invites us to have an involvement in the process.
The opposite of preparation is apathy oftentimes. The strategy of the enemy is for you to become apathetic.
One of the challenges is you approach these big moments, and lean into apathy
God meets you in the preparation
***Personal note: Preparation is essentially faith made real. Noah showed his faith by building the ark for all of those years. Building before you need it is having the faith that it will come to pass. You prepare, you just don't know exactly when it will happen.***
1. Write it down!
	1. Move from a general hope to a specific implementation. Do that, you will see God will move in those ways.
	2. Positioning is showing up. Position yourself to receive something from God.
	3. Preparation is your faith
	4. He wrote down things that seemed crazy, seemed simple.
	5. He doesn't go into his year aimless. God meets us in our level of preparation.
	6. Preparation begins before you can see it.
	7. To write "God, I'm believing for a spouse" seems scary. Trusting God seems like dangerous territory. "What if He doesn't meet me there?" better question "What do I have to lose?"
	8. Just sticking the pen to the paper, you will be surprised to see your ability to trust Him
	9. *Specific prayer doesn't control God's response, it clarifies our dependence*
		1. It is saying "I can't do this on my own, this specific thing i'm trusting you on"
	10. The reason we don't do that is because we are afraid of being let down? It doesn't manipulate God.
2. Make room
	1. We have to make room for God. Clear some things out.
	2. How desperate are you? Desperation always equals the level at which God will meet you.
	3. Let go of some things, don't hang out with some types of people, listen to different music, make room for God to speak to you.
	4. You do have to ask "when is the last time that a piece of scripture spoke directly to you"
	5. Is there any time in your life, where if God wanted to speak to you, He could?
	6. Creating opportunities for the Holy Spirit to speak to you, drive without listening to anything
	7. Maybe you need to fast, give Him the entire day. Clear some things out, get rid of distractions, let Him speak to you.
	8. Your level of hunger and care and desire is directly proportional to the level God will speak to you
3. Obey
	1. Most of the time, you aren't confused about what to do. It is a "am I going to trust God? Am I going to obey Him?"
	2. It is not about the sacrifice, but it will be shown through your obedience.
	3. The first step is obedience when you don't see it
	4. He is always looking for your willingness
	5. Your responsibility is not to have it all figured out, but to choose to obey
If you want to be a person of faith, get specific with it
If I am going to do it, I am going to air on the side of faith
