# Summer Nights
## Expectancy
*God meets us at the level of our expectation*
*The expectation you bring to God is the level of your preparation*
*Expectation without preparation is just a good intention*
The preparedness you come to is how God meets us in our midst
Our expectations can become increasingly vague
*You prepare for the things you care about. Job interviews, client meetings, etc. It is tied to your passion and care for how you lead your life.*
If it is something you are passionate about, it isn't as hard for you
If you feel like it is hard to hear God in your life, it usually comes from a motivation, an expectancy that God isn't going to move in your life
### What are you expecting?
Joshua 3:5 Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the Lord will do amazing things among you
If you read in Exodus and Deuteronomy, they look around for where God is taking them. They lose faith, they complain and curse God. God says "because you have not been faithful, you will not see the promise land"
*You can be set free from bondage, and not be stepping into the promises God has for you*

Joshua 3:5-8 "When you reach the edge of the waters, go stand in it." the water upstream stopped flowing, and the people holding the Ark never stepped foot in water, but dry ground
Consecration - setting yourself apart for God
There are times we are called to set ourselves apart.
Many have an opportunity to participate in what God is calling us to do. Preparation = participation
If God has a miracle for you, He always ensures you have a part in making it happen. He invites us to have an involvement in the process.
The opposite of preparation is apathy oftentimes. The strategy of the enemy is for you to become apathetic.
One of the challenges is you approach these big moments, and lean into apathy
God meets you in the preparation
