## Power that Reaches People
Acts 1:8 You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you, and you will be my witnesses
Power and boldness in our lives is for reaching people
How Do I Approach This?
1. It's all about the heart!
Proximity always leads to passion
The closer you are, the more passion it builds
Get closer to Jesus, it always leads to passion for what He cares about
Serving always tests your heart
It often isnt celebrated when you go the extra mile. It is between you and God.
It isnt to gain anything from it, it is for other people
Ephesians 4:1 I urge you to live a life worthy of the calling you have received
2. Depend on the Holy Spirit
It is not about doing spiritual things, but about yielding to the Holy Spirit
When you yield, it gives Him room to work in your life
The boldness of the Holy Spirit goes beyond personality
3. See it by Faith
We spend most of our time worried and thinking about having faith for ourselves, but we could grow in having faith for somebody else
