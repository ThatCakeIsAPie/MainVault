# Authentic
## A Series on Relationships
Find your people. Find your purpose.
From the time you are young, you are trained to think about what you are going to do. But God is less concerned about what you do, and more about the who.
It is less about the job and more about the people He wants you to meet
Moses and Aaron, Moses gives God excuses, but God gives him Aaron to speak for him.
Ruth and Naomi
David and Jonathan, David's calling is enhanced because of his relationship with Jonathan
Elijah and Elisha
Jesus and the 12 Disciples, it was about the people that God was building with him
God made all these things and said they were good, but the first thing He said was not good was man to be alone. We are meant to be in life-giving relationships
In the book of Ruth, to be married is your safety net
Ruth 1:16-18
We are all looking for significance out of life. We all want to live that way, we just dont know how to get there. If you search for it, you will get frustrated.
He neglected friendships along the way because he didnt think they were as important as his work
What keeps me from having good friendships?
- Insecurity
God adopts us into His family
He affirms you, no relationship will replace that
Insecurity makes you believe you don't belong
You believe when you are a part of it, you don't belong
You start to sabotage those relationships
It all starts and ends with identity
When I feel confident and secure in who I am, I dont have to find it in others
*When you are secure, you add value. When you are insecure, you take score.*
Secure people always back away from insecurity like that
Security means you walk into the relationship asking how you can serve, not be served
- Isolation
A move, job change, relationship that ended, it bubbles up insecurity. It didnt create it, but it brings it up
He would sabotage his relationships
You never find someone who is insecure who says "let me dig into this"
If insecurity is plaguing you, and you never engage, you will continually be frustrated for a position you put yourself in, you are not in kindergarten, you are an adult now. You have to initiate and serve someone else.
You can be mean, but if you can initiate and sell something, you can always find a job
Friendships in your life are the biggest litmus test of your insecurity. If you have a perpetual cycle of disappointment in your friendships, see if you seek affirmations from it
- High Expectations
Offense is the biggest robber of our relationships
At some point, there is going to be a conflict, but most people arent secure enough to push through the conflict
Adults dont really have best friends, it is the wrong expectation. If you have one, it is challenging.
Overdefining friendships can cause problems
Being too close can cause a problem when one gets married
If you are looking for a best friend, that is the wrong expectation
When you overdefine the friendship, you set it up for failure
Dont set up such a high expectation someone else cant fulfill
Ask instead "How can I serve this person? How can I love them?"
The people loving and serving never hurt for friends
People pleasers dont know how to have honest conversations with people, they dont have depth
People pressers make everything a court case. Everything becomes a challenge.
I have a relationship with Jesus, I dont set crazy expectations with others, I just love and serve people.
You overinflate the relationships of others, and underinflate yours
You can start to look at other relationships and think "I want that", but you dont know what goes into that
- Selfishness
If it isnt about serving the other person, it wont play out well
Are you really in the relationship to add value to them, or what they can do for you?
Most all relationships are not receiving, it is mostly giving and serving
Most friendships and relationships are not hanging out all the time, but being there when they need each other
It isnt the job, the money, but the people
