# Vision Weekend
It is easy to start out with a vision, but sustaining it is difficult
Where there is great power, is the ability to sustain great vision over a prolonged period of time
*To sustain vision is to not keep it complicated*
*If you want to sustain vision, join God where He's working*
***It isn't about ability, but availability***
There is something larger than you that sustains your circumstances
Vision can elude us
Vision can leak
There is so much in our world that is so flashy and pretty, we can easily let our eyes drift lower, lower to this temporal existence that is fading fast
The bible says "lift up your eyes, look higher"
***Reminds me of a quote of "You can only look down on someone if you believe yourself to be higher than them"***
Jesus encounters a lame man just laying there. He asks him "do you want to get well?"
How often do we miss God's desire for us because we are too "busy" looking too low
Those who sustain vision are usually the unlikely candidates. Those who are the "likely" candidates think they are too smart for God. They will think He is wrong because He did it on "the wrong day"
*Don't surrender to religious spirit, because you will miss the point.*
John 5:17, 19 "My Father is always at work to this very day, and I too am working" "he can do only what he sees his Father doing, because whatever the father does the Son also does."
It doesn't matter what your barriers are, God is always working
5 Questions We Need to Ask - To help us keep growing in vision
- How do I see God?
	- Vision originates with God, but He shares it with us
I hope you maintain this childlike, giddy-like "Oh my, God is working"
*Curiosity is a sign God is working in your life*
***We have so much hype, we are so hard to impress***
- Where do I find help?
	- A pastor helps connect you to God's vision
Part of what a pastor does is when they preach, it can connect the word to your life
A shepherd recognizes that those things you veer off towards will leave you feeling unfulfilled
- How do my relationships and my church influence this?
	- Vision is not primarily taught, but it can be caught. 1 Peter 2:5
The church is not in the periphery, but the center of what God does
We live in a rugged individualism society. You don't get saved by a group, but a person decision. 1 Peter 2:5 "You also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house to be a holy priesthood"
If you are disconnected, there is an element of your ability seeming lacking because you are disconnected from your whole.
You can't build a church on this ecclesiastical nature, that some people are "important people"
You were created to work with the Father who is working in the earth. Everyone has a part to play.
- How does this integrate with my daily life?
	- God's vision includes my gifts, my job, and my relationships
*Frustration is to have a bunch of passion about something you are doing nothing about*
If you don't know why you are doing it, what you are trying to accomplish, you will eventually quit.
If God's vision is something you touch periodically, it eventually wanes. What if you start to see your work life, your family, all of it as integrated with God's doing?
- What do I do practically?
	- Seek first His Kingdom - Matthew 6:33
	- See where God's moving and join Him
"If God can get it through you, He can get it to you"
*John 5:20 "For the Father loves the Son and shows Him all He does."*
I am more excited about what God is doing now than He was in the past. The quickest way to lose vision is to only talk about what God used to do.
Remember when you were hot for God's vision?
What if it wasn't just about us? What if we show it to the next generation?
His pastor let him preach at 16, he let him preach on the same stage that the pastor preaches on. The transference.
In today's world, showing is more important than telling
