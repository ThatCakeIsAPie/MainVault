# Summer Nights
*God meets us at the level of our expectation*
## Are You Listening?
1 Samuel 3:1-10 Samuel kept hearing the voice coming from the Ark when he was lying down. Eli was confused, but realized that the Lord was calling to him, and told him to ask back "Here I am, Lord, your humble servant"
When you read the Old Testament, you will find that there are unique characters and God uses them in His Plan
You can position yourself to receive from God. When you position yourself, God will meet you there.
There is a recipe to your heart and how to approach God, and what God draws close to
2 things God draws close to/What do we bring?
1. Reverence (our heart posture)
2. Obedience (Our response)
If we are not careful, we can become casual with God, but God will never draw close to a place where He is not revered
There is a difference between when you know something and you don't
There is a difference between knowing what God has asked you to do, and not knowing
Often, it is something simple that God has asked you do. "I am doing good in all these areas" There is a difference between not knowing and knowing but living in disobedience.
It doesn't have to be big and serious, but it is real and something God is pressing you on.
If you feel like God has been distant, go back to the last thing God talked to you about
