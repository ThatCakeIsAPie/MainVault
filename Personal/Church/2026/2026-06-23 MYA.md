John 21:15-22 "You must follow me" The passage is so humanistic, that everything He has done for Peter, Peter denied Him three times, but Jesus still reached back out to him. And yet, Peter immediately turned to John and asked why he couldn't have what John has.
3 Ways this scripture encourages us to live more securely:
1. Celebrate Others
	1. Starts with a spirit of gratitude
	2. We downplay what God has done for us in our lives
	3. It is easy to celebrate others when you recognize what God has done in your life
	4. "God, you did all these things for me that I did not deserve, you don't owe me anything." That is the spirit to carry. Do that, you will recognize what God is doing in others
	5. "He who is refreshed, refreshes others"
	6. Go be a blessing for someone else. Let's publicly celebrate others, for we don't serve a zero-sum God!
2. Live on Mission
	1. *The best way to battle insecurity is to focus on what God has called you to do*
	2. If you get so consumed with what God is doing in your life, you don't even have time to be concerned about what others are doing or want you to do.
	3. Jesus uses the phrase "follow me" 21 times in John. This is not a one time thing and you are done. It is a constant thing, a consistent pursuit, get excited in what God is doing in your life.
	4. If you are focused on "woe is me", it is going to be hard to do much. But if you can focus on the blessings and what God is calling you to do, life gets far more magical.
	5. Get on fire from the things that God commands in your pursuit of Him
3. Trust God's Timing
	1. This is the hardest of the three
	2. There is a tendency to want to know. The when, the how, the what. But you will be frustrated if you go looking for those.
	3. 2 Peter 3 "For the Lord, a thousand years is like a day. For us, a day is like a thousand years."
	4. We have to trust God's timing, that it is for our best, even if we don't see it that way.
	5. When we fall behind, we feel the need to take matters into our own hands.
