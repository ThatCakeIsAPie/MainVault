## Rich Wilkerson
John 3:16-21
### Light Lover and Dark Dwellers
Or
### Don't Let the Old Man In
Clint Eastwood said the secret to staying young is to not let the old man in
*You are saved by grace through faith*
You have not just been saved from sin, hell and God's wrath, but you have been saved for a purpose, a mission, a calling.
Sanctification is the process of becoming more and more like Jesus, you will spend the rest your life becoming more like Jesus
You are not perfect, but you are being perfected
I have to war against my flesh to prevent the B.C. Lyle from coming back in
Jesus accepts Nicodemus even in the dark, He took him as he was
It is in the dark we have questions
Questions start showing up in the night
Negatives are developed in the dark, but so many of us live in the dark for so long, all we have are questions
"If you want to be a part of My Kingdom, you have to be born again"
"You either live in the light, or love the dark"
*A personal relationship with Jesus doesn't mean a private relationship with Jesus*
You cant walk in freedom carrying the old man
*"I got a good dog and a bad dog in me. Which one wins? The one I feed the most."*
Your Instagram feed will indicate which one you feed
***The heart wants what you feed it***
There are believers, deceivers, but there are no such things as in-betweeners
Maybe you need to be born-again, but that old man keeps popping back up in your life
As you grow with Christ, things change
1. Change the Appetite
In just a 3 day window of a fast, he changed his appetite
When you develop an appetite for the light, you wont have a desire for sin
Change your vocabulary, get hungry for God's word
14 days into a carnivore diet, you enter into ketosis, it ends fueling itself from carbs and instead from fat. Stay in it for 24 hours, it feels like jet fuel.
2. Set the Boundaries
Winners and losers have the same goal in life
You dont rise to the occasion, you fall to the level of your training
The Holy Spirit releases you and restricts you, those are what He does.
He gives you standards your friends dont. You arent being legalistic for obeying the Holy Spirit.
A man brought in a snake, took care of it, and it bit him. He asked why, it said "you knew I was a snake when you brought me in"
*Sin happens at the intersection of desire and opportunity. If you cant control it, remove the opportunity*
A thief doesn't care how many locks you lock, as long as you leave one open
You have a right to light
3. Create the Momentum
What God starts in you, He will bring to completion. What God starts, He finishes.
Momentum is less effort, more speed
If you have been born again, everything is different. I dont walk, talk, think like I used to. I have a supernatural strength come upon me.
As children of God, we dont do it so He will love us, we change because God loves me so
"For God so loved the world, so He gave His only Son"
The word "world" In Greek means "cosmos"
James 2:19
You are not loved, you are loved so
When you surrender yourself to the light, He is going to interrupt you and intercept you from the dark
