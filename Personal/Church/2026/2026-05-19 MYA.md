Paster Caleb and Emily-

*Is the bible your authority? Are you trying to align God's Best for your life, in dating, marriage?*
The starting place of it all is this
God said multiple things were good, but the first thing He said was not good was for man to be alone
Genesis 2:18 God has someone for you. If you are in the season of singleness, know that God has someone in your heart for you.
Men and women are equal in value, but different in function
The role of a husband is would you lay your life down for your wife as Christ laid His life down for the church? Your role is to love, serve and lay your life down.
The role of a wife is to respect him and be a helper for him. You aren't standing behind him, but next to him.
The world says "if you are falling out of love, just move on". God says "What He put in motion, no one shall pull apart"
God's design is actually a picture of eternity. A covenant is for two to become one, a picture of eternity.
"This is what God blesses, and we want to help you be there"
When people are single, in their insecurity, they can start to overly identify with that. Do you want to be married? Then why do you say you feel called to be single?
Marriage is a good thing, it is ok to desire that. Just make sure your confession in your season is aligned with God's best
1. Girl POV: How upfront/bold should you be about liking someone?
It is from God in your heart for a guy to pursue you
"Im sitting here every week, what do I do?"
Do you have someone in your life to tell you no? They wouldn't have the life they have now if they didn't have the people around them to tell them yes and no.
She used to be unapproachable. Don't be afraid to hang out in groups, it isn't weird or odd to do that.
Guy POV: Don't make it awkward.
Girls get coaching to be approachable, because many guys are insecure about doing it
A guys job is to pursue
Tori never had to guess that Ty liked her
If we are making it confusing, that is on us as guys. Be clear about your intentions. Don't make it awkward. It is ok to be in intermingle circles with people.
2. How do you know in the talking stage when you should proceed to a dating relationship?
Three stages:
- Is this somebody I'm interested in getting to know better and pursuing?
Especially in this age group, it can get kinda high school-ish, there doesn't need to be gossip and drama
- Dating: Is this the person God has for me to marry?
It isn't "do we enjoy each other? Do we find each other hot?"
- Once you decide, get to marriage quickly.
Once you know, you should get married as soon as possible. The alternative is not good.
If you spend two years with someone, questioning it, it is not the person.
Tori didn't care about the napkins at the wedding, majoring over the minors, she just wanted to be married to Ty.
People extend their relationships because they are "trying out" marriage
It is better to get married than to burn
When you elongate that season, you can open the doors to the enemy to attack
The shorter you can keep the engaged season, the more fruitful it can be
Don't major on the minors and minor on the majors
The dating season is so significant, because you are trying to find out. Just make sure you have a good vision for your future spouse so the person you find aligns with that.
The engagement season, the only thing on your mind should be just planning the wedding.
3. How do I tell the difference between a red flag and a growth area?
The biggest thing is "are they teachable? are they coachable?" or are they like "im not changing"
If you don't have that heart posture of changing, you don't have authority in your life. If you are humble, you can grow
- Character
How is it going on in your heart? What is God doing in your heart?
The guy and the girl need to be on the same trajectory and direction
- Calling
What you are called to and what they are called to, those have to come together
- Chemistry
Do you want to rip their clothes off and be with them all the time?
There is an ease to it, a flow to it. It shouldn't be too difficult.
*You need all three*
We can tend to put it in a box to satisfy our desire for marriage
When single, the only thing you can really work on is character.
You want to character reference and check them
If you feel you have initial chemistry, but they have poor character and you are justifying a character related thing, then that is a red flag. Seasonal language, spiritual language, etc.
4. Where is the line physically in a dating relationship (outside of sex), and is there a defined one or up to the conviction of the couple?
It isn't the right question, it is asking "what can I get away with?" "How close can I get to being an adulterer while still honoring God?"
Ask the question "what barriers do I have to set up to follow Jesus more?"
If you asked him the question, he would press on your heart. See how far away you can get away from this line.
Purity is a good thing, a massive blessing in your life. It is not only for you, but a legacy for your children.
They would not be together without having another person in the room.
*You can do it, but you have to be 100% intentional in this space*
If you feel like you have some guilt and shame in this space, you can honor God right now.
God gives you everything you need that pertains to Godliness
God will honor your commitment to that
5. How do you keep it fresh when you're married?
Dating doesn't stop when you are married.
Ephesians 5 says for wives to submit to their husbands out of honor and respect
It helps you create a rhythm inside your house
If your starting place ends up being the perspective of the covenant of marriage, you can be grateful when your spouse ends up texting you that they are praying for you today.
The bible says for wives to encourage their husbands, not to be his accountabili-buddy. It is better to honor him in a desert than to have a naggy wife
As a guy, do you have any authority in your life?
Have people to hold you accountable. He has people who can call him when he is about to do something who tell him he wont do something, and he says "yes sir"
A wive's perspective is "no one else is doing it, I have to", but their calling is to submit anyway and that he is to do it. To pray for your husband.
