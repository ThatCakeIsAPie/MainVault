# Rooted
## When Scripture Becomes Personal
Colossians 2:6-7 As you therefore have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith, as you have been taught, abounding in it with thanksgiving.
*It is a person you are following, not a thing.*
People who don't go into their root system, they become uprooted
Isaiah 37:31 And the remnant who have escaped of the house of Judah shall again take root downward, and bear fruit upward
***Personal Note: I love the phrasing of that. You must take root downward in order to bear fruit upward.***
Acts 20:7-12 A story of Eutychus. In a church full of disciples, they don't call them a disciple, but a young man. The young man was not rooted, he fell asleep in church, fell three stories and died, Paul went down and held him, said "Do not trouble yourselves for his life is in him". When they brought him in alive, they were not a little comforted.
God doesn't want a little comfort, He wants a lot of it. God is not a God of little, but a lot, it all, complete.
When you are not rooted in the house of God, you are full of distractions.
When you live a life without roots, you are without accountability.
We all need someone to poke us, to encourage us, to provoke us to good works. We join a workout club to have someone go "Where are you? We miss you."
For Eutychus, he was there, listening to Paul for days, weeks, months, years, but the gospel never left him. You have to go deeper in hearing.
*Go into the scripture and ask God to speak to you personally with His voice. Don't just read the word, but meet the Man.*
Acts 13:26-28 Many sat in scripture in Jerusalem, but never met Jesus. You can be in the letter, but without the Spirit, it becomes law. Lifeless, fruitless. ***Personal note: This one is huge, it really explains how policing behavior without addressing the heart just becomes tyranny, every time.***
You don't mess with God. You either go all the way, or don't go at all.
He heard the voice of God one day, God said "Today is the best day of your life". He got the call that his dad had a heart attack. He lost his earthly father, but gained the reality of a Heavenly Father that day. At the funeral, people said his dad said that his son would become a preacher. Wouldn't you know it.
When God whispers to you, speaks to you, He is going to give you enough to run on for eternity.
The only second time he heard Him, it was to build a church in Austin. He spoke to his wife, and she had also heard the whisper to build a church in Austin.
God is simply looking for people who will say "God, here I am, send me."
*Ask "God, if you can send that man, send me.*
If a man can fly F-18s, save America, but lose his soul, it is for naught.
God has a plan for you, wants you to go deeper.
Hebrews 3:7, 12-14 "Today, if you will hear His voice..." Many translations miss the word "will" in there. It is that He is speaking, you just have to listen. When He speaks to you, your faith comes alive. When scripture becomes personal. His primary way of speaking to us is through His word.
Anyone can see when someone is not real.
When God speaks, there's provision.
When God speaks, doors open.
When God speaks, there's a price to pay.
Philippians 3:14 I press toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus
It is not a reward, but a gift. The gift is to hear God say "well done"
