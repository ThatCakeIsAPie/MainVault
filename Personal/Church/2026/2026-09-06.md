## Next Gen Weekend
***Psalm 127:1 Unless the Lord builds the house, they labor in vain who build it*** Personal note on this, this is how I feel about Faleth Capital. I feel like it wasn't really me who developed the system, but the Lord.
Psalm 127:3-4 
Next Gen Weekend isn't just about the next generation of pastors, but the next generation in general
He wanted to make his dad proud for so long, but it wasn't until he brought him into church and let Him work, that he got to that end.
Church and ministry is an apprenticeship model
Don't allow your kids to be busy in things that don't matter, not just externally, but internally
What matters is what is going on in their heart
How is their heart?
Psalm 92:13 Planted in the house of the Lord, They will flourish in the courts of our God.
It isn't just hanging around the garden, but getting planted
There is nothing that can replace being consistently around the environment
Just stay consistent, stay authentic. The more you are, the more it will spill onto your kid.
He is working in them more often than you are sometimes, you are going to be amazed at what they can do.
*God is writing your kid's story*
