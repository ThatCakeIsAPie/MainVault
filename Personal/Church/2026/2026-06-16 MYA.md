# CTRL
## A series on Transition
### Who's Really in Control?
Maybe you aren't where you want to be in your life, but it can often lead to you trying to take control
The area we struggle in the most tends to be in calling and purpose
Life transition highlights what and who you trust
The starting place to being successful in life, is to make Jesus the source of your life. If we aren't careful, we can let something else become our source, you become dependent on it.
*The one thing tested in your season of uncertainty is your identity*
A season of change doesn't create, it just reveals what is inside of you
When you are insecure and you treat someone else incorrectly. The pressure just revealed where you are at all along.
*You aren't pleased with how you react when pressured? You have to go back and make Jesus the source of your life.*
What is the question when you grow up? What do you want to be.
The question of "what do you want to do?" always seems to come up in our lives, because we make it our identity.
*It has less to do with what you do and instead who you are. Jesus doesn't care what you do.*
What you say to those closest to you reveals what Jesus wants to heal in your life
Proverbs 3:5-6 Trust in the Lord with all your heart... lean not on your own understanding
We can put our trust in anything, but when we put it in Jesus, He makes our paths straight.
There is a season where purpose and calling is exciting as it seems endless. But it can also create so much anxiety because it gets overwhelming.
People always start from a place of "what is the easiest thing for me?"
*You have to choose your hard* God will grace you on whatever you choose
Romans 12:3-8 Think not of yourself more highly than you ought to think, but according to the measure of faith that God afforded to you. We are one body in Christ, with many parts of the body. There are many giftings and many callings. If you are a servant, serve with humility. If you are a leader, lead with zeal. Run your race and win it, let others run and win their races.
*Calling is not about forcing God ot bless the path you picked. Calling is about surrendering enough to let God show you the path He designed.*
God doesn't want to clean up the leftovers, but be the One who designed it.
*You can make it work with anyone, at any job. But God has a plan for you, a redemptive purpose. He has a perfect person for you, a perfect job.*
