# Game Finish Bridge Strategy (2026)

**Context (2026-06-28):** Lyle is post–mass-layoff, GovCon has **two closed deals** with payment **~2–3 months away** (work-in-progress + vendor fulfillment). Cash **timing** is the gap — not deal quality or vendor reliability.

## Thesis

Finish and ship the **in-progress game** in parallel with **light-touch GovCon upkeep**, to:

1. **Bridge income** — any sales during the gap beats zero.
2. **Train market muscle** — revenue from what buyers want, rewiring away from employee/paycheck cognition (aligned with [[concepts/offshoots/asset-creation-over-linear-income]]).

## Vs automation-services bridge

| Track | June 2026 automation bridge | Game finish bridge |
|--------|------------------------------|---------------------|
| Time to $ | Sales cycle + networking | Ship → store → discoverability |
| Lyle energy | Client acquisition + delivery | Deep build + launch |
| Asset | Client relationships (exit-friendly) | **Product** (long-tail sales) |
| Fit now | Day job was draining hours | **Calendar cleared**; game already started |

**Status:** Game bridge **primary focus** for gap period; automation services **on hold / optional** unless cash forces faster linear income.

## GovCon parallel rules

- Vendor executes known work — Lyle's job = **coordination, client comms, unblock**, not heroics.
- Weekly **touchpoint** (vendor status + any CO/customer updates) — non-negotiable minimum.
- Do not let game scope creep **delay** vendor milestones that gate payment.

## Success criteria (draft)

- **Ship definition:** playable, store-listed, price set, support channel defined.
- **Minimum bridge:** cover meaningful expenses before GovCon checks land (exact $ target TBD with runway math).
- **Identity:** market feedback without fusing identity to launch-week sales (see mental-diet / Christ-not-scoreboard).
