# Automation Services Bridge Strategy (2026)

**Context**: Lyle built a temporary bridge plan to replace day job income (~$800/week) while government contracts pipeline matured.

**Update 2026-07-05:** Day job **ended** (layoff ~2026-06-27). **VXE with Leonard** accelerating — **~$10k awarded**, **~$60k** pending budget with brother; unemployment filed (pending). **Primary gap** = **~2 months cash timing**, not lack of deals. Automation services remain **on hold** unless runway math breaks before GovCon pay.

**Update 2026-06-28:** Day job ended (layoff); GovCon deals closed with payment ~2–3 months out; game-finish bridge noted — see [[faleth/bridge-strategy/game-finish-bridge-2026]].

## Model

- **Pricing**: $1,000 – $4,000 setup fee (depending on complexity: cloud vs fully local high-power setups)
- **Retainer**: $250/month per client (for API credits + on-demand remote support)
- **Target**: 13–16 paying clients to comfortably exceed $800/week
- **Sustainable base**: 20–25 clients (referral-driven churn replacement)

## Positioning

Clients own the full setup. They only pay the monthly retainer for peace of mind and support. When Lyle eventually exits the bridge, clients are not left dependent on him.

## Acquisition

- Networking events focused on business owners
- Bespoke conversations to uncover real pain points
- One high-quality client per week is the target pace

## Constraints

- Calendar cleared post-layoff; still bounded by God → wife → kids → business.
- Must remain a bridge, not become the main business
- High-ticket local setups can cover a full month on their own

## Status

**On hold** as of 2026-07-05 — VXE contract pipeline + unemployment bridge the gap; reactivate only if **2-month runway** fails before payments.
