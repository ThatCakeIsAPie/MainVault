# Automation Services Bridge Strategy (2026)

**Context**: Lyle is building a temporary bridge to replace his day job income (~$800/week) while the government contracts pipeline matures. He has decided to stop waiting on external timelines that have repeatedly slipped.

## Model

- **Pricing**: $1,000 – $4,000 setup fee (depending on complexity: cloud vs fully local high-power setups)
- **Retainer**: $250/month per client (for API credits + on-demand remote support)
- **Target**: 13–16 paying clients to comfortably exceed $800/week
- **Sustainable base**: 20–25 clients (referral-driven churn replacement)

## Positioning

Clients own the full setup. They only pay the monthly retainer for peace of mind and support. When Lyle eventually exits the bridge, clients are not left dependent on him.

## Acquisition

- Networking events focused on business owners
- Bespoke conversations to uncover real pain points
- One high-quality client per week is the target pace

## Constraints

- Limited energy after day job + relationship + Amway + counseling
- Must remain a bridge, not become the main business
- High-ticket local setups (e.g. Deepseek + multiple Nvidia Spark machines) can cover a full month on their own

## Status

Active bridge strategy as of June 2026. Goal is to generate enough cash flow and time freedom to go full-time on Faleth without relying on government contract timelines.
